# Comic Storyboard: Session 4.5 (Reality Anchors)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 4.5: Reality Anchors & Dead Pigs**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Bacon Culture Shock

#### Panel 1: Dead Pigs
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel showing the Academy dining hall. Britt the gray fungal-turtle is poking a strip of crispy bacon on her plate with a fork, looking completely bewildered. Aggie sits next to her, giggling. Pip holds a cup, and Kael the student stands nearby. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Britt):* "So you take the pig... and you make it crispy? The concept of 'dead pigs' for breakfast is bizarre."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Pip):* "It's bacon, Britt! It's delicious!"

### Page 2: Silas Thorne's Class

#### Panel 1: Reality Anchoring Lecture
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel showing Professor Silas Thorne pacing wildly in front of a giant chalkboard covered in sketches of reality spheres and decaying grid lines. He waves his hands dramatically, eyes wide. The students watch in tense silence. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**

#### Panel 2: The Walkout
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**

### Page 3: The Dead Island Chase

#### Panel 1: Rushing the Hallway
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing Aggie, Britt, and Kael chasing Professor Thorne down a long, white stone corridor, their robes trailing behind. Thorne is walking fast, muttering to himself. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Aggie):* "Professor Thorne, wait! What is a 'dead island'?"

#### Panel 2: The Warning
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A close-up panel focusing on Professor Thorne's face as he stops, leaning in close to the students with a haunted, intense gaze. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Thorne):* "A dead island... where matter remains but the energy is a complete vacuum. Nothing flows. The Mizizi forest's resistance is a symptom of it. Guard yourselves."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled bottom-right corner):* "Reality Anchoring keeps the void away. But Vumbua's anchors are starting to slip."
