# Comic Storyboard: Session 9 (Five Sectors in the Sand)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 9: Five Sectors in the Sand**, styled as a detailed 2D graphic novel.

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive manga-style linework, crisp black ink outlines, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic volumetric lighting`
*   **Aesthetics:** Oxfordian/Victorian architectural elegance first, gaslamp steampunk machinery second, grounded low-fantasy third.
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text artifacts, subtitles, watermarks, generic fantasy castles, modern vehicles, top hats, clock faces`
