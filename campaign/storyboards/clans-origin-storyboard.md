## 🪶 Core Worldbuilding: The Clan Sparks & The Six Clans

* **The Clan Philosophy (Embodied Energy):**  
  Each clan possesses a unique **Spark**—an innate elemental manifestation that defines who they are. Rather than building mechanical nodes or technological machines like Harmony Prime, the ancient clans **embodied the energy directly**, basing the very physics of their world on their living bodies, beasts, and natural elements.
* **The Six Mandala Clans & Canonical Physical Traits:**  
  1. **Root-Kin Dwellers (Mizizi):** Fungal-turtle hybrid humanoids with turtle-like skin (light grey to olivaceous green), pointed ears, vine/root hair, moss, subtle fungal spore drifts, and back shells (lumpy turtle shell with fungal caps for scholars, red-spotted Amanita mushroom cap for Aggie). Draped in living green moss and woven ivy robes.  
  2. **Volcanic Ember Islanders (Ash-Blood):** Volcanic Ember Islanders with dark brown/obsidian skin, glowing yellow-orange eyes, soot dusting, and literal burning orange-red flames for hair forming an active crown. Wearing beaten bronze scale armor or obsidian traveler's cloaks.  
  3. **Subterranean Clay Dwellers (Trench-Kin):** Subterranean clay-and-stone Earthkin humanoids, short and stocky with a rounded soft clay physique (rounded, not cut or muscular), packed-dirt clay brown skin, smooth grey basalt stone accents. Wearing a dark green seaweed robe with a polished metallic silver pauldron on right shoulder. NO SHELL.  
  4. **High-Peak Tempest Riders (Fulgur):** High-peak tempest humanoids with olivaceous green skin, electric blue scale armor, dark hair floating in static coils, flashing static blue eyes, riding giant biological dragonflies.  
  5. **Canyon Stream Dwellers (Wadi):** Desert canyon dwellers with pale porcelain skin, vibrant blue hair, lean agile frames, woven reed garments, and clay water gourds.  
  6. **Cloud Sky-Dwellers (Renali):** Sky dwellers with pale porcelain skin, silver-white hair (or pale-skinned with blue hair, or wrapped in heavy winter furs), flowing iridescent purple silk gowns, flying giant biological dragonflies.
