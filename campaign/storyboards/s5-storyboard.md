# Comic Storyboard: Session 5 (Cards on the Table)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 5: Cards on the Table**, styled as a detailed 2D graphic novel.

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive manga-style linework, crisp black ink outlines, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic volumetric lighting, warm atmospheric twilight gradient`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, spacesuit, tank treads`

---

![Final Page - Session 5, Page 1](assets/s5_page1.png)

*Layout Design:* **Inset Panel Layout** — 1 large vertical panel showing the backroom negotiation, with a small square detail inset panel in the bottom right corner showing the tiny clockwork beetle on the table.

#### Panel 1 (Main Vertical): Lucky's Anomalies
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A tall vertical comic book panel set in Lucky's cluttered, backroom warehouse closet. Lucky the grifter (from reference image) sits across from Iggy (clay-turtle from reference image) and ignatious (Ash-Blood human with spiky fire-red hair from reference image). Lucky points to a diagram on the wall. A speech bubble above him says: "I track anomalies. Show me what you've got." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic lighting.`
