**Timeline:** Monday morning (First Day of Classes) -> Monday Night -> Tuesday Morning.
**Themes:** Overwhelming academic pressure, social navigation, Mizizi secrets, and the contrast between "Gilded" life and "Surface" reality.


- **Pip (from [prima](obsidian://open?file=locations%2Fprima.md))**
    
    - **Vibe:** Bubbly, helpful, and perpetually hydrated.
    - **The Interaction:** Pip is a "Dew-Gatherer" who carries a small pouch of Dew Crystals. As she moves into her unit, she’s stopping at every door to offer a "morning splash"—a small crystal that condenses a cup of perfectly chilled, fresh water. "First day jitters are easier to swallow with a clear throat! I'm Pip, if you need a refill, I'm in 4-B."
    - **Why she's here:** She finds the industrial dorms too dry and loves the way the morning mist clings to the [Block 04](app://obsidian.md/Block%2004) cliffs.
- **Kael (from [octo](obsidian://open?file=locations%2Focto.md))**
    
    - **Vibe:** Socially eager, tech-savvy, and generous with information.
    - **The Interaction:** Kael is setting up a small, low-power Speaking Stone on a pedestal in the common area. He’s inviting everyone to "tune in" their personal stones so they can share a communal "Block 4 Study Feed." "I'm Kael. I figured if we all pool our notes from the different seminars, the Loom won't know what hit it. No gatekeeping in the cliffs, right?"
    - **Why he's here:** He was assigned here by mistake, but after seeing the view of the [Ash-Blood Isles](app://obsidian.md/Ash-Blood%20Isles) from the terrace, he refused to move.
- **Saffron (Harmony-born)**
    
    - **Vibe:** Artistic, calm, and observant.
    - **The Interaction:** Saffron is a "Botanical Architect" student who is currently hanging hand-made sun-catchers made of **Focus Glass** from [Timon](obsidian://open?file=locations%2FTimon.md) along the railings of the walkway. When she sees [britt](obsidian://open?file=characters%2Fplayer-characters%2Fbritt.md) or [aggie](obsidian://open?file=characters%2Fplayer-characters%2Faggie.md), she offers them a small, woven wristband. "It’s just hemp and a bit of copper wire, but it helps ground the static from the Aether-lines. I'm Saffron. Let me know if the light from the catchers gets in your eyes; I can move them!"
    - **Why she's here:** She requested the Mizizi block specifically because she finds the "geometric" architecture of the main campus too rigid for her creative process.
- **Bramble (from [Mizizi Petrified Forest](app://obsidian.md/Mizizi%20Petrified%20Forest))**
    
    - **Vibe:** Protective, "big brother" energy, and practical.
    - **The Interaction:** Bramble is a fellow Mizizi student, but he’s been at the Academy for a few weeks longer as part of the advance construction crew. He’s standing by the communal lift with a large crate of "Sun-Cakes" he bought from **Ma'o** in the market. "Eat up, cousins. The walk to the [deep-hull](obsidian://open?file=locations%2Fdeep-hull.md) classrooms is longer than it looks on the map. I'm Bramble—if anyone gives you trouble about your rank or your roots, you tell 'em to come find me in the gym."
    - **Why he's here:** He’s the unofficial "Floor Warden" of Block 04, making sure the new arrivals don't get lost in the bureaucracy.

## Part 3: The Day 2 Market (Outside the Gates)

A "positive" encounter to offset the rough night before. 

**Mood:** Vibrant, bustling, smells of roasting nuts and ozone-cleaning fluid. People are haggling, but there's a sense of communal preparation for the "Great Filter."

### Set Piece: The "Kindling" Exchange
An elderly street-food vendor, **Ma'o**, spots the party as they navigate the market. They look noticeably more beat-up and exhausted than the average first-year student. Without waiting for them to order, he hands them a tray of "Sun-Cakes" (warm, dense pastries filled with spiced honey). 
- *Ma'o:* "You look like the 'Filter' already chewed you up and spat you out, kids. Survival isn't just about passing tests; it's about remembering to eat. First round is on the house—call it a Vumbua Welcome."
- **Benefit:** Eating the warm cakes provides a minor "Comforted" buff (advantage on the next Stress-related roll).

[[brawn]] eating one too...

### Distraction Options:
1. **The "Broken" Compass:** A vendor is being bullied by a student over a "malfunctioning" aether-compass. The party can intervene and realize the compass is actually tracking the nearest source of HTech.
2. **Lucky's Rivals:** A street-game of "Crown & Ruin" where the dealer is actually being fair, unlike Lucky. A chance to win back some silver or gain a minor "Good Luck" buff.
3. **The Scrivener's Error:** A frantic student has lost their notes. Finding them reveals a secret shortcut through the ventilation to Hangar 12.
