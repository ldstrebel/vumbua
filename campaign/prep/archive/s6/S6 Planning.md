## 🕵️ Scene 2: Hangar Infiltration & Rill's Save (Wednesday Night, 9:00 PM)

> [!NOTE]
> ### 🎙️ DM Readout — Infiltrating the Hangar
> *"The academic hum of the dormitory corridors dies away, replaced by the deep, heavy thrum of the hangar bay vibrating through the stone walkway. Ahead of you, the structure opens into a cavernous vault carved directly into the basalt cliffs, its high arches built of pristine white marble integrated seamlessly with the raw rock. Suspended hundreds of feet above the docking bays is the Gantry, where the air smells of jasmine, ozone, and cold vapor. Down on the floor, the massive racing rigs—ships of brass, wood, and crystal—lie cradled in glowing hexagonal berths like resting predators. A curtain of shimmering iridescent mist, the Prism Gate, hangs across the walkway, its colors shifting slowly like oil on water, waiting to read the resonance of anyone who dares pass."*

### 🛡️ The Prism Gate Barrier

To cross the Gantry, [[ignatious|ignatious]] and [[iggy|Iggy]] must pass through the **Prism Gate** (vaporized Prism Water).
- **The Imbuement:** The mist is imbued with a concept of *approved access* matching the Chief of the Guards, Rovaldi.
- **Daggerheart Check:** **Instinct (Stealth)** vs. **DC 12** to slip through.
  - *Failure:* The mist refracts a discordant, flashing amber warning light. Warden Rovaldi's passive scan is alerted.

### 🕶️ Warden Rovaldi's Investigation

If the check fails, or if the players behave suspiciously, **Warden Rovaldi** approaches. He looks through his faceted crystal lens (the **Prism Eye**), scanning them for overlapping resonance signatures.

- **GM Monocle Interpretation Check (2d10 + 2):**
  - **9 or lower:** *"A split seam in the fabric. Dissonant maintenance. Correct your uniform, student."* (He ignores them).
  - **10 to 14:** *"There is a chaotic frequency in this quadrant. A dissonant growth, perhaps? Stop where you are."* (He moves to inspect. Run the **Hiding from the Guard** mechanics below).
  - **15 or higher:** *"Intruders detected. Overlapping signatures!"* (He calls guards. Skip to escape/Rill save).

### 🎲 Hiding from the Guards (Iggy's Stealth Mechanics)

If Warden Rovaldi moves to inspect or the Indigo Turbans actively search the bay, [[iggy|Iggy]] can attempt to hide. 

#### **1. The Action Roll: Finesse (Stealth) vs. DC 14**
Iggy rolls **2d12 + Finesse**. The outcome dictates the narrative tension:

- **Success with Hope (14+):** *Invisible.* Iggy blends seamlessly into a basalt alcove or slips under a hydro-glider rig. The enforcers walk right past. Iggy clears **1 Stress**.
- **Success with Fear (14+):** *Tense Hiding.* Iggy finds a spot, but a guard stops right next to him, tapping his tuning fork against the metal. The high-pitched chime resonates painfully in Iggy's ears. Iggy hides successfully but takes **1 Stress**.
- **Failure with Hope (< 14):** *Close Call.* An enforcer points a flashlight at Iggy's corner, but before he spots him, [[rill|Rill]] clatters a heavy wrench on a metal chassis nearby: *"Warden! Over here, the coolant line is leaking!"* Rill's distraction saves him, allowing him to slip away.
- **Failure with Fear (< 14):** *Spotted.* Warden Rovaldi's crystal monocle catches the shifting pattern of Iggy's earthen form. Rovaldi strikes his tuning fork, sending a resonance wave that deals **1 Stress** and locks Iggy in place.

#### **2. Iggy's Special Earth-Blending Option**
Since Hangar 12 is carved directly into raw basalt cliffs, Iggy can leverage his **earthen physiology** to hide:
- **Mechanics:** Iggy can spend **1 Hope** or mark **1 Stress** to activate his passive blending ability. If he stands completely still against the basalt walls or stone pillars, he gains **Advantage** on his Stealth roll, or the guards' search DC increases to **17**.
- **Complication:** If he fails the roll while blended, his earth-slime leaks onto the pristine marble floor, leaving a clear trail for Rovaldi to trace later.

### 🔧 Saved by Rill

Before Rovaldi can expose them, [[rill|Rill]] slides out from under a nearby hydro-glider rig, holding a heavy wrench:
- **Rill's Dialogue:** *"Warden! My apologies. These two grease monkeys are mine. I told them to carry the heavy intake valves inside this coat to protect the crystals from the Gantry's dampening mist. Get to work, you two—bay 4, now!"*
- **Rill's Limits:** Once Rovaldi moves away, Rill pulls the PCs aside: *"I saved your skins, but my bay is off-limits, and I'm not sticking up for you again. Rovaldi's monocle scans are active tonight. Go back to your dorms before he double-checks your IDs."*

### 🎲 Pushing Luck vs. Retreat

- **The Retreat:** The PCs return safely to their dorms. Gain **1 Hope**.
- **Pushing Luck:** The PCs try to scout the competitor rigs or find the missing Ash-Blood node.
  - *GM Mechanic:* Requires a **Finesse (Stealth)** check vs. **DC 15**.
    - *Success:* They find that the Ash-Blood node slot on the Echelon rig is vacant and find details on the spires (gain +2 to a race check tomorrow).
    - *Failure:* Rovaldi catches them. **Re-tuning Penalty:** Rovaldi strikes his tuning fork. The targets take **1 Stress** and suffer **Disadvantage on their next Academic or Loom roll**. They are escorted back to their dorms.

## 🎫 Scene 3: Thursday Morning Class & The Valerius Ticket Confrontation (Thursday Morning)

Class wraps up under the dry hum of Professor Kante's lecture. [[loami|Loami]]'s rival, [[finch-gable|Finch Gable]], sneers and hides his cartography notes as they pack up. 

In the courtyard, Pip and Bramble nudge [[aggie|Aggie]]: *"There he is! Go talk to Valerius! Ask for the tickets!"*

### 👑 Roleplaying Valentine Sterling

[[valerius-sterling|Valerius "Val" Sterling]] is surrounded by a crowd of adoring candidates and groupies. He looks visually exhausted, overwhelmed by his high-society popularity, and is desperately looking for any excuse to escape gracefully.

- **Val's Dialogue:** *"Oh, thank the Heavens! Aggie! Yes, yes, I must consult with you about... logistics. Excuse me, everyone, study group business!"* He dumps his entourage.
- **Val's Ground Offer:** Val tells Aggie: *"Look, my dad’s business partners and a bunch of stuffy captains are all over the Zephyr platforms. I don't want to go up there. I'd love to join you guys on the ground in the Bleachers if I can sneak away. We can drink some of that grog Loami was talking about!"*

### 🎲 Persuading Val to go up (The Zephyr tickets)

If the PCs want to watch from the airships (Zephyrs) to network, they must persuade him to pull strings.
- **Daggerheart Check:** **Presence (Influence)** vs. **DC 13** (Aggie gets Advantage if she mentions their shared time at the Celestial Lounge; Loami gets +2 if he reminds Val of his Crown & Ruin win).
  - *Success:* Valerius sighs: *"Alright, alright. I'll get you VIP passes. But you have to let me hang out with you on the deck, and if my father comes by, you pretend you're presenting a project."*
  - *Failure:* Valerius remains firm: *"I really can't face my father today. I'll meet you on the ground by the Bleachers."* They get Bleacher tickets instead.

## 🏁 Scene 4: The Reso Race Sandbox (Thursday Afternoon)

> [!NOTE]
> ### 🎙️ DM Readout — The Reso Race Basin
> *"The sheer scale of the Basin hits you first—a mile-wide canyon of turquoise water and solid clay carved into the volcanic cliffs. Above, the sky is choked with the golden silk envelopes of colossal dirigibles and moored luxury platforms, the Zephyr tier, where noble houses watch in gilded comfort. Below them, the Rim-Tracker stands are a sea of brass magnifying panels and lenses catching the afternoon sun as tutors and scholars track the tracks. And down at the basin rim, the Bleachers are a roaring, rowdy mass of half a million souls, smelling of roasted meat and cheap grog. On the starting line, half on the clay and half in the water, the rigs idle, their resonance engines venting hot steam that glows with the warm, deep-umber light of charged crystal cores."*

Rather than asking the players "What now?", use the sandbox hooks below based on their chosen vantage point to actively pull them into the scene:

├────────────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┤

### 🏟️ Option A: The Bleachers (Ground Level)
- **The Grog Business:** Loami and [[lucky|Lucky]] set up their crates, selling "Ambrosia of Luck" grog to the rowdy crowd.
- **NPC Prompt — [[lucky|Lucky]]:** Lucky pulls Loami and ignatious aside, whispering: *"Hey, check it out. The Echelon ship—Val's team—is carrying a custom Vox capacitor. It's radiating purple waves. See that? If that capacitor blows, it's going to trigger a massive resonance backlash. Do we bet against them?"*
- **NPC Prompt — [[hollow-jack|Hollow Jack]]:** The scruffy Fringe bookie approaches: *"Hey, Union boy! Loami! You worked the spires. Pudge is the underdog on that giant bird. The odds are 20-to-1. You think that bird can sync the Chime Spire, or is it going to stall in the Weeping Tunnel?"*
- **Active Ground Actions:**
  - *Distracting Rivals:* **Presence** check vs. DC 12 to cheer/jeer, causing a rival pilot to miss a prong strike.
  - *Fringe Bettor:* **Finesse** check vs. DC 13 to pickpocket a rival team's supporter.

### 🎈 Option B: The Zephyrs (Airships)
- **The VIP Deck:** The PCs stand on a gilded wooden deck under a massive silk balloon. Servants pass glasses of sparkling wine.
- **NPC Prompt — Lady Glimmer:** She approaches Aggie and Britt, inspecting their clothes: *"Ah, the Mizizi candidates. Tell me, is it true your petrified forest responds to a B-flat resonance? The Scrivener Guild is offering a handsome bounty for any new node frequencies. What secrets did you bring from the woods?"*
- **NPC Prompt — Valerius:** Valerius is hiding behind a silk curtain, muttering: *"Psst! If my father looks over here, start talking about volumetric cartography stress lines. Fast!"*
- **Active Air Actions:**
  - *Harmonic Analysis:* Use mounted magnifying lenses to scan the track. **Instinct (Analysis)** check vs. DC 13 to spot a localized wind shear, giving Pudge’s griffin a navigation advantage.
  - *Captains Networking:* **Presence (Influence)** check vs. DC 14 to impress an observing Captain.

### 🏎️ Narration Guide: The 4-Beat Montage

The DM rolls a background **d20** to determine the race status at each beat. Use the results to narrate the action and prompt players.

```
       [ DM Race Background Roll ]
                  d20
 ┌─────────────────┼─────────────────┐
 ▼                 ▼                 ▼
[ <= 7 ]        [ 8-14 ]          [ 15+ ]
"Chaos"        "Neck-Neck"       "Underdog"
Stalls &       Echelon fights     Pudge's griffin
crashes.       Jax's ironclad.    surges ahead.
```

- **Beat 1: The Launch / Grid Release:** The starting gates drop. Rigs roar with umber crystal fire.
  - *Low Roll:* A rig stalls, creating a massive pileup. *Prompt:* *"ignatious, the Echelon rig is caught in the pileup. Do you warn Val's crew or let them burn?"*
  - *High Roll:* Pudge launches perfectly.
- **Beat 2: The Weeping Tunnel:** Skiffs pass through a dark stone tunnel where dripping water acts as a natural harmonic mute.
  - *Moderate Roll:* Jax's ironclad uses brute steam power to push through.
  - *Prompt to Loami:* *"Loami, you know the Soft Forge spire is just past the exit. How do you direct Pudge to align his Prong striker?"*
- **Beat 3: The Vertical Climb / Aether-Shear:** Hulls fight high winds near the floating rock spires.
  - *Low Roll:* A gust of wind shear threatens to throw Pudge's griffin into the canyon wall.
  - *Prompt to Aggie/Britt:* *"From the Zephyr deck, you see the wind shear hitting. How do you react to save Pudge?"*
- **Beat 4: The Final Stretch:** The ships sprint across the turquoise water starting grid.

**Post-Race Reveal:** The race ends (Pudge wins). The crowds disperse. ignatious or Iggy realize the Ash-Blood node was missing from the wreckage/berths. Run the **Finding Rill** scene as they seek out faculty.

## 📝 Scene 6: The Written Exam & Confluence (Friday)

### ✒️ Scene: The Basin Written Exam

> [!NOTE]
> ### 🎙️ DM Readout — The Written Exam
> *"The roaring crowd of the race is gone, replaced by a silence so heavy you can hear the wind whistling through the high arches of the Basin. Seventy thousand stone desks sit arranged in concentric tiers descending down the white marble walls. Seventy thousand candidates sit in absolute stillness, the only sound the synchronized scratching of steel-nibbed pens on heavy vellum paper. In the air, the scent of charcoal ink and parchment is thick. Proctors in deep blue robes glide down the stone steps. At every desk, the magic of the Resonant Ink is visible: where a student writes a truth, the ink sets into a clean, shimmering azure blue; where they falter, the ink dissolves into a spreading, charcoal-grey smudge, showing their failure to the entire hall."*

#### **Exam Written Checks:**
1. **Volumetric Cartography:** **Agility** check vs. **DC 12** to plot navigation coordinates.
2. **Harmonic Resonance:** **Knowledge** check vs. **DC 13** to analyze Sympathetic Dissonance.
3. **The Blind Landing:** **Instinct** check vs. **DC 14** to land the simulator.

#### **The Drop of the Failed:**
Calculate final scores: **Baseline** (Gold 95, Silver 80, Copper 60) + **Exam Points** (maximum of 100 points scored from choices/rolls).
- Those who score **Rust (< 100)** watch their desks fold flat, sliding down the concave Basin walls to the floor below to be escorted to the departure ports.
