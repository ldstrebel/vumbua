## I. COMMISSION PROCEDURES & OPPORTUNITIES

Following the final sorting at the Looms, all passing candidates (Gold, Silver, and Copper) are required to secure an exploratory internship berth for the upcoming **one-month Proving Voyage**. The Proving Voyage represents your initial field deployment into the "Safe Zone" and surrounding frontier Periphery.

### 1. The Rank Placement System
Your Loom score establishes your baseline eligibility for specific vessel tiers:
*   **Gold-Rank Berth:** Grants placement on premium diplomatic cruisers, high-tier research flagships, and advanced combat rigs.
*   **Silver-Rank Berth:** Grants placement on standard tactical crawlers, silent scouts, and specialized geological surveyors.
*   **Copper-Rank Berth:** Grants placement on industrial freighters, heavy salvagers, and frontline patrol craft.

### 2. Discretionary Direct Commendations
Candidates are advised that all observing Captains retain the authority to issue a **Direct Commendation** (often referred to as a *Sponsorship Commission*). If a candidate demonstrates exceptional situational competence, resourcefulness, or synergy during the Proving Run, a Captain may override Loom rank limits and commission that candidate directly to their crew regardless of baseline score.
