# The Ash-Blood Isles

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Clan territory — volcanic chain |
| **Clan** | [[Ash-Bloods]] |
| **Integration Status** | 100% (24 months ago) |
| **Discovered By** | Captain Elara Thorne |
| **Current Significance** | [[Vumbua Academy]]'s current location |
| **First Mentioned** | [[session-00\|Session 0]] |

The Ash-Blood Isles are a chain of volcanic mountains with perpetual smoke and magma flows. The [[Ash-Bloods]] live among the vents and lava tubes, where temperatures would kill most other beings. [[Vumbua Academy]] relocated here 6 months ago.

## What Players Know

The volcanic chain is visible on the horizon from the edge of the [[Mizizi Petrified Forest]]. During [[session-00|Session 0]], the GM described: *"You can see on the horizon the smoke of their perpetual volcano."*

The Isles were discovered 24 months ago by **Captain Elara Thorne** — just one week after [[valentine-sterling-sr|Valentine Sterling Sr.]] found the [[Mizizi]]. The [[The Power System|Panda line batteries]] (which could store a connection to the land for up to 3 days outside a node) made both discoveries possible.

### lavsidian
The Isles produce **lavsidian** — an obsidian-like heat stone that burns 80% hotter per gram since integration. It has a halflife oscillation (heat tapers then rises) and loses potency with distance from the Isles. Used in [[Vumbua Academy]]'s boiler room alongside crystal batteries.

### The Great Cooling
Before Harmony's arrival, the [[Ash-Bloods]] were experiencing "The Great Cooling" — their volcanoes were slowly dying. After integration, the heat returned. This is the core reason the clan embraced Harmony.

### Ash-Blood Genesis Myth
According to Ash-Blood lore shared by [[ignatious]], the islands were formed millennia ago out of tragedy and love. The Great Dragon of the Sky and the Great Dragon of the Sea respected each other but were separated by the horizon. Tricked by a demon into believing they could meet if the Sky Dragon dove from the sun, the Sky Dragon plummeted, unable to slow down, and caught fire. 
Burning up entirely, it was caught by the kind Sea Dragon in a loving embrace. The extinguished flames and the burned body of the Sky Dragon formed the Ash-Blood Isles. It is this loving embrace that continues to heat the islands and the stones used by the clan today.

# Bloomfield

> Harmony's timekeeping standard — flowers that bloom with mechanical precision.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Node City |
| **Node Resource** | Clockwork Blooms |
| **Faction** | [[Scrivener Guild]] (The Loreborne) |
| **Node Rating** | Moderate (+30 to +100 amps) |
| **Status** | Integrated |

## Overview

Bloomfield produces Clockwork Blooms — flowers that bloom with mechanical precision every 60 seconds. For centuries, Clockwork Blooms were the standard unit of timekeeping across Harmony before more precise instruments were developed. The [[Scrivener Guild]] adopted these as their timekeeping standard, essential for synchronising maps, logs, and research across the empire. The blooms retain historical significance and are still used ceremonially.

# Harmony Prime (The Seat)

> *"The Seat of Harmony. It's the biggest city... huge. 8 million people."*
> — [[serra-vox|Serra Vox]], [[session-02\|Session 2]]

## Overview

|                     |                                             |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Type**            | Capital city                                |
| **Population**      | ~8 million                                  |
| **Government**      | High Council (Domestic Council)             |
| **Key Figures**     | [[lady-ignis\|Ignis]](highest voting power) |
| **First Mentioned** | [[session-02]]                              |

Harmony Prime is the static capital city of the Harmony Empire, far from the frontier where [[Vumbua Academy]] currently operates. It is the seat of the High Council and the administrative center of the empire.

To flesh out [[Harmony Prime]] (The Seat), we have to look at it as a vertical metropolis of 8 million people where your altitude is directly tied to your "Charge" (social and literal energy). 

Here are the primary families and factions that operate within the capital's spires:

### The Highborne (The Upper Spires)
These families control the fundamental resources of the empire and hold permanent seats on the Domestic Council.

| Family/Faction       | Focus                     | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[house-gilded]]     | **Energy & Finance**      | The "Old Money" of Harmony. They own the Chime Spires and the Crystal Banks. They are currently obsessed with maintaining the appearance of wealth while their energy reserves dwindle. The resonator disaster set them back and let them be overtaken - though still ludicrously rich. [[lyra-castellan]] and [[ludo-castellan\|Ludo]] are trying to poach [[valerius-sterling\|Val]]  |
| **House Sterling**   | **Exploration & Mapping** | Known as "The Paper Men." They funded the original expeditions that mapped the Safe Lanes. [[valentine-sterling]] comes from this line. They are the primary rivals to the Castellans. Their money comes from exploration and tight connections with Vox. They are the marketing arm of the Vox                                                                                         |
| **House Vox**        | **Industry & Power**      | Based in [[umbra-mountain]] but maintain a massive "Syndicate" in the capital. They control the distribution of crystal batteries. They are seen as "New Money" by House Gilded.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **The Vane Lineage** | **Governance & Law**      | The family of [[dean-isolde-vane]]. They have historically produced the empire's greatest administrators and judges. They view themselves as the "Adults in the Room."                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

---

### The Loreborne (The Mid-Spires)
The bureaucratic engine of the empire. They don't own the resources, but they control the *information* about them.

- **The [[scrivener-guild]]: The most powerful non-noble faction. They manage the **Receipt System** and the **Loom**. If you want to prove you exist in Harmony, you have to go through a Scrivener. They are based in the "Archive Spire" in the city center.
- **The Chronos Society**: A sub-faction of the Scriveners based in [[Bloomfield]]. They manage the empire's timekeeping and synchronization. They are the ones who ensure the mobile cities like [[Vumbua Academy]] stay on schedule.
- **The Aether-Merchants**: A loose collection of wealthy trade families (including the **Castellan Family**). They operate the luxury markets, dealing in "Aether-Wine," "Living Scent Wood" from [[Minnow]], and "Focus Glass" from [[Timon]].

---

### The Iron Union (The Lower Spires & Foundations)
The labor force that keeps the 8 million people alive. They live in the "Rust" and "Copper" tiers where the air is thick with steam.

- **The Grinders**: A massive labor union of boiler-workers, engineers, and maintenance crews. They are the ones who actually handle the **lavsidian** and the heavy machinery. [[loami]] would have strong ties to this culture.
- **The Silt-Walkers**: A faction of spies and couriers who use "Silent Silt" from [[Nstyl]] to move through the city unheard. They are the primary source of the "Academic Secrets" that [[ludo-castellan]] prizes.
- **The Battery-Scavengers**: A desperate underclass that hunts for "leaking" or discarded crystal batteries in the city's foundations. They are technically illegal but essential for the city's waste management.

---

### The Council of Outliers (The Diplomatic Quarter)
Because of the **Inverse Power Doctrine**, the most "primitive" or "wild" clans actually hold the most voting power in the capital to prevent the Highborne from steamrolling them.

- **The Ash-Blood Embassy**: Led by [[lady-ignis]]. Because the [[Ash-Bloods]] provide the most "Variance" (Wild Magic) to the grid, she holds the highest voting power on the Council.
- **The Mizizi Enclave**: A small, moss-covered district in the lower spires where [[Mizizi]] representatives live. It is the only place in the city where things are allowed to "die" naturally, which makes the neighbors very uncomfortable.
- **The Trench-Kin Shadows**: They have no official embassy (as they are "Hidden"), but rumors persist of a flooded level in the city's base where their representatives meet in secret.

---

### The Castellan Family (The Aspirants)
The Castellans don't fit into a major faction yet—that is their entire struggle.
- **Status**: High-tier Aether-Merchants.
- **Location**: The "Gilded-Adjacent" district (The 89th Floor).
- **The Goal**: To move from the "Mid-Spires" to the "Upper Spires" by securing a marriage or blood-oath with House Sterling. They are the ultimate "Social Climbers" of [[Harmony Prime]].

## What Players Know

[[serra-vox]] revealed during [[session-02|Session 2]] that the capital has approximately 8 million people. She slipped that she'd spent time there under "political pressure" — hinting at her hidden noble background as a member of House Vox.

[[lady-ignis]], the [[Ash-Bloods|Ash-Blood]] matriarch, serves on the High Council here. Under the [[Inverse Power Doctrine]], she holds the highest voting power of any council member.

# Settika

## Overview

Settika is built around the Prism Falls — a water source that responds directly to conceptual imbuement and structural disharmony. 

### The Magic Science of Prism Water
* **Concept Imbuement:** A ritual can be performed to imbue a specific concept (e.g., "authorized entry") into the water. When a person passes through the mist or water, it momentarily taps into their soul, searching for the exact moment or memory when that concept was transferred to them (e.g., when they were granted permission by an authority). If the connection is found, the water aligns and passes them (often flashing a soft blue).
* **The Base State (Diagnostic/Mood Ring):** Without a specific imbued concept, raw Prism Water reacts to physical, structural, or spiritual disharmony. It changes color to highlight anything that is "wrong" or not working as it was designed to. A tear in cloth, a diseased kidney, a fractured bone, or a disguised silhouette will light up in distinct warning colors, the meanings of which scholars still actively debate.

Because of this, Prism Water is widely used across Harmony for medical diagnostics, engineering inspection (spotting structural cracks), food safety, and security.

In [[Nstyl]] and other cities, Prism Falls water carries an almost sacred reverence — people trust what the water reveals about a person's nature. This belief can be exploited by those who understand how the water actually works.

[[Azor]], a street-smart Settikan, used Prism Falls water to manipulate [[Tus]] and [[Zyykl]] from [[Nstyl]], convincing them they were "true Exploranauts" who deserved a place at [[Vumbua Academy]]. The water *does* reveal true nature — but Azor's interpretation of it was the lie.

# Trail-Tail Trench

> Bioluminescent seeds in the deep water — swim through and leave a trail of light.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Frontier Node (Wild) |
| **Node Resource** | Trail-Tail Seeds |
| **Node Rating** | Moderate (+30 to +100 amps) |
| **Clan Territory** | [[Trench-Kin]] |
| **Status** | Not integrated — no settlement |

## Overview

Trail-Tail Seeds are found in the deep waters of [[Trench-Kin]] territory. When ingested, they cause the consumer to leave a glowing trail of light behind them, like a Tron light-cycle. Fish in the trench use the seeds naturally, creating bioluminescent trails through the dark water.

There is no city or settlement here — this is a wild node in Trench-Kin territory. It represents a potential future integration target for Harmony, though the Trench-Kin remain hidden.

> [!comment]- GM Note
> **Not Iggy's Domain.** While Iggy is an Earthkin who emerged from the water with an Ash-Blood artifact, the Trail-Tail Trench is exclusively Trench-Kin territory and **is definitively not** the hidden civilization Iggy comes from.

The node itself is incredibly powerful. Great aquatic beasts call this trench home, thriving off a consistent biological lure that draws land and sea creatures—including unwary explorers—directly to their bellies if they linger too close.


Based on the "Frequency Error" theory from [[professor-aris|Doctor Aris]] and the specific node cultures like **Light-trails**, life in this biome wouldn't evolve for physical survival in the traditional sense. Instead, it would evolve to manage, manipulate, and consume **Resonance**.

Here is how life thrives in this biome based on your node classifications:

### 1. The "Frequency Mimics" (Camouflage & Luring)
Since life is a "persistent frequency error," the most successful predators evolve to **mask their error** by matching the background radiation of a Node.
*   **The Siren-Stalkers:** These creatures evolved to mimic the **Light-trail** node culture. They don't just look like violet ribbons; they emit the same **60 BPM pulse** (The Siren Song). 
*   **Thriving Strategy (The Lure):** In the wild, their 60 BPM hypnotic pulse lulls unwary creatures and pilots to sleep, mimicking the gentle draw of the deep sea. When a victim feels that "biological sense of safety" and steers toward them, they drift directly into the maws of the great beasts waiting in the dark. The predator doesn't attack the body—it "de-syncs" the prey's frequency, causing them to lose physical cohesion before they even hit the ground.

### 2. The "Temporal Scavengers" (Feeding on Echoes)
Because of the **Temporal Ghosting** effect seen in Light-trails, an organism's "echo" remains behind for several seconds.
*   **Resonance-Leeches:** As noted in your lab sessions, these don't feed on blood. They travel the "Leech-Highways" (Light-trails) to find these temporal after-images.
*   **Thriving Strategy:** They consume the "stability" of the ghost. Because the ghost is linked to the living host, the host feels a "Symptomatic Resonance Shift." The leeches thrive by eating the *past* of a creature, effectively weakening its *present* form until it vanishes into the Mist.

### 3. "Anchor-Flora" (Spatial Stability)
In a biome where the Z-axis is a "liar" and space is non-Euclidean, plants cannot rely on roots in the dirt.
*   **Bioluminescent Moss:** This flora thrives by "recording" the frequency of the nodes. It acts as a biological hard drive.
*   **Thriving Strategy:** It grows in areas smelling of **ozone and wet earth**. By anchoring its frequency to a stable Node (like a **Chime Spire**), the moss remains "solid" even when the physical ground around it warps or dissolves. It survives by being more "real" than the rocks it grows on.


### Summary of the Biome's Food Chain:
1.  **Producers:** **Spires & Nodes** (The source of all frequency/energy).
2.  **Primary Consumers:** **Bioluminescent Moss** (Absorbs frequency) and **Light-trails** (Records events).
3.  **Secondary Consumers:** **Resonance-Leeches** (Eat the echoes/stability).
4.  **Apex Predators:** **Siren-Stalkers** (Use the 60 BPM pulse to lure prey into gravity wells).


### "The Sync" (Hive-Mind Symbiosis)
Despite the intense danger, a resilient crew can nurture a resistance against the lure. Within the controlled environment of the Trail-Tail domain, it is even possible to attain an almost hive-mind state of symbiosis with the frequency of the trench. If an explorer or crew can attune to the trench's pulse rather than fighting it, they achieve "The Sync."

**DM Tip for Reso Race Mechanics:** 
*   **The Danger (The Lull):** During a race, the trench is wildly dangerous. If crews are not actively aware and resisting, the Siren lure lulls them to sleep, drawing their vehicles toward the beasts.
*   **The Reward (The Sync):** If a pilot or crew successfully rolls to "Sync," they immediately become immune to those hypnotic effects. Moreover, they receive a massive rush of energy and local awareness, allowing them to see faint, glowing frequency trails of *all other teams* in the area for exactly 1 minute.

# The Walker-Core

The Walker-Core is one of [[Vumbua Academy]]'s three permanent Cores — a four-legged industrial power unit positioned at the edge of the grounds. It houses the engineering department, power systems, and [[The Loom]] (an ancient, trusted conduit that utilizes a combination of grades and karmic magic to organically group students into squads).

## What Players Know

### The Power Plant
The power plant within the Walker-Core has "Vumbua POWER" written over its archway. Inside is a large warehouse with machinery — glowing gems plugged into panels, connected to whirring cogs and rotating machines. Turbines powered by [[The Power System|Panda 5 resonator batteries]], with a catwalk level containing offices and a chalkboard covered in [[Professor Kante]]'s research notes.

[[Iggy]] explored the interior during [[session-02.5|Session 2.5]], discovering the battery infrastructure and meeting [[Professor Kante]].
