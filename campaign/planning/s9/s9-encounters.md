## 🎓 Daggerheart GM Quick Reference (First-Time GM Cheat Sheet)

### 1. 🎲 How to Call for Action Rolls
When players attempt an action with uncertain outcomes, prompt them for an **Action Roll** using 2d12 (Duality Dice: 1 Hope Die + 1 Fear Die) + Trait Modifier.
* **Easy DC:** 10 | **Moderate DC:** 13 | **Hard DC:** 16 | **Heroic DC:** 18+
* **With Hope (Hope > Fear):** Player succeeds with positive momentum. They earn 1 Hope.
* **With Fear (Fear > Hope):** Player succeeds, BUT you (the GM) gain 1 Fear point or add a complication.
* **Failure with Hope:** Player fails, but gains a silver lining or 1 Hope.
* **Failure with Fear:** Player fails and faces immediate negative consequences / GM spends Fear.

### 2. ⚡ Spending GM Fear Points
As GM, you can spend **Fear** to:
* Trigger a sudden **Node Environmental Hazard** (e.g. steam blast, static shock, low-gravity drift).
* Have a **Rival Squad NPC** execute a special tactical maneuver (e.g. Finch measuring wind to deflect an arrow, Zephyr discharging lightning).
* Introduce an unexpected **Aether-Beast blip** or second rival squad entering the scene.

### 3. 🧠 Daggerheart Trait & Skill Triggers for Players
* **AGILITY:** Sprinting across shifting terrain, dodging steam vents, leaping between floating boulders.
* **STRENGTH:** Forcing open rusted tag clamps, holding anchors against rushing water, physically restraining enemies.
* **FINESSE:** Picking mechanical locks, calibrating node components, snickering items from rivals.
* **INSTINCT:** Spotting hidden tags, sensing localized node fluctuations, tracking footsteps in mud/sand.
* **PRESENCE:** Intimidating rival squads, rallying allies, giving persuasive pitches to watching Captains.
* **KNOWLEDGE:** Analyzing node resonance frequencies, identifying pre-collapse alloys, calculating wind-shear math.

### 4. ⚔️ Daggerheart Combat Resolution Guide (First-Time GM)

#### 🔄 Dynamic Turn Flow (Action Tracker)
* **No Initiative Rolls!** Daggerheart does not use round-robin initiative.
* **Player Actions:** Any player can act when it makes narrative sense. Whenever a player acts, they place an **Action Token** on the Tracker.
* **GM Turns:** The GM takes a turn whenever a player rolls with **Fear**, **Fails an Action Roll**, or when all players choose to pass.

#### 🎯 Resolving Attacks Step-by-Step
1. **Attack Roll:** Attacker rolls **2d12 (Duality Dice) + Trait Modifier** vs Target's **Evasion**.
2. **Damage Roll:** Roll weapon/spell damage dice (e.g. `1d8+2 Physical` or `2d6 Magickal`).
3. **Compare to Damage Thresholds:**
   * **Below Minor Threshold:** Ignores damage (scratched armor/glancing blow).
   * **Minor Damage (Hit >= Minor Threshold):** Mark **1 HP**.
   * **Major Damage (Hit >= Major Threshold):** Mark **2 HP**.
   * **Severe Damage (Hit >= Severe Threshold):** Mark **3 HP**.
4. **Armor Slot Mitigation:** A defender can mark **1 Armor Slot** to subtract their **Armor Value** from incoming damage *before* comparing to thresholds!
