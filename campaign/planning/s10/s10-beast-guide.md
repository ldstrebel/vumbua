## *"The Monsters (and NPCs) Know What They're Doing"* Edition

> *"An Aether beast is not a wild animal driven by hunger. It is an extension of the world's resonance grid. It does not hunt your meat—it hunts the frequency of your soul, and it never forgets where a node used to bleed."*

## 📚 Tactical Framework: "The Monsters Know What They're Doing"

In accordance with Keith Ammann's tactical philosophy (*The Monsters Know What They're Doing*), creatures and NPCs in Vumbua act according to their **stat distribution, sensory capabilities, environmental advantages, target prioritization, and morale/retreat conditions**. They do not make sub-optimal moves or stand still to take damage.

---

## 🐆 1. Mwaza-Chui (Memory Jaguar) — Apex Solo Boss

### 📊 Stat Profile & Tactical Role
* **Role:** Stealth Ambush Stalker / High-Frequency Assassin.
* **Key Stats:** Instinct (+4), Agility (+3), Finesse (+3) | Evasion 13 | 6 Armor Slots.
* **Traits:** `Signal Lock` (+2 to Hit vs mind-links), `Mirror-Step` (40ft decoy teleport), `Memory Surge` (psychic feedback).

### 🎯 Target Prioritization
1. **Primary Target:** [[britt|Britt]] & [[aggie|Aggie]] whenever their telepathic mind-link is active. The mind-link emits a portable mini-node frequency that Mwaza-Chui perceives as a territorial beacon and energy food source.
2. **Secondary Target:** Any spellcaster or PC with high active Stress.
3. **Ignored Targets:** High-defense frontline tanks ([[loami|Loami]], [[bramble|Bramble]]) unless they physically block its line of attack.

### 🏃 Movement & Combat Loop
* **Pre-Combat:** Prowls 40ft out in the high Nordic pine canopy. Does not attack until a mind-link is opened or an Aether spell is cast.
* **Attack Phase:** Pounces from shadows onto the highest-frequency target with `Signal Lock`.
* **Repositioning (`Mirror-Step`):** Immediately after delivering a blow or when targeted by a heavy counter-attack, teleports 40ft into tree shadows, leaving a purring Aether decoy.
* **Critical Reaction (`Memory Surge`):** Taking Severe damage forces a DC 14 Presence Check or 1 Stress on all nearby PCs as buried node memories erupt.

### 🏳️ Morale & Retreat Conditions
* **Morale:** Cold, calculated self-preservation.
* **Retreat Trigger:** If reduced to **2 Armor Slots** and Rill's Signal Jammer is active, Mwaza-Chui disengages into the deep pine forest canopy to stalk the party from a safe distance rather than fighting to the death.

## 🐲 2. Gale-Wyrm — Apex Aerostatic Dragon

### 📊 Stat Profile & Tactical Role
* **Role:** Ranged Heavy Artillery & Air Superiority Controller.
* **Key Stats:** Agility (+4), Instinct (+3), Strength (+3) | Evasion 12 | 5 Armor Slots.
* **Traits:** `Gravity Drop` (2d10+4 Severe damage), `Gravity Reversal` (ranged attack penalty & floating PCs).

### 🎯 Target Prioritization
1. **Primary Target:** Stationary targets, candidates pinned under debris, or exposed ranged spellcasters.
2. **Ignored Targets:** Melee-bound PCs standing on the ground who cannot reach its 60ft flight ceiling.

### 🏃 Movement & Combat Loop
* **Positioning:** Hovers 50–60ft above the pine canopy out of melee range.
* **Bombardment Loop (`Gravity Drop`):** Uses its aerostatic gravity aura to lift 500lb mossy granite boulders into the air, then hurls them down onto pinned cadets or defensive walls.
* **Defensive Stance (`Gravity Reversal`):** Reverses local gravity when targeted by ranged attacks (imposing Disadvantage on ranged rolls) and causing any melee PC who jumps/teleports near it to float helplessly 15ft in the air.

### 🏳️ Morale & Retreat Conditions
* **Retreat Trigger:** If grounded by a **Weighted Scrap Net** and reduced to **1 Armor Slot**, it releases a localized gravity blast to knock back surrounding PCs and ascends vertically into the cloud layer to escape.

## 🐱 3. Stunted Canopy Cats — Minion Swarm

### 📊 Stat Profile & Tactical Role
* **Role:** Skirmishers & Pinned-Target Finishers.
* **Key Stats:** Agility (+3), Finesse (+2) | Evasion 11 | 1 HP each.
* **Traits:** `Mizizi Instinct` (+2 to Hit vs pinned/prone targets).

### 🎯 Target Prioritization
* **Exclusive Target:** Candidates or PCs who are pinned under boulders, trapped in tar moats, or knocked prone.
* **Behavior:** Will **completely ignore** standing, healthy PCs with ready weapons to feast on vulnerable targets.

### 🏃 Movement & Combat Loop
* Move through the upper pine branches to bypass frontline tanks. Pounce in groups of 3. If the first cat hits, remaining cats gain Advantage on follow-up strikes.

### 🏳️ Morale & Retreat Conditions
* **Retreat Trigger:** If **2 of the 3 cats** in a pack are killed in a single round, the surviving cat flees up a pine tree trunk.

## 🦈 4. Dagger Sharks — Abyssal Predators

### 📊 Stat Profile & Tactical Role
* **Role:** Aquatic Shock Troops & Pressure Interceptors.
* **Key Stats:** Agility (+3), Strength (+2) | Evasion 11 | 2 Armor Slots each.
* **Traits:** `Pressure Scent` (Advantage vs Iggy / pressure nodes).

### 🎯 Target Prioritization
* [[iggy|Iggy]] (his soil/clay pressure node frequency mimics rival Leviathan engines) and any candidate wading through flooded estuary water.

### 🏃 Movement & Combat Loop
* Attack from rushing estuary water or mud pools. Execute drive-by bites (`1d8+2 Physical damage`) and attempt to drag targets into deep mud.

## 🌊 5. Rill (Wadi Researcher / Futurist) — Tactical Combat Aide

### 📊 Stat Profile & Tactical Role
* **Role:** Buffer, Disruptor & First Responder.
* **Key Stats:** Finesse (+4), Knowledge (+3), Agility (+3) | Evasion 12 | 7 Armor Slots.
* **Traits:** `Aether Signal Jammer` (cancels Signal Lock), `Wadi Flow Buff` (+1 Attack / Agility Advantage).

### 🎯 Tactical Objectives
1. **Protect Britt & Aggie:** Deploys `Signal Jammer` to break Mwaza-Chui's focus.
2. **Recruit Squad 907:** Keeps the party alive while pitching **Professor Ink's Scrivener Guild Expedition**.

### 🏃 Movement & Combat Loop
* Rides her insectoid rig (*The Reed-Runner*). Stays within 15ft of PCs to maintain her `Wadi Flow` buff (+1 Attack rolls). Fires suppressing pulse bursts at Canopy Cats.

## 🪵 6. Bramble (Plant-Kin Ally — Squad 06) — Frontline Anchor

### 📊 Stat Profile & Tactical Role
* **Role:** Chokepoint Tank & Defender.
* **Key Stats:** Strength (+3), Instinct (+3) | Evasion 9 | 8 Armor Slots.
* **Traits:** `Root Wall` (+2 Evasion cover).

### 🎯 Tactical Objectives
1. Protect [[dr-rose-halloway|Dr. Rose Halloway]] and wounded candidates.
2. Anchor the timber longhouse entrance in Zone 5, raising `Root Walls` to absorb incoming attacks.
