# Session 10 Plan: Survival Instincts & The Aether Beast Breach

## 📜 Session Summary
Session 10 shifts the campaign into high gear as a sudden **Global Resonance Drop** causes the colossal **Walker Core** under Zone 0 to short out, triggering a cascading barrier failure across the Apex Ring. The candidates of Squad 907 ([[iggy|Iggy]], [[britt|Britt]], [[aggie|Aggie]], [[loami|Loami]]) must transition from material scavenging to immediate, high-stakes survival.

The Mizizi Forest is a dense, misty **Nordic Pine Forest**—towering evergreen pine canopies, damp pine needle floors, and moss-covered granite boulders (there are **no stone temple ruins**—ancient Mizizi structures were weathered timber longhouses and palisades).

As wild **Aether Beasts** flood in from the deep wild borders outside the clan, **[[rill|Rill (The River-Born)]]** reaches Squad 907 first! Rill was performing field research on "Weeping Nodes" in the area for **[[professor-ink|Professor Ink]]** (Scrivener Guild Captain & Fungal Biology Specialist), who is seeking to recruit talented cadets—specifically Britt, Aggie, and Iggy—for his high-stakes Mizizi Forest Memory Expedition. Rill acts as the party's primary combat aide during the beast breach, using her experimental *Wadi Flow Tech* to disrupt Mwaza-Chui's signal lock while pitching Ink's offer between waves.

## 🎭 Major Narrative Beats & Flashbacks

### 1. 🌊 Rill's Intercept & Professor Ink's Recruitment Pitch
* **First Responder:** Rill reaches Squad 907 near the Walker Core perimeter on her minimalist insectoid rig (*The Reed-Runner*).
* **Professor Ink's Mission:** Rill explains that Professor Ink needs cadets with deep cultural and resonance intuition (Britt, Aggie, Iggy) to join the **Scrivener Guild** for a dangerous expedition to study the "memories" stored in petrified pine bark.
* **Combat Aide Support:** In combat, Rill deploys her *Aether Signal Jammer*, pulsing a 30ft field that negates Mwaza-Chui's Signal Lock advantage over Britt and Aggie, while granting allies +1 to Attack rolls.

### 2. 🐆 The Mwaza-Chui Flashback (Aggie & Britt)
* **Mizizi Lore:** In the Nordic pine forest, Aether beasts are stunted due to the absence of a central node stone. When Britt and Aggie first established their telepathic mind-link network, their combined mental frequency acted like a portable mini-node.
* **The Scene:** A flashback opens the session. Young Britt and Aggie are sitting around a pine forest campfire late at night after establishing their link for the first time. Out of the dark underbrush steps a **Mwaza-Chui** (Thought/Memory Jaguar). Instead of attacking, the beast sits silently at the perimeter, purring in harmony with their Aether signal, using them like a warm battery.
* **The Elder Secret:** Mizizi elders actively hunt Mwaza-Chui because these beasts possess an immutable, generational memory of the *buried true node*. Agatha suspects this secret, while other elders simply declare the jaguars an infestation.
* **Session 10 Parallel:** When Aggie and Britt use their network link during the Apex Arena storm, a full-powered, hyper-intelligent Mwaza-Chui that breached the barrier locks onto their unique frequency, hunting them down across Sector 3.

### 3. 🪵 The Squad 06 Rescue (Bramble & Dr. Rose Halloway)
* **Distress Call:** During the storm, [[bramble|Bramble]] contacts Aggie via Kael's prototype study net frequency. Squad 06 (The Harvesters) is pinned down near the Petrified Pine Forest / Wetlands border (Zone 1/3) by a hunting **Gale-Wyrm**.
* **Tactical Choice:** Squad 907 & Rill can choose to rush to Squad 06's aid or focus solely on their own fortification. Rescuing Bramble and [[dr-rose-halloway|Dr. Rose Halloway]] brings Bramble's root-manipulation abilities and plant-kin intuition to Squad 907's camp, as well as securing Squad 06's alliance for future Captain backing!

### 4. ⚡ The Walker Core Collapse & Bureaucratic Bottleneck
* **The Breach (Zone 0):** The global resonance drop drains the Apex Ring grid. As the Walker attempts to draw power reserves, the high-voltage Resonator Coils fry, collapsing the Aether barrier.
* **Academy Failure:** Officers ignore early Walker Watch warnings from [[dee|Dee]], assuming it's minor grid strain. When the failure occurs, automated recall beacons fail to ignite.

## 🛠️ Survival & Fortification Mechanics (Scrap Utilization)

Party can combine Session 9 loot into defensive traps at the Timber Canyon Camp:
* **Bungee Tripwire Trap:** (Bungee Cords + Wire) Restrains Mwaza-Chui / Canopy Cats (DC 13 Finesse to set; DC 14 Agility escape).
* **Sticky Tar Moat:** (Tar Buckets) Halves enemy movement speed; ignites for 1d8 Fire damage if lit by torch.
* **Weighted Entangling Net:** (Lead Weights + Rope) Pins Gale-Wyrm to ground for 1 round (DC 13 Strength to throw).
* **Absorbent Barrier Wall:** (Sponge Boom + Tar) Absorbs Dagger Shark pressure waves and electrical arcs.
