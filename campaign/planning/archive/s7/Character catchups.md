### Britt (Sophie)

- **What You Have Learned:**

## Part 2: Study Path Selection

### Node: `study_textbooks`

- **Narrative:** The library's official manuals are beautifully bound in brass, but the language is ancient, dry, and heavily propagandized. You read through endless pages glorifying legendary tactical maneuvers like the "Crescent Sweep"—which your notes suggest might be a total fiction invented by the Scrivener Guild.
    
- **Roll Check:** Roll **Knowledge (DC 13)** to extract actual patterns from the corporate fluff.
    
- **Outcomes:**
    
    - **Success with Hope (13+):** You see right through the fluff. You realize the textbooks over-emphasize heavy machinery because it looks good for corporate sponsors. You gain the **"Scrivener's Skepticism"** clue and clear 1 Stress. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        
    - **Success with Fear (9-12):** You memorize the core formulas, but your head is spinning with useless dates and historical bias. You take 1 Stress from mental exhaustion but lock in the standard formulas. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        
    - **Failure (8 or less):** You get completely bogged down in dry prose. You find yourself memorizing the "Crescent Sweep" and "Magnetic Boots" rules, failing to realize they are massive traps designed to test real-world adaptability. You take 2 Stress. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        

### Node: `study_val_guide`

- **Narrative:** You smooth out the clean, elegant pages of the study guide Val wrote (and sold to Lucky for stories). Written in his flowing, immaculate cursive, the guide represents a genuine, painstaking attempt to map out the curriculum. You can sense his deep desire to be helpful—he wants his classmates to succeed. Yet, despite his brilliant, smooth academic mind, his high-society upbringing occasionally glosses over the grittier, blue-collar realities of survival, and at the end of the day, he is still just a single first-year student trying to predict the minds of tenured professors.
    
- **Roll Check:** Roll **Instinct or Presence (DC 12)** to read between the lines of his highly structured, polished summaries.
    
- **Outcomes:**
    
    - **Success with Hope (12+):** You connect with his clean, logical structures perfectly. You realize Val's earnest deductions map out exactly how a high-society mind analyzes the professors' lecture styles. You gain **"Val's Polished Guide"** and clear 1 Stress. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        
    - **Success with Fear (8-11):** Val's notes are beautifully thorough, but his elegant, conceptual summaries occasionally miss the raw practical reality of under-deck survival. You take 1 Stress trying to translate his polished diagrams into real-world mechanics, but you lock in the core annotations. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        
    - **Failure (7 or less):** You lose yourself in his complex, multi-clause sentences and high-theory terminology. Trying to parse his beautifully clean but overly complex formulas leaves your head spinning. Take 2 Stress. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        

### Node: `study_block_feed`

- **Narrative:** You slip on Kael's prototype Speaking Stone. The frequency buzzes with a high-pitched static before Pip's excited, biscuit-muffled voice blasts into your ear, followed by Kael's quiet, hyper-technical corrections and Bramble's steady sighing.
    
- **Roll Check:** Roll **Resonance or Knowledge (DC 12)** to filter the chaotic student chatter into usable data.
    
- **Outcomes:**
    
    - **Success with Hope (12+):** Kael's mathematical breakdowns are flawless. He explains that the "Dead Island" phenomenon represents a zone where local resonance reaches absolute zero. You lock in perfect technical understanding, gain **"Kael's Core Mechanics"**, and clear 1 Stress. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        
    - **Success with Fear (8-11):** You get some amazing insights, but Pip keeps interrupting the feed to scream about dining hall bacon ("dead pigs!"), causing a massive auditory spike in your Speaking Stone. You take 1 Stress but manage to retain the core notes. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
        
    - **Failure (7 or less):** The Speaking Stone's feedback loop glitches out, blasting raw, unshielded resonance directly into your temple. Your ears ring, your notes are scrambled, and you take 2 Stress. _(Proceed to `subject_choice`)_
