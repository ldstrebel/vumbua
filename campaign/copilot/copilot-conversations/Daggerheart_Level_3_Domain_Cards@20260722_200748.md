**ai**: In **Daggerheart**, the number of domain cards a player has is determined by their level. At **Level 3**, each player will have a total of **4 domain cards**.

Here is the progression breakdown:
- **Level 1:** 2 Domain Cards (one from each of the character's two domains).
- **Level 2:** +1 Domain Card (Total of 3).
- **Level 3:** +1 Domain Card (Total of 4).

### Key Rules to Remember for your Session 9:
- **Active vs. Vault:** Even though they have 4 cards at Level 3, players can have a maximum of **5 active cards** in their loadout at any time. Since they only own 4 cards at this level, they can have all of them active at once if they choose.
- **Level 2-3 Cards:** At Level 3, players can choose their new card from either the **Level 1** or **Level 2** domain decks (they do not get access to Level 3+ cards until they actually reach those levels).

Since your [[s9-plan]] involves the "Basalt Run" gauntlet and potential combat with ether beasts, ensuring your players have their 4th card selected will be important for their tactical options!
