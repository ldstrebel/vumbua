### **Lucky’s "Guaranteed Pass" Study Guide**
**(Lucky's Annotated Edition)**

> **Lucky’s Intro to Vumbua "Learning":**
> "Look, the Academy wants to turn you into a 'Venture Professional.' That’s just a fancy word for an **Ablative**—a part they can replace when it breaks. I didn't survive the wreck of the *Star-Crossed* by reading a manual. I survived because I knew when the ship was lying to me. Don't study to pass; study so you don't end up as a 'System Error' in some Archivist's ledger."

---

### **VOLUMETRIC CARTOGRAPHY**

**1. The Z-Axis Paradox**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "The manual says 'Synchronized Helix.' That’s how you get your hull twisted like a soda can. On the streets, if a crowd is moving one way, you don't walk *with* them—you find the gap and punch through. In the Mist, that’s the **Dead-Stick Drop**. Cut the engines, let gravity take the wheel, and ignite when you see the 'real' floor. It’s the only way to beat a Solar Gale without burning your sails to ash."

**2. Aether-Graph Plotting**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "I once saw a navigator try to map a 'Ghost-Archipelago' using a **Brass Sextant**. The Mist turned the brass into lead in his hands. If the gravity is shifting, trust the **Umber Crystal Weights**. They don't care about 'North' or 'South'; they only care about 'Down.' And in the Mist, 'Down' is the only direction that stays honest."

---

### **HARMONIC RESONANCE**

**3. The Feral Resonance**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "Aris calls it 'weeping spores.' I call it a 'Death-Rattle.' Before my old ship went down, the engine core started making that B-Minor sob. The Captain tried a 'Standard Overwrite'—basically shouting at the Node to shut up. It blew the bulkhead. If the Node is crying, use **Sympathetic Dissonance**. Match its misery. If you sound like the Node, it won't try to 'correct' your frequency (by killing you)."

**4. The Core Crack**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "If the casing is cracked, don't aim for the 'Geometric Center.' That’s for people who have time to do math. **Hit the Internal Core.** It’s like a street fight—you don't punch the guy's jacket; you punch his ribs. Inject the energy where it hurts, or the whole thing is going to pop."

---

### **REALITY ANCHOR DRILLS**

**5. The Anchor of Self**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "The teachers want you to visualize 'The Future' or 'The Law.' That’s for kids who have homes to go back to. When the Mist starts eating your mind, focus on **The Flaw**. Remember the scar on your arm? The hunger in your gut? The Mist can't fake your pain. Pain is the only thing that proves you’re still solid. Focus on the hurt until the world stops shaking."

**6. Spatial Distortion**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "If the gravity shifts 90 degrees, don't bother with 'Harmony Breathing.' I’ve seen 'monks' fall 40 feet into a turbine while they were still exhaling. **Grab a railing (External Bracing).** Your lungs won't keep you from breaking your neck, but a solid brass pipe will."

---

### **XENOBIOLOGICAL SCAVENGING**

**7. First Contact Protocol**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "The Academy says 'Lay down your weapons (Vulnerability).' If I did that on the Lower Docks, I’d be a corpse in five minutes. But **Projecting Strength (Authority)** works. If you look like you’re ready to burn the whole place down, the tribesmen usually wait for a better target. Don't be a victim, but don't be the first one to swing."

**8. The Toxic Bloom**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "If a flower causes paralysis, it’s not a 'resource.' It’s a trap. **Burn it on Sight.** I don't care how many 'Amps' it’s worth. You can't spend money if you’re a statue. If it’s too dangerous to touch, it’s too dangerous to keep."

---

### **CRISIS MANAGEMENT & HISTORY**

**9. The Sacrifice**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "This is the 'Ablative' test. The Academy wants to see if you’ll follow orders (Patch the Kitchen). **Patch the Engine.** I’ve spent a week in a dark hull eating cold paste because the engine was the only thing keeping the air-scrubbers humming. You can live without a hot meal, but you can't live without a heartbeat. The engine is the ship's heart. Everything else is just 'extra'."

**10. The Siren's Lag (Light-trails)**
*   **Lucky’s Note:** "I saw a deckhand walk straight off the port-side into a violet streak once. He had this look on his face—like he just found his lost mother. He didn't even scream when the **Leeches** hit him. If you see a light that makes you feel 'safe' or 'warm,' it’s a biological lie. It’s matching your heart rate to make you stop thinking. If you see your own ship's 'ghost' behind you, it means the predators are already timing their jump. **Don't look at the light.**"

---

If the players follow Lucky's advice:
