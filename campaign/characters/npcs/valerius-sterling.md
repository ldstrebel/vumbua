## Overview
Valerius Sterling is the older cousin of [[Valentine Sterling]] and a previous graduate of Vumbua Academy who never left. He hosts the popular (and possibly unauthorized) radio show that recaps academy events, maintaining his grip on the school's social narrative. He is named after his famous uncle, [[valentine-sterling-sr|Valentine Sterling Sr.]].

## Relationships
- **[[Valentine Sterling]]**: His younger cousin and the family Scion/Student. Valerius watches over him (and critiques his performance on air).
- **[[valentine-sterling-sr|Valentine Sterling Sr.]]**: His famous uncle and namesake.
- **[[Ember]]**: Employs her as a field reporter for his radio show to gather gossip and social stories.

## Personality
- **Public Persona:** Arrogant, perfect, and critical of "Variance" (chaos/unpredictability). He often antagonizes the party on air, calling them "Uncalibrated."

## GM Secrets

> [!warning]-
> The following information is not known to the player characters.

### Reso Race Sponsoring & The Core Swap
Valerius sponsors the second-year team **Shatter Stamper** (which is crewed by Ash-Bloods). Originally, the crew's main goal was to showcase their Ash-Blood core's fire and energy, but with the eleventh-hour cancellation of the Ash-Blood Spire (Ignite node) by the gamemakers/Scriveners, that became impossible. To give them a real chance of winning, they tried to swap in a Harmony-compatible replacement node (a late spec-change) that could harmonize with standard track pillars.

### Confiscation & The Impending Leak
This last-minute change was confiscated by Warden Rovaldi because it was submitted too late to be approved by the judges. Ember is pushing Valerius hard to do something about this. Valerius is preparing to use his next radio show broadcast to leak this unfair confiscation to stir up public outrage and pressure the gamemakers.

### The Hangar Compromise
Valerius doesn't know yet that his team's rig is fully compromised (disabled/crippled without the core) and that Finch Gable has already traded the news of the confiscation to one of his own club reporters/enforcers in exchange for access to the observer balloon.
