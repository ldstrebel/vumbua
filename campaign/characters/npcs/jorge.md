Jorge is a Dragonkin first-year candidate at Vumbua Academy and a classmate/friend of [[Valentine Sterling]]. He is supportive of Valentine and enjoys the tactical aspects of the Reso Race.

## Session Appearances

- **The Potato Map:** Found in the cafeteria with Valentine and Ludo, helping Valentine model the Reso Race track layout using hollowed-out potatoes and peas.
