# The Deep-Hull

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Permanent Core — ironclad submersible/steamship |
| **Parent Location** | [[Vumbua Academy]] |
| **Function** | Classrooms, Logistics, Mess Halls |
| **First Mentioned** | [[session-01\|Session 1]] |

The Deep-Hull is one of [[Vumbua Academy]]'s three permanent Cores — a massive ironclad steamship that resembles a Titanic-sized cruise ship. It is permanently moored in the deep bay of the [[Campus Harbor]], serving as the primary logistics base, mess hall, and floating classroom for the academy.

## What Players Know

Described during [[session-01|Session 1]]'s courtyard reveal as *"a monstrous ironclad cruise ship with brass fittings sitting in a harbor."* Gantry structures elegantly connect the central island in the harbor to the Deep-Hull, with bridges extending from there to the shore, allowing students and staff easy pedestrian access. The sheer scale of it dwarfed the students. It also serves as the primary storage and research vessel. It looks like a cruise ship but it is a titanic-esque ship with brass accents and a large open deck with glass canopies and only one large steamstack. It is a classroom first, cruise ship second.
