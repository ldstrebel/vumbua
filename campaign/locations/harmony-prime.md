# Harmony Prime (The Seat)

> *"The Seat of Harmony. It's the biggest city... huge. 8 million people."*
> — [[serra-vox|Serra Vox]], [[session-02\|Session 2]]

Harmony Prime is the static capital city of the Harmony Empire, far from the frontier where [[Vumbua Academy]] currently operates. It is the seat of the High Council and the administrative center of the empire.

To flesh out [[Harmony Prime]] (The Seat), we have to look at it as a vertical metropolis of 8 million people where your altitude is directly tied to your "Charge" (social and literal energy). 

Here are the primary families and factions that operate within the capital's spires:

### The Highborne (The Upper Spires)
These families control the fundamental resources of the empire and hold permanent seats on the Domestic Council.

### The Loreborne (The Mid-Spires)
The bureaucratic engine of the empire. They don't own the resources, but they control the *information* about them.

- **The [[scrivener-guild]]: The most powerful non-noble faction. They manage the **Receipt System** and the **Loom**. If you want to prove you exist in Harmony, you have to go through a Scrivener. They are based in the "Archive Spire" in the city center.
- **The Chronos Society**: A sub-faction of the Scriveners based in [[Bloomfield]]. They manage the empire's timekeeping and synchronization. They are the ones who ensure the mobile cities like [[Vumbua Academy]] stay on schedule.
- **The Aether-Merchants**: A loose collection of wealthy trade families (including the **Castellan Family**). They operate the luxury markets, dealing in "Aether-Wine," "Living Scent Wood" from [[Minnow]], and "Focus Glass" from [[Timon]].

---

### The Iron Union (The Lower Spires & Foundations)
The labor force that keeps the 8 million people alive. They live in the "Rust" and "Copper" tiers where the air is thick with steam.

- **The Grinders**: A massive labor union of boiler-workers, engineers, and maintenance crews. They are the ones who actually handle the **lavsidian** and the heavy machinery. [[loami]] would have strong ties to this culture.
- **The Silt-Walkers**: A faction of spies and couriers who use "Silent Silt" from [[Nstyl]] to move through the city unheard. They are the primary source of the "Academic Secrets" that [[ludo-castellan]] prizes.
- **The Battery-Scavengers**: A desperate underclass that hunts for "leaking" or discarded crystal batteries in the city's foundations. They are technically illegal but essential for the city's waste management.

---

### The Council of Outliers (The Diplomatic Quarter)
Because of the **Inverse Power Doctrine**, the most "primitive" or "wild" clans actually hold the most voting power in the capital to prevent the Highborne from steamrolling them.

- **The Ash-Blood Embassy**: Led by [[lady-ignis]]. Because the [[Ash-Bloods]] provide the most "Variance" (Wild Magic) to the grid, she holds the highest voting power on the Council.
- **The Mizizi Enclave**: A small, moss-covered district in the lower spires where [[Mizizi]] representatives live. It is the only place in the city where things are allowed to "die" naturally, which makes the neighbors very uncomfortable.
- **The Trench-Kin Shadows**: They have no official embassy (as they are "Hidden"), but rumors persist of a flooded level in the city's base where their representatives meet in secret.

---

### The Castellan Family (The Aspirants)
The Castellans don't fit into a major faction yet—that is their entire struggle.
- **Status**: High-tier Aether-Merchants.
- **Location**: The "Gilded-Adjacent" district (The 89th Floor).
- **The Goal**: To move from the "Mid-Spires" to the "Upper Spires" by securing a marriage or blood-oath with House Sterling. They are the ultimate "Social Climbers" of [[Harmony Prime]].

## What Players Know

[[serra-vox]] revealed during [[session-02|Session 2]] that the capital has approximately 8 million people. She slipped that she'd spent time there under "political pressure" — hinting at her hidden noble background as a member of House Vox.

[[lady-ignis]], the [[Ash-Bloods|Ash-Blood]] matriarch, serves on the High Council here. Under the [[Inverse Power Doctrine]], she holds the highest voting power of any council member.
