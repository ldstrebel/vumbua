# The Campus Harbor

The Campus Harbor is the primary maritime and aerial port of [[Vumbua Academy]]. It is a deep-water basin serving as the central anchorage for Vumbua's transport vessels, explorer airships, and the permanently moored Core vessel, the [[Deep-Hull]].

---

## Visual & Physical Description

### The Basin & The Grassy Islands
The harbor features a deep-water basin filled with deep, dark turquoise water that drops precipitously to a depth of approximately 1,700 feet. Low-profile grassy islands with stone features rise gently from the bay.
- **Mooring Points & Bridges:** Stone walkways and bridges elegantly connect these grassy islands to the mainland and to each other. These islands serve as the primary docking hubs for large vessels, including opulent solar-galleon airships with massive golden dirigible balloons, wood-paneled hulls, and rigging masts.
- **The Gantries:** Elegant gantry structures connect the harbor islands to the side of the permanently moored [[Deep-Hull]].

### The Entrance Gates & Parkway
The main landward entrance to the harbor area from the campus features:
- **Stone Gateposts:** Heavy stone pillars topped with ornate Victorian gas lamps casting a warm amber glow.
- **Wrought-Iron Gates:** Ornate black wrought-iron gates that open onto a manicured circular garden parkway with gravel paths and green hedges, leading directly down to the water's edge.

### Moored Vessels & Infrastructure
- **[[The Deep-Hull]]:** Moored permanently on the eastern side of the bay, acting as a massive floating classroom and logistics center.
- **The Glass Hangars:** A row of four massive, arched glass-and-steel hangars (including Hangar 12) stand at the south edge of the harbor basin. These hangars feature vaulted copper/brass roofing and large arched glass frontages that open directly onto the water channels for airship launch and recovery.
- **Docking Slips:** Wooden docks extend from the southeastern shore to accommodate smaller cargo skiffs, rowboats, and regional transport.

## Session Appearances

### Session 8
- **Iggy's Recovery:** Suffering from a severe hangover and dehydration, [[Iggy]] plunged off the harbor dock and cannonballed to the bottom of the 1,700-foot basin to recover under hydrostatic pressure.
- **Britt's Signaling:** [[Britt]] waded into the harbor and dropped pebbles (and eventually a large boulder) to signal Iggy to surface. Iggy emerged covered in seaweed and harbor silt, holding the stones in his arms.
