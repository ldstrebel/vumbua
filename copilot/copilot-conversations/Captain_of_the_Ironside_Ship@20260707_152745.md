Unlike the clean labs of the Academy, this room is cluttered. It’s where HTech is integrated with physical machinery.
- **NPC: Kaelen.** A brilliant but stressed senior who is failing his "Practical Results" quota. He is desperate for anything that generates power without HTech crystals.
- **GM Hook:** Kaelen has a prototype device that is vibrating at a frequency that attracts Aetherbeasts. He needs the players to "calibrate" it (or throw it overboard) before the ship is attacked.

#### 3. The Captain’s Bridge (Command)
The heart of the ship, filled with brass speaking tubes and glowing navigational crystals.
- **NPC: Captain Valentine.** A veteran of the Anchorage who believes in "The Long Hustle." He doesn't care about grades, only results.
- **GM Hook:** Valentine reveals he was the one who authorized the "high-stakes exploration" that killed Lucky's parents. He wants to know if Lucky is "ready to settle the debt" or if he's still just a street urchin.

#### 4. The "Ablative" Berths (Security & Storage)
The air is thick with the smell of engine grease and cheap rations. This is where the real work happens.
- **NPC: [[Sarge]].** He’s acting as the ship’s Master-at-Arms. He’s protective, gruff, and deeply suspicious of the "Silver Tier" students.
- **GM Hook:** Sarge pulls a player aside and hands them a "Survival-First" kit. He tells them that the ship isn't as stable as it looks and that Valentine is planning a "Pivot" that might get everyone killed.

#### 5. The Cargo Hold (The Underbelly)
A dark, echoing space. Crates are stacked high, many bearing the "Ambrosia of Luck" brand.
- **NPC: "Greasy" Jax.** A mechanic who knows every bolt on the ship. He’s a contact for Lucky and handles the "discarded" junk.

### Summary of GM "Work-Off" Points
1. **The Verticality:** Use the "Glass Pit" to create tension. If a fight breaks out, the threat of falling through the floor onto the racers below is a great environmental hazard.
2. **The "Sus" Economy:** Everything on this ship is a hustle. NPCs shouldn't just give information; they should trade it for "Practical Results" or "Old School" scraps.
3. **Sarge’s Secret:** [[Sarge]] is clearly working against the "Silver Tier" establishment from the inside. He can be a powerful ally or a dangerous obstacle if the players are seen as "Academy Loyalists."
4. **The Race Connection:** Events in the Reso Race below should physically affect the ship (e.g., a massive explosion below causes the *Aurelian Sovereign* to tilt, sending drinks and seniors sliding across the glass).
