# Lucky’s "Guaranteed Pass" Guide
**"I didn't read the books, but I saw the sparks. That's basically the same thing."**

> **Lucky’s Intro:**
> "Look, the Academy wants to turn you into a 'Venture Professional.' That’s just a fancy word for an **Ablative**—a part they can replace when it breaks. I didn't survive the wreck of the *Star-Crossed* by reading a manual. I survived because I knew when the ship was lying to me. If you don't know the answer, pick the one that sounds the most expensive or the one that keeps you from getting poked."

---

### **VOLUMETRIC CARTOGRAPHY**

**1. The Z-Axis Paradox**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "The teacher kept talking about 'Helix' this and 'Helix' that. Sounds like a fancy way to get a headache. When the *Star-Crossed* hit the gale, the floor became the ceiling and I just fell. If the wind is pushing you, just let go. if you fall fast enough, the wind can't catch you. *Probably.*"

**2. Aether-Graph Plotting**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "They have these shiny brass tools, but brass gets hot and itchy in the Mist. I saw a guy once using a **Silver-lined Graph**. It looked expensive, so it must be the one that works. If it’s shiny and costs a lot of Amps, the Academy probably wants you to use it."

---

### **HARMONIC RESONANCE**

**3. The Feral Resonance**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "Aris says the Node is 'weeping.' I don't know what a 'B-Minor' is, but when things cry, they want to be left alone. If you try to 'Overwrite' it, you’re just being a bully. I’d just **Wait and Observe (Passive Observation).** If you don't poke the glowing thing, it won't poke you back. That’s how I survived the Lower Docks."

**4. The Core Crack**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "If something is broken, you fix the break. Duh. **The Crack Itself.** Why would you fire energy at the 'Geometric Center'? That’s just empty space. You gotta plug the hole. It’s like a leak in a canteen—you put your thumb on the hole, not the middle of the bottle."

---

### **REALITY ANCHOR DRILLS**

**5. The Anchor of Self**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "The Mist makes you forget who you are. Sarge says the 'Law' is for people with clean boots. I say you gotta think about **The Flaw.** Remember that time you tripped and everyone laughed? Or the scar I got when the bulkhead slammed my arm? The Mist can't make up a memory that hurts that much. If it hurts, it’s real. Focus on the pain."

**6. Spatial Distortion**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "If the room flips, don't 'breathe.' You can't breathe if you're upside down and your lungs are in your throat. **Grab a railing.** I spent three hours hanging onto a pipe when the *Star-Crossed* lost its gravity. My hands bled, but I didn't drift away. Hold on tight and don't let go."

---

### **XENOBIOLOGICAL SCAVENGING**

**7. First Contact Protocol**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "If a guy has a spear, he wants your stuff. If you 'Lay down your weapons,' you’re just making it easier for him. You gotta **Project Strength.** Stand tall, look 'em in the eye, and act like you have a bigger spear hidden somewhere. People don't mess with things that look like they'll bite back."

**8. The Toxic Bloom**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "A flower that makes you go stiff? That’s not a resource, that’s a weapon. **Priority Extraction.** If I can get that thing into a jar without touching it, I can sell it to the guys in the Black Market for a fortune. Use tongs, use a bucket, just get it in the bag. Amps are Amps."

---

### **CRISIS MANAGEMENT & HISTORY**

**9. The Sacrifice**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "This is a trick. If you patch the engine, you can fly, but you'll starve. If you patch the kitchen, you can eat, but you're stuck in the dark. I like eating. **Patch the Kitchen.** Sarge can fix an engine with a wrench and some spit, but nobody can fix a 'not-having-sandwiches' problem."

**10. The Siren's Lag**
*   **Lucky’s Guess:** "I saw these violet trails once when I was hiding in the vents of a freighter. They’re beautiful. They look like... I don't know, like a warm blanket. I think they’re **Shortcuts.** If the Mist is dark and scary, and there’s a bright purple road, why wouldn't you take it? It’s probably a 'Gift' from the Aether."
