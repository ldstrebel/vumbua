# Surveyor's Compass

A navigation device discovered by the player characters during Intake Week.

## What the Players Know

The Surveyor's Compass was found by [[Britt]] inside an abandoned explorer's cache near a runoff pipe outside the city basin on Wednesday afternoon.

### Description and Appearance
- It is a pristine navigation device housed in a leather pouch.
- The dial points consistently in one stable direction (towards [[Harmony Prime]]).
- The tip of the dial features the sunburst logo representing [[Harmony Prime]].
- The back of the device has a carved inscription: *"momentum is life always towards home."*

### Mechanics
- **Navigation Bonus:** Grants a `+2` bonus on navigation checks.
- **Rerolls:** Allows the user to spend `1 Hope` to reroll a navigation check.

### Origin and Fungal Decay
The compass belonged to a Harmony explorer from 10 cycles ago. The Mizizi elders buried the cache and engineered alert mushrooms to protect their isolation secrets. Handling the compass seeps ancient fungal decay into Britt's skin. To cure this decay and receive a fragmented knowledge vision of the past, Britt must seek out Elder Zaza in the Mizizi Forest.
