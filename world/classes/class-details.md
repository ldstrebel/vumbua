# Academy Class Reference: Monday - The Great Filter

This document details the core classes held in Hangar 12 (The "Echo Chamber") and specialized labs during the first week of the Academy.

---

## 1. Intro to Resonance
- **Instructor:** [[professor-kante|Professor Kante]]
- **Location:** Hangar 12 - The Central Pillar
- **Aesthetic:** Kante stands atop a 40-foot brass pillar that vibrates with a low thrum. His voice isn't just loud; it resonates in the students' chests.
- **Opening Line:** *"Look at your hands. Feel the hum in your marrow. You are not solid; you are a frequency. And by Friday, I will determine if you are a symphony or just noise."*
- **Teaching Style:** **Amplified Lecture.** He uses the Central Pillar to pulse the room, forcing students to 'tune' their tablets to his frequency to receive notes.
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#MODULE 2: HARMONIC RESONANCE|Module 2: Harmonic Resonance]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"It's all about the C-Major Pulse. Protocol is king. Just follow the rhythm and you won't explode."*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"Kante is a romantic. He thinks Nodes are alive. If the test asks about a weeping node, don't use a pulse—match the frequency. He wants 'Sympathetic Dissonance.' Write that down."*



## 2. Volumetric Cartography
- **Instructor:** Senior Exploranaut [[hesperus|Hesperus]]
- **Location:** Hangar 12 - North Navigation Tier
- **Aesthetic:** Surrounded by flickering blue holographic star-charts that seem to drift and warp in real-time. He paces like a caged animal.
- **Opening Line:** *"If you trust your eyes in the Mist, you’re already dead. If you trust the manual, you’re just a well-read corpse. Now, who can tell me why the Z-axis is a liar?"*
- **Teaching Style:** **Reactive Charting.** He throws 'ghost-islands' into the holographic field and shouts at students to plot a course before the island 'dissolves.'
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#MODULE 1: VOLUMETRIC CARTOGRAPHY|Module 1: Volumetric Cartography]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"Recalibrate the sextant every hour. Standard procedure. Keep it simple."*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"Hesperus hates the manuals. He mentioned the 'Needle-Thread' maneuver three times today. If there's a question about a rotating island, the manual answer (Synchronized Helix) is a trap. Choose the Needle-Thread."*

## 3. Aetheric Ballistics
- **Instructor:** Instructor Hallow
- **Location:** Hangar 12 - South Logistics Deck
- **Aesthetic:** A series of heavy brass cannons and supply-cranes. Static arcs dance between the terminals. Hallow wears a flight suit caked in dry aether-oil.
- **Opening Line:** *"Trajectory is the difference between a landing and a crater. I don't care if you can do the math in your head—can you do it while your ship is on fire and the windows are blacked out?"*
- **Teaching Style:** **Kinetic Drills.** He literally throws objects (chalk, wrenches, weighted pods) at students to test their predictive reflexes.
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#PRACTICAL: THE BLIND FLY|Module 5: Practical: The Blind Fly]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"Watch the dials. The compass never lies, even if the windows are blacked out."*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"Hallow hit Ignatius with chalk today because he didn't 'feel' it coming. The exam's Blind Fly isn't a math problem—it's an Instinct check. If the windows black out, trust your marrow, not the compass."*

## 4. Resonance Biology
- **Instructor:** Doctor Aris
- **Location:** Hangar 12 - East Bio-Containment Tiers
- **Aesthetic:** Glass tanks filled with bioluminescent moss and chittering resonance-leeches. The air smells of ozone and wet earth.
- **Opening Line:** *"Life is a persistent frequency error in a chaotic universe. Today, we will learn how to harvest that error without shattering the delicate math that keeps it alive."*
- **Teaching Style:** **Projected Dissection.** She uses a microscopic camera to project 30-foot tall images of pulsating alien organs, pointing out the 'resonance nodes' within.
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#MODULE 4: Xenobiological Scavenging|Module 4: Xenobiological Scavenging]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"Scavenge everything. If it's rare, it's valuable. We're here to discover, right?"*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"Aris gave a twenty-minute lecture on the 'distress' of sentient mineral veins. She's a preservationist. If you see a 'Void-Lily', the 'correct' answer isn't to harvest it—it's to falsify the report and leave the ecosystem intact."*

 The "Navigational Lure" (The Trap)
The Concept: This connects Resonance Biology to Hesperus's Volumetric Cartography.

The Question for Players: "Hesperus warns that Light-trails are the 'siren songs' of the Mist. Biologically speaking, why does a ship's crew feel a physical compulsion to steer toward them?"
The Answer: The Light-trails emit a frequency that harmonizes with the human inner ear. It creates a hypnotic lull and a biological sense of "home" or "safety," overriding the pilot's logic and putting them to sleep.
Future Plan: Force a "Will" or "Resonance" save. The players see a beautiful violet trail (the Light-trail culture) and every instinct tells them it’s the way out, but they begin to feel incredibly drowsy. If they fail, they are lulled to sleep even though their instruments (and Hesperus’s training) say the trail leads straight into the maws of massive aquatic beasts.

## 5. Survival Ethics
- **Instructor:** [[Dean Isolde Vane]] (via Hologram)
- **Location:** Hangar 12 - The High Podium
- **Aesthetic:** A flickering, 50-foot tall translucent projection of the Dean looking down on the students. Her image is perfectly steady, while the room around it seems to blur.
- **Opening Line:** *"Exploration is the art of choosing who dies so that the Venture may live. You think you are heroes? By the end of this hour, you will realize you are just accountants of human life."*
- **Teaching Style:** **Pressure Scenarios.** The holograms surrounding the students shift into 'The Sacrifice' scenario—fire, alarms, and a countdown. They must lock in their decision on their terminals within seconds.
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#MODULE 5: CRISIS MANAGEMENT|Module 5: Crisis Management ("The Sacrifice")]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"Always patch the Engine Room. No engine, no ship. Simple math."*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"The Dean's scenario is a trick about exits. She said, 'The captain who stays to patch the kitchen is a saint, but the captain who leads their crew to the airlock is a leader.' Don't patch either—look for the exit strategy."*

## 6. Aetheric History
- **Instructor:** Archivist Thorne
- **Location:** Hangar 12 - West Archive Overflow
- **Aesthetic:** Towering shelves of physical scrolls and heavy brass data-slates. The air is cool and smells of dust and electrostatic discharge.
- **Opening Line:** *"History is the only anchor that prevents us from drifting back into the Great Silence. If you forget where you came from, the Aether will be happy to remind you by unmaking your very soul."*
- **Teaching Style:** **Ancestral Recitation.** He reads from the Founding House logs in a dry, rhythmic drone, pausing only to eye-contact anyone who stops writing.
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#MODULE 3: REALITY ANCHOR DRILLS|Module 3: Reality Anchor Drills ("The Law")]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"Memorize the 12 Houses. The timestamps of the Great Desync are the key."*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"Thorne admitted today that the 'Shatter' timelines are messy. Logical anchors are brittle. If the mist starts dissolving your ego, happy thoughts won't save you. Focus on your 'Flaw'—the parts of you that are too ugly to dissolve."*

## 7. Aetheric Defense
- **Instructor:** Instructor Kaelen
- **Location:** The Gravity Well (Sub-level beneath Hangar 12)
- **Aesthetic:** A circular room with reinforced blast-glass windows looking into a central vacuum chamber. Kaelen stands inside the chamber, gravity seemingly not affecting her heavy armor.
- **Opening Line:** *"The Aether hates you. It wants to pull the air from your lungs and the weight from your bones. Today, we learn how to tell the universe 'No'."*
- **Teaching Style:** **Stress-Field Induction.** She slowly increases the gravity in the room while lecturing. Students must maintain their 'Reality Anchors' just to stay standing.
- **Exam Alignment:** [[Gened exam#PRACTICAL: THE DENSITY CHAMBER|Module 3: Practical: The Density Chamber]]
- **Val's Guess (Morning):** *"Just grab a railing. If gravity triples, use the environment to hold you down."*
- **Val's Guess (After Class):** *"Kaelen says 'The environment is your enemy.' If gravity hits 400%, the railings will buckle. You have to create an internal pressure core—don't lean on the room, be the room's center."*
