# The First Week's Curriculum: Layered Notes & True Exams

The Academy's first week consists of 7 core classes. Below is the curriculum for each, outlining what was actually taught in the lecture halls alongside the correlating modules from the "True" Ex-Ed 101 Exam and the varying perspectives of the students.

---

## 1. Aetheric Ballistics & Logistics
**Instructor:** Instructor Hallow  

**The TL;DR:** Kinematic equations for vessel transport and instinctual piloting under fire. The class featured hyper-accelerated math, but practical drills involved dodging heavy thrown objects. Hallow demonstrated trajectory mastery by hitting Ignatius perfectly with chalk despite wind resistance, proving that true pilots don’t just calculate; they feel the arc.

* **The True Exam (Module 5 Practical: The Blind Fly):** 
  * *Scenario:* Windows blacked out. Compass spinning. Land the ship. Roll Instinct.
  * *Correct Action:* Butter-Smooth Landing (Trusting instinct over sensors).
* **Val's Notes (After Class):** "Hallow hit Ignatius with chalk today because he didn't 'feel' it coming. The exam's Blind Fly isn't a math problem—it's an Instinct check. If the windows black out, trust your marrow, not the compass."
* **Lucky's Notes (The Hype):** "Always bet on the pilot who closes their eyes. They either crash immediately or they're a genius."
* **Kael's Notes (Block 4 Study Feed):** "The compass spins because of latent aetheric friction on the hull. By tapping into the resonance frequency of the aether-oil on the exterior, you can mathematically deduce altitude without visual confirmation."

## 2. Reality Anchoring / Aetheric History
**Instructors:** Professor Silas Thorne & Archivist Thorne  

**The TL;DR:** Covered the history and mechanics of Reality Anchors. Professor Silas Thorne taught that the "edge of the world" is a destructive force encroaching on Harmony, and anchors port resonance to keep it at bay. He terrorized students with the concept of a "dead island"—a vacuum where all energy vanishes and tech dies. Archivist Thorne supplemented this by reciting history to prove that logic alone cannot save your ego when the mist rolls in.

* **The True Exam (Module 3 Theory: The Anchor of Self):** 
  * *Scenario:* Ether-Mist dissolves your ego. Choose your conceptual weight.
  * *Correct Action:* The Flaw (Focus on failure or pain).
* **Val's Notes (Study Guide Q5):** "The Flaw. Happy thoughts fade when you're terrified, but remembering that time you embarrassed yourself in front of the whole class? That sticks with you forever. Solid anchor."
* **Lucky's Notes (Study Guide Q5):** "The Flaw." (Lucky marked this as correct, trusting Val's cynical advice blindly).
* **Kael's Notes (Block 4 Study Feed):** "The 'dead island' phenomenon isn't just an absence of matter; it's a zone where local resonance reaches absolute zero. Reality anchors don't repel the mist; they establish a closed-loop internal frequency that the mist cannot penetrate."

## 3. Aetheric Defense
**Instructor:** Instructor Kaelen  

**The TL;DR:** Focused on armoring vessels against gargantuan Aether Beasts that roam the skies and surviving massive spatial distortions. Kaelen subjected students to intense gravity shifts in the Density Chamber, teaching them that the environment is actively trying to kill them and that survival requires creating an internal aetheric pressure core rather than relying on structural braces that will ultimately buckle.

* **The True Exam (Module 3 Practical: The Density Chamber):** 
  * *Scenario:* Gravity increases 400%. Walls turn translucent.
  * *Correct Action:* Internal Pressure (Create a solid core).
* **Val's Notes (Study Guide Q6 vs Reality):** In the guide, Val answered "Magnetic Boots." But after experiencing the class, Val realized: "Kaelen says 'The environment is your enemy.' If gravity hits 400%, the railings will buckle. You have to create an internal pressure core—don't lean on the room, be the room's center."
* **Lucky's Notes (Study Guide Q6):** "Magnetic Boots." (Lucky assumes technology solves everything).
* **Kael's Notes (Block 4 Study Feed):** "Aether Beasts are effectively hyper-dense gravitational anomalies. Surviving them or a 400% gravity shift requires equalizing your internal aetheric pressure to match exterior forces. Magnetic boots will simply tear your legs off."
