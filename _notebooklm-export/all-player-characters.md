## Appearance

A turtle-mushroom hybrid of the [[Mizizi]] clan. She has light grey, textured turtle-like skin, expressive green eyes, and neat shoulder-length white hair adorned with a green leaf crown. She wears a simple white linen dress with vine embroidery. Her shell is a vibrant **red-and-white spotted mushroom** (Amanita cap) which she can retreat fully into. Moss, mycelia, and fungal growths are natural features.

## Personality

Aggie is **cautious, curious, and quietly suspicious**. Compared to [[Britt]], she's less aggressive in her questioning of Harmony, but she shares the same core concerns. She's wary in a quiet, accusatory way — she believes Harmony is responsible for something, she just doesn't know what yet. She goes with the flow while keeping her eyes open, and gravitates toward dark corners and safe spots when overwhelmed.

**Key Traits:**
- Thoughtful and observant — watches before she acts
- Less confrontational than [[Britt]], but shares the same suspicions
- Diplomatic instinct — "just trying to be nice to everyone, not piss anyone off"
- Gravitates toward comfort zones — looks for dark, fungal-feeling spaces when stressed
- Curious enough to want to explore: "Curiosity is growing. She wants to see more."
- Shares concerns privately rather than voicing them openly
- The quieter investigator to Britt's louder agitator

## Background

Like [[Britt]], Aggie grew up in the [[Mizizi Petrified Forest|Petrified Forest]] with the [[Mizizi]] clan's unique death-and-decay philosophy. She and Britt are cousins — same year at "turtle camp" — who bonded over shared gossip and concerns about Harmony. They weren't best friends growing up (different social groups), but their shared suspicions drew them closer, and now they have a mission together.

Aggie's approach is different from Britt's: where Britt wants to infiltrate and confront, Aggie wants to observe and uncover the conspiracy quietly. She believes Harmony is responsible for what's happening to their people — she just needs proof.

### What She Knows
- The forest has stopped dying naturally, causing deep concern
- Harmony has been trading with the [[Mizizi]] elders — tools and weapons for forest materials
- Her clan's relationship with outsiders is new and uncertain
- The clan's lore says the forest keeps them apart from the world for a reason

### Approach

Britt is **sure-footed, outspoken, and fiercely protective**. Where her cousin [[Aggie]] might be swept along by the crowd, Britt holds her own — throwing elbows when necessary to push through. She's the more aggressive of the pair: cynical, passionate, and suspicious of Harmony's motives. ## Background

Born in the [[Mizizi Petrified Forest|Petrified Forest]] of the [[Mizizi]] homeland, Britt grew up with the clan's unique philosophy on death and decay — *when things stop dying, we start dying*. When Harmony arrived on the shore and began working with the elders, Britt watched with suspicion as "the paperman" brought tools and weapons in exchange for forest materials. She and [[Aggie]] are cousins, same year at "turtle camp," who bonded over shared concerns about what Harmony really wants.

Britt didn't come to the academy for adventure. She came because *something is wrong*, and she can't discover what from the outside. She's here to infiltrate — or at least to investigate from within.

### What She Knows
- The forest has been "not dying" which is deeply concerning to her people

- Previous [[Mizizi]] youth attended Vumbua last cycle
- The clan uses the mycelium highway network to travel through the forest
- The clan's lore says the forest exists to keep them apart from the world — "when the world comes, things stop dying"

### Suspicions
- Something is fundamentally wrong with the Harmony relationship
- The elders are distributing Harmony's tools without understanding the full picture
- The academy isn't what it seems — "What is hiding below the surface? Because something is."

## GM Secrets

> [!WARNING]- GM Secrets
> - **The Compass Origin & Elder Secret:** The Surveyor's Compass found by Britt belonged to another Harmony explorer who successfully crossed the ocean and made contact with the Mizizi clan a decade ago. The Mizizi elders kept this visit a secret to protect their isolation, buried the explorer's cache, and engineered specific alert mushrooms around it.
> - **Fungal Decay Curse:** Because Britt disturbed the cache and handled the compass, the protective spores of the alert mushrooms have seeped into her skin. She will soon begin to rapidly decay—the elders' ancient security mechanism to prevent knowledge of the outside world from spreading.
> - **The Quest for Elder Zaza:** The only way to cure the decay is to return to the forest and seek out **Zaza**, the oldest elder and caretaker of the forest secrets (planned for around Session 10). Zaza will reluctantly explain the forest's true purpose: it is a repository for storing ancient knowledge. She will direct Britt into the forest's ruins to retrieve the knowledge that can save her, teaching her that their true calling is not death and destruction, but preservation and growth.
> - **The Fragmented Vision:** This revelation will be delivered to Britt as a fragmented, hallucinatory vision. Aggie will also experience this vision as she anchors Britt in the mycelial network. The GM will write this as a script for the two players to piece together.

## Appearance

A small, clay-and-soil-kin creature of the Earthkin origin. His body is composed of packed dirt and clay, with green moss and small sprouts growing from the top of his head. He has giant, wide-eyed brown eyes covered by oversized round copper **goggles** filled with protective fluid/water to keep them moist. Extremely light-sensitive — starts **steaming** in sunlight and is always applying a version of sunscreen. Pressure-sensitive — feels relief when weight is applied, wearing a dark, oversized heavy wool robe/coat.

## Personality

Iggy is **bewildered, curious, and unconventional**. He navigates the world with a child-like wonder that sometimes causes chaos — like destroying a testing cubicle's throughput by using the receipt paper as a tissue. Inspired by **The Mole from Atlantis** — grumpy, comedic, chronically sickly.

**Key Traits:**
- Constantly confused about his surroundings — genuinely doesn't know how civilization works
- Curious to the point of causing problems — prone to "shiny penny" moments
- Secretly enjoys being included — when invited to the bonfire he was thrilled, but he'll never admit it
- His grumpy side emerges when people interfere with what he wants to do
- Not anti-social, just unaccustomed to attention
- Incredibly secretive — lots of mumbling to himself, cursing at steaming, covering his tracks
- "If you have a god, please pray" — what attendants say after meeting him
- Takes zigzagging, indirect routes everywhere to absorb information
- Deeply cares for his people despite them never caring for him

## Background

Iggy's origins are mysterious — even to himself. He emerged from the water near the docks and was given a trench coat by a stranger before making his way to the academy. He covers his tracks obsessively and doesn't want anyone to trace where he came from.

- Parents were on the council — gave him access to most places back home
- Found an obsidian tablet sent by the [[Ash-Bloods]] rejecting something called the Exchange
- Didn't fit in with the normal crowd — always the odd one out
- Has a hidden "treasure trove" of collected trinkets and findings
- Has never been caught sneaking anywhere — combination of parental access, nobody caring, and a natural ability to blend in
- "The nature of his people — they're so one with the soil, they are part of the earth"

### What He Doesn't Know
- Basically everything about how civilization works
- What a receipt is
- Where he is
- Why things work the way they do

### What He Now Knows (Session 2.5)
- The [[The Power System|Global Amplitude]] measures Harmony's total ether power — and it's declining
- The Ash-Blood integration only produced ~20 amps (expected +300 to +800)
- Crystal batteries (Panda 5) are surge regulators, not storage
- Integration works through cultural connection, not resource extraction
- The Night of Sparks burned the world for two months before umber crystals existed
- "Trail tales from the Trench" — his people are known to Harmony as border defenders
- His worldview is being challenged: he was told connection with the outside world would destroy his people, but [[Professor Kante]] argues the opposite

## Skills & Abilities

**Daggerheart Mechanics (revised in Session 2.5):**
- **Passive stealth** — a stress-triggered blending ability he doesn't realize he's using. When stressed, he unconsciously blends into his surroundings. He may crash and wake up in a random place a day later.
- **Active earth manipulation** — spending hope to intentionally move or shape earth, tied to hyperfixation states
- **Strange Patterns:** When Iggy rolls a **7** on either die, he gains a hope and clears a stress

**Natural Talents:**
- Exceptional technical/mechanical intuition — figured out battery design philosophy before Kante explained it
- Detailed sketching and note-taking
- Natural stealth (earth-bonded physiology)
- Information absorption — takes winding routes specifically to observe everything

## Appearance

A rugged, broad-shouldered humanoid mechanic. He has short brown hair, brown eyes (one showing a subtle green tint), a short beard, and is perpetually smudged with dark engine grease and soot. He wears a brown woolen flat cap with an Italian flag ribbon, a heavy canvas collar, and metallic shoulder plating/pauldrons over his working shirt. His appearance is that of a seasoned, blue-collar union worker.

## Personality

Loami is **practical, worker-conscious, and quick-witted**. His immediate instinct upon seeing the overwhelmed exam attendants was to check on their working conditions. He has strong ties to labor and the boiler worker community, and never misses a chance to remind people that the infrastructure doesn't maintain itself.

**Key Traits:**
- Union-minded ("family born and raised, boiler mega-union")
- Concerned about worker treatment — stops to chat with attendants, checks on conditions
- Smooth talker — comfortable with both the working class (Sarge, Lucky) and the upper-crust (Percy)
- Practical and observant — notices things others miss
- Down-to-earth despite the extraordinary circumstances
- Quick to improvise — his "noises in the boiler room" cover story was seamless
- Proud of public infrastructure: "This city would fall apart without people like Loami"

## Background

Unlike the clan characters, Loami is a **Harmony native** from the Octoumba continent — where the previous version of Vumbua was located. He comes from a **long line of boiler workers** — his family has the original boiler schematics hung up in their living room. He can trace his lineage all the way back to the founding of Harmony, where his ancestors built the first boiler.

He's been working as a maintenance person at the academy during its construction, watching it transform from "just a boat and a ship" to a functioning campus. His family and co-workers pushed him to apply for the explorer trials: *"Someone's got to fix the ship if it goes wrong."*

### What He Knows
- The academy (Vumbua) moves every 50 years to a new location
- The two main vessels (ironclad cruise ship and massive airship) form the academy's core
- Islands slowly drift toward each other over centuries
- The discovery of two new clans ([[Mizizi]] and [[Ash-Bloods|Ash Bloods]]) is the biggest news in a thousand years
- No major civilizations had been found in ages — only ancient bones
- The academy uses [[lavsidian]] from the [[Ash-Blood Isles|Ash-Blood Isles]] to fuel the boilers

### The Explorer Dream
Like many Harmony citizens, Loami has been swept up in the resurgence of explorer fever caused by the new discoveries. Getting Explorer rank is like becoming "an NFL star, a bull rider" — instant celebrity and lifetime government stipend. His family sees him as the "big smart guy" who works at the academy and figures he should make the jump.

- **Mechanical knowledge** — deep familiarity with how Harmony's machines and power systems work

## Appearance

Humanoid but perpetually dirty — you don't really know where skin ends and where dirt begins. Like Pigpen from Peanuts, there's always a cloud of grime and grease around him. He doesn't feel the need to shower after a shift. His Earthkin heritage is distant enough that he looks mostly human, but the connection to earth shows in a subtle, ever-present layer of soot and engine grease.

## Personality

Lomi is **practical, worker-conscious, and quick-witted**. His immediate instinct upon seeing the overwhelmed exam attendants was to check on their working conditions. He has strong ties to labor and the boiler worker community, and never misses a chance to remind people that the infrastructure doesn't maintain itself.

**Key Traits:**
- Union-minded ("family born and raised, boiler mega-union")
- Concerned about worker treatment — stops to chat with attendants, checks on conditions
- Smooth talker — comfortable with both the working class (Sarge, Lucky) and the upper-crust (Percy)
- Practical and observant — notices things others miss
- Down-to-earth despite the extraordinary circumstances
- Quick to improvise — his "noises in the boiler room" cover story was seamless
- Proud of public infrastructure: "This city would fall apart without people like Lomi"

## Background

Unlike the clan characters, Lomi is a **Harmony native** from the Octoumba continent — where the previous version of Vumbua was located. He comes from a **long line of boiler workers** — his family has the original boiler schematics hung up in their living room. He can trace his lineage all the way back to the founding of Harmony, where his ancestors built the first boiler.

He's been working as a maintenance person at the academy during its construction, watching it transform from "just a boat and a ship" to a functioning campus. His family and co-workers pushed him to apply for the explorer trials: *"Someone's got to fix the ship if it goes wrong."*

### What He Knows
- The academy (Vumbua) moves every 50 years to a new location
- The two main vessels (ironclad cruise ship and massive airship) form the academy's core
- Islands slowly drift toward each other over centuries
- The discovery of two new clans ([[Mizizi]] and [[Ash-Bloods|Ash Bloods]]) is the biggest news in a thousand years
- No major civilizations had been found in ages — only ancient bones
- The academy uses [[lavsidian]] from the [[Ash-Blood Isles|Ash-Blood Isles]] to fuel the boilers

### The Explorer Dream
Like many Harmony citizens, Lomi has been swept up in the resurgence of explorer fever caused by the new discoveries. Getting Explorer rank is like becoming "an NFL star, a bull rider" — instant celebrity and lifetime government stipend. His family sees him as the "big smart guy" who works at the academy and figures he should make the jump.

## Skills & Experiences

- **Boiler operations** — lifetime of hands-on experience with furnaces, steam systems, and infrastructure
- **Mechanical knowledge** — deep familiarity with how Harmony's machines and power systems work
- **Social instincts** — reads people well, remembers shared experiences ("that one time you ordered a drink that was way too strong")
- **Improvisation** — quick on his feet when a cover story or smooth talk is needed
- **Infrastructure awareness** — understands how cities function at a systems level
