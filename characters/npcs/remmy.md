## Appearance
A Caucasian halfling female nurse with fair skin and kind features. Wears a clean white linen apron over a dark grey tunic, and a small, tilted red cloth hat on her short wavy brown hair.


## Overview
Remmy is a halfling nurse and lieutenant stationed at the Vumbua Academy infirmary. She wears a white apron and a small, slanted red hat. She handles medical concerns, hangovers, and potential contamination reports for students at the academy.

## Personality
Practical, no-nonsense, and supportive of student safety. She prioritizes diagnosing and containing unknown hazards, and she operates as a confidential resource for students, advising them to report external contaminants to professional recovery teams rather than handling them themselves.

- Monitored a heavily hungover [[Iggy]] on an infirmary cot, ordering water instead of coffee.
