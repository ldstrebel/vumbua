## Overview
Vex was a star student in the Rig-Engineering department until they were caught "borrowing" components from the faculty lab to build a private hover-bike. Now, they live in the shadows of Pit-Row, making a living by helping others bypass the very security systems they once helped design.

## Personality
- **Bitter:** Still resents the Academy for "stifling their genius."
- **Professional:** Takes great pride in a clean job. If Vex jacks a rig, it doesn't have a scratch on it.
- **Daring:** Loves the adrenaline of the heist more than the coin.

## Hooks for PCs
- **The Competitor:** Vex is planning to steal the *Glass-Sail* (Team 5) during the Tuesday night shift. If the PCs are there, they might encounter Vex in the rafters.
- **The Mentor:** If a PC shows interest in rig-jacking, Vex might offer to teach them how to bypass a **Soul-Sync**—for a price.
- **The Distraction:** Vex needs someone to trigger the fire suppression system in Hangar 4 so they can slip out with a "hot" rig. They'll offer the PCs a "Ghost-Sync" module in exchange.

## Relationships
- **[[hollow-jack]]**: Frequent business partner. Jack finds the buyers; Vex finds the rigs.
- **[[sergeant-krell]]**: Has a "professional understanding" with the Sergeant. Krell doesn't see Vex, and Vex doesn't cause trouble on Krell's watch.
