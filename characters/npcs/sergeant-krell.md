## Overview
Krell is a career guard who has seen a dozen Resonance Races. He's tired of the "Academy Elite" looking down on the security staff. He maintains a facade of absolute discipline, but his loyalties were bought long ago by the **Iron Union**.

## Personality
- **Stern:** To the students, he is a wall of iron.
- **Weary:** He's seen too many "scions" get away with sabotage.
- **Calculating:** He only takes risks that have a high probability of remaining undetected.

## Hooks for PCs
- **The Blind Eye:** If the PCs are caught sneaking into the hangars on Tuesday night, Krell might be the one who catches them. Instead of turning them in, he might "accidentally" leave his **Settika Sweep Kit** (containing Prism Water) on a bench while he goes for a smoke.
- **The Favor:** Krell needs a specific **Resonance Ledger** from [[dean-isolde-vane]]'s private study. He can't get it, but a "curious student" might. In exchange, he'll leave the service hatch to Hangar 7 unlocked.
- **The Warning:** If the PCs get too close to the Iron Union's operations in Pit-Row, Krell might pull them aside for a "friendly" warning to back off.

## Relationships
- **[[hollow-jack]]**: Krell's handler. Jack holds the debt; Krell provides the access.
- **[[lucky]]**: Krell finds Lucky's small-time hustles annoying but mostly ignores him as long as he stays away from the rigs.
