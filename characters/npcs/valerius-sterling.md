## Overview
Valerius Sterling is the older cousin of [[Valentine Sterling]] and a previous graduate of Vumbua Academy who never left. He hosts the popular (and possibly unauthorized) radio show that recaps academy events, maintaining his grip on the school's social narrative. He is named after his famous uncle, [[valentine-sterling-sr|Valentine Sterling Sr.]].

## Relationships
- **[[Valentine Sterling]]**: His younger cousin and the family Scion/Student. Valerius watches over him (and critiques his performance on air).
- **[[valentine-sterling-sr|Valentine Sterling Sr.]]**: His famous uncle and namesake.

## Personality
- **Public Persona:** Arrogant, perfect, and critical of "Variance" (chaos/unpredictability). He often antagonizes the party, calling them "Uncalibrated."
