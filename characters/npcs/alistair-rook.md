## Overview
Alistair is a Second-Year cadet who hails from the lower-tier industrial sectors of Harmony. Unlike the "Gilded" scions like [[Valentine Sterling]], Alistair's family has spent generations working the boiler rooms and shipyards. He earned his nickname "The Rook" for his stubborn, unmoving defensive style and his tendency to "castle" his rig during high-stress maneuvers.

## Personality
- **Stubborn:** Once Alistair picks a line, he does not deviate.
- **Pragmatic:** Views racing as a mechanical puzzle to be solved with torque and momentum, not "flair."
- **Rivalry:** Has a deep-seated disdain for the Sterling family's "Panda Line" flashiness. He believes raw Iron-Union steel will always win out over experimental Scrivener math.

## The Resonance Race
Alistair pilots **The Ironclad** (Team 1). His strategy is simple: strike the first Major Spire, dump 100% of the energy into forward thrust, and act as a mobile roadblock for anyone trying to pass.

## Relationships
- **[[Valentine Sterling]]**: Social and technical rival. Alistair takes great pleasure in "accidentally" bumping Valentine's rig during training heats.
- **[[Iron-Jaw Jax]]**: Respects the Warforged's strength but thinks Jax is too reckless with his maneuvers.
- **Squad 06**: His crew, composed of fellow industrial-track sophomores who treat the race like a 9-to-5 shift.

## Mechanics
- **Signature Move:** "The Rook's Gambit" - Forcing a collision that damages both rigs, knowing his Ironclad is built to survive it while the opponent's isn't.
