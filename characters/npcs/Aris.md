## Overview
Dr. Aris is a professor of Resonance Biology at Vumbua Academy. She is a dedicated preservationist who teaches frontier survival, resource extraction, and ethical interaction with alien environments. She speaks in rapid-fire medical jargon and often advocates for protecting fragile ecosystems (like the Void-Lily) from corporate strip-mining.

## Key Teachings & Events
- **Siren Song Effect:** Co-taught a seminar with Senior Exploranaut [[hesperus|Hesperus]] explaining how the biological pulse of the Light-trail ribbons in the Mist (emitting a Harmonic Pulse at exactly 60 BPM) induces drowsiness and fake gut instincts in pilots.
- **Preservation Philosophy:** Believes that the Void-Lily isn't just a resource but an anchor for the local ecosystem. She advises students that the "correct" survival option is often to falsify reports to protect the site.
