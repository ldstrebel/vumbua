## Overview
Warden Rovaldi (who introduced himself in-game as **Captain Raldi**) is the Chief of the Guards at Vumbua Academy. He is responsible for security around the hangar bays and the Apex Ring, enforcing curfews and managing access control. He wears a dark red (maroon) turban instead of the standard blue worn by the Indigo Turbans, and his right eye is entirely mechanical, rotating and whirring as it scans different spectrums.

## Session Appearances

### Session 6
- **Hangar Infiltration:** Confronted [[Iggy]] after Iggy slipped past the security line in Hangar 12. Rovaldi caught Iggy using his mechanical scanning eye.
- **The Conversation:** Vaulted 40 feet down from the catwalk to speak with Iggy on his level. He showed curiosity about Iggy's clockwork eye and offered him a choice to either leave or discuss the crafts in his office. After realizing Iggy was just curious and didn't know about the race, he escorted him out and suggested he talk to [[Rill]] instead.

## GM Secrets

> [!WARNING]- GM Secrets
> - **Locked replacement node:** Warden Rovaldi is holding the replacement resonance node (the Harmony-compatible core) for Valerius's backed team (Shatter Stamper) locked in Hangar Bay 4. He confiscated it due to a late-spec submission violation. He told an enforcer: *"Valerius's team won't find their replacement node. The Warden has it locked in Bay 4."* This was overheard by [[Britt]] while tailing the guard.
> - **Iggy's Dissonance & Surveillance:** During their confrontation in Hangar 12, Rovaldi's mechanical eye scanned Iggy and detected a different frequency—a jarring dissonance that suggested Iggy is not as "imbued" as a normal Harmony citizen. Intrigued and unsettled, Rovaldi has tasked his mechanical pet owl to shadow Iggy. Iggy will have to roll perception checks going forward to spot this surveillance, which reports back to the Warden.
> - **Political Conflict:** While Rovaldi is fundamentally a decent man, he is not immune to political intrigue or bribery. As he gathers more intelligence on Iggy's anomaly, he is torn on whether to report this finding to Dean Vane or directly to Valentine's father, [[valentine-sterling-sr|Valentine Sterling Sr.]], which could provide a more direct channel to investigate Iggy's origins.
