## Overview
Jack is a Warforged whose chassis is literal "hollow" in several places, replaced by reinforced cargo compartments. He operates out of the **Container-Yard** in Pit-Row. He doesn't care about the race; he cares about the flow of high-value mechanical assets.

## Personality
- **Transactional:** Everything has a price. He doesn't do favors unless they lead to bigger profits.
- **Calm:** Even with Turbaned Guards at the gate, Jack remains unbothered. He knows he's too useful to the Syndicate to be shut down.
- **Hoarder:** His containers are filled with "junk" that turns out to be rare 1st-generation resonance dampeners.

## Hooks for PCs
- **The Supplier:** The PCs need **Settika Prism Water** or a **"Prism-Doped" battery**. Jack has a hidden stash of "Inspection Grade" water, but he wants the PCs to "test" a new resonance-tracking device during the race.
- **The Information Broker:** Jack knows exactly which teams are using illegal modifications. He can sell this info to the PCs (or their rivals).
- **The Debt Collector:** Jack is looking for **[[lucky]]**, who owes him for a shipment of Umbra-Crystals. He might pay the PCs to "bring Lucky in for a chat."

## Relationships
- **[[vex]]**: The only person Jack trusts to handle high-end gear.
- **[[iron-jaw-jax]]**: Distant cousin (as far as Warforged go). Jack finds Jax's academic pursuit amusing but a waste of a good chassis.
