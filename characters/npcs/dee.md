## Overview
Dee is an Arena Logistics Officer for Vumbua Academy responsible for the acquisition, tracking, and placement of industrial scrap and equipment across the Apex Ring. Known to unwind at high-end venues like the bar on the airship *Zephyr*, her casual venting over strong drinks inadvertently provided [[Loami]] with the critical intelligence regarding the pre-seeded arena sectors prior to the first-year Proving Run.

## Appearance & Personality
- **Physical Features:** Caucasian female in her early 30s with fair skin, sharp brown eyes, and short dark styled hair styled in a chic side-swept bob.
- **Outfit:** Wears an elegant, form-fitting black silk dress with silver trim suitable for high-end VIP lounges, often carrying a rolled parchment manifest tucked under her arm or laid out on the bar.
- **Vibe:** Highly vocal, expressive, and passionate when venting about unrealistic arena logistics deadlines; loves strong grog and blue-collar bar banter.


## Session Appearances

### Session 9
In a flashback on the VIP deck of the *Zephyr* prior to the Reso Race, a heavily inebriated Dee complained to [[Loami]] behind the bar about the "preposterous" task of resetting the arena ring within 24 hours. She laid out a manifest detailing five specific sectors of the Apex Ring tin that had been ordered to be seeded with scrap materials, speculating that the Academy was preparing a physical test for the incoming student squads.
