## Part 1: Post-Zephyr Courtyard — Britt's Sickness

*Thursday night. The Zephyr has landed, and the fairgrounds are winding down. Student candidates are filtering back to their dorm blocks. Aggie and Britt stand in the courtyard, catching their breath.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Aggie, where were you? I was looking everywhere for you.

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** I was hanging out with Pip and Bramble. Studying and exchanging notes.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** What did you see? Who did you talk to? That tonight was a whirlwind.

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** Mostly studying. Where have you been?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Well, I tried to find [[Loami]]. I thought I saw him and I waved him over, but then somebody put this tiny human in my arms and then there were people cheering and then I lost him and I don't really know.

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** The human child. Should we be concerned?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** I don't know. But now... I just noticed in the mirror that I don't know. I look strange. I look not like myself. Have you noticed that?

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** Angela and I were actually just talking about that earlier. They kind of look like dry spots on your shell. Are you feeling okay?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** I don't know. I can't tell. I think so, but something's obviously wrong. And this has never happened to me before. Have you seen anything like this before?

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** I think I've seen it once maybe. I mean, it was like ten years ago. I don't remember it really well. But I don't remember him getting better. I don't remember anyone really knowing what was going on, but he might have been from a different clan.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Do you think it was the human child? Do you think it was some sort of contagious thing?

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** I think they saw it before you handled the human child.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** What do we do?

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** Do you want to go maybe see the infirmary here and see if they know anything? And if not, maybe we go talk to Great-Aunt Angela and see if she knows of anything. She's been around a lot as traveling.

*GM Narration:* Aggie notes that it would be a long walk to the forest and being back before the exam in the morning would be a stretch.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** I think I'm also worried about the exam and staying up too late. So maybe we go and rest. Maybe I'll feel better in the morning. Maybe if I just sleep some I'll be better. And then if I'm not, we can go to the infirmary when it's open in the morning.

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** Would you want me to come with you in the morning if you want to go?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Yes, please.

*GM Narration:* As they look back, preparing to walk away, they notice the slumbering snores of Iggy behind a barrel of grease in the open storage bay of the Zephyr. He is passed out, wearing Loami's oversized apron that says "Loami" on it.

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** Should we worry about him? *[Points at Iggy.]*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** I think Loami is around here somewhere.

## Part 2: Friday Morning — The Infirmary

*Friday morning. The Vumbua Academy infirmary is open. Iggy lies on a hospital cot, letting out faint, miserable moans from alcohol poisoning. Loami stands over him, wafting a cup of coffee towards him.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Hey, listen. I know it doesn't sound good right now, but it's just got a tiny little bit of the grease in it.

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** Help me.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** You got after it a little bit last night. I get it. The resi race, it's exciting. You can get caught up in the moment. I hear you. I'm glad you at least got some shut-eye when we were up there.

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** I thought it was just hydration and it did this.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Uh yeah, not quite hydration, I'd say. Potentially dehydration. Um, actually, your goggles are looking a bit dry. We'll have to figure that out. We'll get you checked out.

*GM Narration:* A halfling nurse wearing a white apron and a slanted red hat, marches over to the cot.

**[[Remmy]]:** Iggy, you have to drink. Not that though. *[Points at the cup.]* Get that away from him. He needs water. We performed watergation ten times tonight while Iggy was sleeping. Well, you do need someone to sit with you. So, and it's not going to be me. So yeah, why don't you just, you know, do what I told you and fill it up with water this time.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** All right, fine. Let me drink it.

*GM Narration:* The door twinkles as Britt and Aggie walk into the infirmary ward. Remmy turns to greet them.

**[[Remmy]]:** How can I help you? Are you feeling okay? Do you need help?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** I don't know what's happening, but these keep appearing and I don't know why. *[Points to the dry, discolored spots on her neck and shell.]*

**[[Remmy]]:** Well, why don't we take you back over here and I'll check you out. Have a seat here. Your friend can stay outside or she can come in with you.

*GM Narration:* Remmy leads Britt and Aggie behind a partitioned curtain. Meanwhile, Loami returns to Iggy's cot with a cup.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Here's your water. Here's your water. And it's actually water. I told you it was last time, but this one is actually water.

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *[Slaps the cup away. And raspily challenges Loami]* I don't believe you.

*GM Narration:* Back behind the partition, Remmy prepares to examine Britt. She pulls out a small vial of clear liquid and a spatula-like applicator.

**[[Remmy]]:** Okay, well, do you mind if I take a couple of readings?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Sure.

*GM Narration:* Remmy gently brushes the liquid onto Britt's neck. As it touches the spots, the clear fluid reacts immediately, turning a vibrant green that draws stark outlines around the affected areas.

**[[Remmy]]:** Oh, oh dear. Yeah, you definitely have something that you're... that's not connecting with you. So, I guess have you gotten into anything weird lately? Have you eaten something weird or gone to a new place?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Well, I'm in a new place and everything I've eaten is weird.

**[[Remmy]]:** Yeah, that's true. Of course, very few people are from here. This to me feels like something that would have happened within a day or two of exposure, and you've been here for almost a week now. So, what about in the last couple days? Anything?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** *[Speaking telepathically to Aggie]* I also think it was that. I saw some weird green sludge stuff near the northeast corner of the city when I dropped my book. I was close to it. Should I say something about that? Maybe I'll just be vague.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** *[Turning to Remmy]* I was studying and I dropped my book.

**[[Remmy]]:** Well, let me just stop you there. This sounds like dodging if I have ever heard it. You're not gonna get in trouble if you tell me. And to be honest, I don't know that I will be able to solve it because there's a lot of crazy things in the world. But I'll tell you that if you are thinking that there might be something that you came into contact with, you should probably tell someone where it is and bring it here or to someone who can study it because the way I look at this, it looks like something almost splattered on you. And it is reacting quite poorly. It doesn't seem like it's going to get any better anytime soon, and I don't have anything here that can treat you not knowing what you got into.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** It was by the northeast corner of the city overlooking the river. And it was green.

**[[Remmy]]:** Okay, well, we will have someone pick this up and by the time you're done with your test today, we'll probably have a sample here and have some more information for you. So come back after your exam, and good luck for that, by the way. Just know that you were a student here when this happened, so we will take care of you regardless of the results of the exam. 22,000 will go home today, they say, and I hope that's not one of you. Try not to let this get into your head too much.

*GM Narration:* Remmy pulls back the curtain to reveal that Iggy has slipped out of his cot and vanished from the ward, leaving only Loami behind.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Oh, he's just being dramatic.

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** Did you see him leave?

**[[Loami]] (PC):** No, not really. He went to get his own water and syringe to refill his goggles.

*GM Narration:* Loami walks over to Aggie and Britt, pulling back the curtain fully.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Ah, I thought it would be you two.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Yeah, there aren't other Mizizis around here.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** I missed you on the Zephyr. Were you all there?

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Yeah, we were there. I thought I saw you. I tried to wave to you, but then there was this baby.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Ah, well, yeah, they did kind of want you there as a bit of political goodwill, I think, unfortunately. But I got a shift as a bartender and was really enjoying myself, I might add. Did some bar tricks, was able to sell the engine grease. Well, "sell" is relative, but people seemed to enjoy it, so maybe next time we'll be able to sell it.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Is that what happened to Iggy?

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Yeah, I... Lucky made these cool backpack dispensers that had a tap on the side and we could wear the product and sell it. I looked back when we were in the engine room looking at the crystals and stuff and they were latched on to the spot pretty good and just doing a good old-fashioned kegstand.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Well, they don't seem too good this morning.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Yeah, hey, good enough to get themselves out the door, I guess. And they didn't drink the hair of the dog. That would have absolutely helped. It helps me every time.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Hair. You drink hair.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** It's an expression, you know, the hair of the dog that bit you. You basically drink a little bit of what you got drunk on the night before and it helps level you out a little bit.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Oh, we say eat a little bit of the bark of the tree you climbed.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Yeah, exactly the same concept. That makes a lot of sense. Genuinely, yesterday was one of the best days I've had here. Got to see the race, talk to people about the product. I think Lucky and I got a hit on our hands.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Did you make money? You said "sold."

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Yes, in tips. But we were hoping to sell it by the barrel, but they assumed it was a free sample for the Zephyr. But they might get a taste for it and want a barrel or two for the next race. It's the long game.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Well, I'm glad you got to see the race. I knew you were excited about that.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** Yeah. I hope we can build something and get out there.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** I hope so too.

## Part 3: Pre-Exam Scramble — The Harbor

*Friday, late morning. Two and a half hours remain until the exams. Large campus posters direct all candidates to report to the Apex Arena via the Colonnade entrance. Loami splits off to search for Lucky, but after an hour of hunting in the crowded dining halls and corridors, he loses the trail.*

*Meanwhile, Iggy, feeling dehydrated and shriveled like a raisin, staggers across the campus lawn heading toward the harbor. Britt spots his dragging trench coat and jogs to catch up.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Iggy, where are you going? There's an exam today! We only have like an hour until we have to start this exam.

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** Need water. Water. Need water.

*GM Narration:* Iggy reaches the edge of the harbor dock. The deep harbor basin descends roughly 1,700 feet into Vumbua's aquatic infrastructure. Iggy takes a step off the dock and plummets like a stone, cannonballing into the deep water to seek the hydrostatic pressure his clay body needs to recover.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** *[Shouting after him]* Okay, well, we have to be quick!

*GM Narration:* Britt stands on the shore, watching the water. One minute passes. Two minutes. Five minutes. The surface remains completely flat—no bubbles, no ripples, no sign of Iggy. Britt begins wading out, floating on the surface to look down, but the depth is too dark. She returns to the shore, scoops up a handful of pebbles, and begins dropping them into the water one by one.

*Time crawls forward. It is now 30 minutes before the exam starts, and a steady flow of candidates is streaming toward the Apex Arena.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** *[Telepathically to Aggie]* I'm at the harbor. Iggy's here, I think. But I'm going to leave soon if I don't see them surface.

*GM Narration:* Desperate to get Iggy's attention, Britt searches the shoreline, lifts a large rock, and hurls it directly into the water.

*Deep below, curled at the bottom of the basin, Iggy has been collecting every single pebble Britt dropped. The massive rock plunges down through the water column. With a quick reaction, Iggy catches the boulder, cradling it in his arms along with the handful of pebbles. He kicks off the bottom and surfaces next to the dock, covered from head to toe in seaweed and harbor debris, but completely restored and bright-eyed.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** Here's your rocks back.

**[[Britt]] (PC):** Okay. Well, I see you got my messages. It's time to go.

*GM Narration:* Aggie joins them at the harbor edge, carrying a collection of apples and food she grabbed from the dining hall to share.

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** Let's go.

## Part 4: The Apex Ring — The Exam

*Friday midday. The massive grandstands of the Apex Arena have been converted into an exam hall. Desks are erected on every other row, wrapping around the half-mile-wide basalt canyon. 77,000 student candidates scramble to find their seats. Enforcers and proctors patrol the rows at a tight 1-to-3 ratio. Britt, Aggie, and Iggy rush in just before the gate closes, locating their seats near Loami.*

*As they settle in, Britt attempts to speak telepathically to Aggie, but the message comes through garbled and suppressed. The arena is blanketed in a magic-damping ward to prevent cheating.*

*Loami sits near the edge of the ring, looking down at the race floor below. He notices that walker-core crews are actively preparing the terrain, but strangely, suspicious debris remains scattered in sectors that should have been completely cleared.*

*Before he can study it further, a turbine enforcer steps in front of each desk, placing a copper-lined slate and a glowing crystal paperweight in front of them.*

*Dean Isolde Vane stands at the center dais, her voice echoing across the stadium.*

**[[Dean Isolde Vane]]:** Hello students. No words, just tests. Begin.

*GM Narration:* The paperweights flare, and the written questions of the Exploration 101 General Education Exam write themselves across the slates. The candidates work in absolute silence for two hours.

*Once the final bell rings, the crystal paperweights flare again, and the slates begin to glow with their grading results. Across the stadium, the slates change color. Gold and Silver lights illuminate successful students, while a dark, rusty crimson red indicates failure.*

*Bjorn, sitting near Loami, looks down at his slate. It glows a dark, rusty crimson.*

**[[Bjorn]]:** It was fun while it lasted. *[Shrugs and stands up.]*

*GM Narration:* Aggie's slate glows Gold. Britt's slate glows Gold. Iggy's slate glows Silver. Loami's slate glows Silver. Roughly 25% of the stadium's slates glow crimson rust, representing 22,000 eliminated candidates. Whimpers and groans ripple through the stands.

**[[Dean Isolde Vane]]:** Well, that's your exams. If you didn't get the result you're looking for, especially one that means you have to leave, we ask that you please do so with as minimum violence as possible. We will retribute very quickly. We don't want to, though.

*GM Narration:* A disturbance erupts at the front of the hall. Lyra Castellan stands at her desk, screaming at the proctors.*

**[[Lyra Castellan]]:** No! Get your hands off of me! Get!

*GM Narration:* Her brothers step in, shouting at the guards.

**Castellan Brother:** Don't you touch her! Don't!

*GM Narration:* Security guards immediately seize the siblings by their arms and forcibly march them out of the stadium.

**[[Dean Isolde Vane]]:** With that said, you are now all officially in the first year of Vimilia. Congratulations. However, that is just the start, because as you know, you have one week to gain the captain savers. And if you don't, well, you might still be in the class, but you won't be exploring. And what's the fun in that?

*GM Narration:* Dean Vance snaps her fingers, and their slates update to display three digital resources: a Scatters List of the arena floor, a Captain's Dossier, and Professor Kante's Loom Analysis.

**[[Dean Isolde Vane]]:** I have three things for you. One is a list of things you will find on the arena floor. Two is a list of captains and what they're looking for. And three is an analysis by our great Professor Kante of this year's Loom results. So you have the rest of today. Join your squad.

*GM Narration:* The slates refresh to show their official team assignment: **Squad 907**, containing Aggie, Britt, Loami, Ignatius, and Iggy.

**[[Dean Isolde Vane]]:** Find your squad and do something incredible, I guess. Don't waste this opportunity. Welcome to school.

*GM Narration:* Mechanical lifts rise from the lip of the arena. Students immediately scramble onto them, pressing buttons to descend to the canyon floor below for the Freshman Run.
