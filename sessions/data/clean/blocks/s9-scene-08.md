Loami leaned over the counter, his sharp blue-collar gaze scanning the sector grid coordinates inked on Dee's manifest. "Dee... do you know why they need all this heavy hardware seeded in the sand?" <!-- L0800 --> <!-- L0820 -->

"I don't know," Dee mumbled, resting her cheek against the cool mahogany. "The Dean never explains logistics to the supply officers. But they always sort candidates into squads right after the written exam on Friday morning. It stands to reason they're putting them to some kind of field test." <!-- L0880 -->

The words hit Loami like an electric spark jumping between copper contacts. The rumor among harbor mechanics and furnace-tenders had always been that Vumbua squad assignments weren't random bureaucratic placements—they were calculated from the natural harmonic frequencies and working bonds forged between candidates during their very first week of classes. <!-- L0890 --> <!-- L0900 -->

Loami looked down at the five sector coordinates on the parchment. In his mind's eye, the crew snapped into sharp, unyielding focus: the blazing hearth-fire and reckless aerial courage of Ignatius; the sturdy root-kin resilience and fierce loyalty of Britt; the secretive subterranean instincts of little Iggy; and the quiet, observant mycelial wisdom of Aggie. Squad 907 was already built before the first exam slate was handed out. <!-- L0910 -->
