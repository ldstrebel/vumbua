## CHAPTER 17: THE THERMAL SURGE & THE CELESTIAL LOUNGE

The night wind whipped off the bay, carrying salt spray and the bitter tang of cooling ash across the courtyard. Ignatius stared into the glowing embers of the Block 99 pit, his thoughts turning over the mystery of his homeland. <!-- L1071 -->

"Our elders warned us of the Great Cooling," Ignatius said quietly. "For fifty years, the magma chambers grew sluggish. We thought the flame of the world was dying. But the moment Harmony's surveyors arrived, the stones flared back to life. It's not a coincidence. Either Harmony is doing something to our land, or the world itself is reacting to the broken isolation." <!-- L1073 -->

Lomi leaned forward, nodding grimly. "Down in the boiler decks, we track the numbers. Two years ago, when the first shipments of Lucidian stone arrived, they measured a baseline thermal output. Today? That same mass of stone produces eighty percent more heat per gram." <!-- L1094 -->

Britt frowned. "Eighty percent? Why?" <!-- L1096 -->

"Nobody knows," Lomi said. "Municipal boilers across the capital have been rupturing because the stone burns hotter than the brass fittings were engineered to withstand. People started stockpiling it, creating a black market of secondary traders selling off surplus. It burns clean, but it's volatile—and the further it travels from the Ember Isles, the stranger its decay curve becomes. The heat doesn't bleed away steadily; it dips, holds, and surges in waves." <!-- L1099 --> <!-- L1101 --> <!-- L1104 -->

"It has a pulse," Ignatius whispered, clenching his fist around the basalt in his pocket. "It's alive." <!-- L1108 -->

Across the quad, the bonfire was dying to a bed of dull crimson coals. The crowds of candidates had begun to thin, drifting toward their assigned dorm blocks or clutching their printed intake slates. A few aristocratic youths strolled by, casually displaying their gold-stamped receipts like badges of nobility. <!-- L1122 --> <!-- L1128 --> <!-- L1132 -->

Ignatius stretched his arms over his head, popping his knuckles. "Look at those well-dressed candidates heading toward the western bluff. What do you say we take a walk before turning in? I'm not ready to stare at a bunk ceiling yet." <!-- L1133 --> <!-- L1135 -->

"It's pretty late," Aggie pointed out, stifling a small yawn behind her hand. <!-- L1136 -->

"We already had prime time here at the bonfire," Ignatius grinned. "Now I'm just curious how the other half lives." <!-- L1139 --> <!-- L1143 -->

Lomi pushed himself up from his crate, brushing dust from his trousers. "When I was crossing the bridges earlier, I overheard some upperclassmen whispering about the Celestial Lounge. An exclusive rooftop club sitting on top of the grand manors along the western cliff." <!-- L1150 --> <!-- L1152 -->

Britt looked up at the pale crescent moon hanging above the spires. A small, reckless smile touched her lips. "I think we've got one more party in us. Lead the way." <!-- L1155 --> <!-- L1163 --> <!-- L1165 -->

They set off together, leaving the dying coals of Block 99 behind. The path led them across high arched stone bridges suspended over deep harbor chasms, where the titanic hull of the Deep-Hull lay moored in dark turquoise water below. Moving northwest past the administrative colonnades, the rough timber scaffolds gave way to polished flagstones, gas-lit wrought iron lampposts, and manicured terrace lawns. <!-- L1170 --> <!-- L1174 -->

Beyond the perimeter wall, the atmosphere shifted entirely. Three palatial manors rose against the cliffside like gilded monuments. In the courtyards below, liveried attendants and heavy-set bouncers guarded wrought-iron gates, checking golden signet pins before ushering guests inside. High above the manors, perched on the highest glass-domed pavilion overlooking the entire harbor, a glowing sign radiated electric violet light into the midnight fog: **THE CELESTIAL LOUNGE**. <!-- L1176 --> <!-- L1178 -->

Ignatius looked up at the neon beacon, embers shimmering in his eyes. "Shall we see what kind of tea they serve at the top of the world?" <!-- L1179 --> <!-- L1181 -->

Lomi grinned, pulling his flat cap low. "Let's find out." <!-- L1182 --> <!-- L1184 -->
