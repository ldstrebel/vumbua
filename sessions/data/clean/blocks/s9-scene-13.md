## CHAPTER 85: AIR SHOPPING

The instant the steam-lift struck the arena floor, Ignatius ignited the embers at his boots. Tiny, flickering flame-wings unfurled from his ankles, lifting him twenty feet into the air. He caught Britt under her arms in an improvised harness, grinning down at the chaos on the canyon floor.

"We're taking the express route!" Ignatius yelled over the rush of wind. "We're air shopping, baby!" <!-- L1510 -->

They banked hard into Sector 2, where shifting directional gravity zones pulled the desert sands into swirling forty-foot vertical waves. Ignatius skimmed low beneath overhead salvage crane booms, dodging jagged basalt needles as gravity flipped laterally. Britt leaned out from the harness with her hunting hook, snatching bundles of hollow bamboo, four heavy tar-sealed fuel bladders, forty feet of braided high-pressure coolant hose, and a sponge boom block from an abandoned supply depot. <!-- L1520 --> <!-- L1540 --> <!-- L1550 -->
