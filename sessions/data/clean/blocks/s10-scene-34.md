## CHAPTER 94: THE DAGGER SHARKS

Eight o'clock in the morning arrived with a deafening siren that shook the basalt canyon walls. Across all five sectors, the colossal Walker-Core suppression pylons released with an ear-splitting crack, discharging sheets of violet static lightning down their iron legs as the barrier dissolved.
