A thunderous, earth-shaking thud shattered his relaxation. Stomping through the sulfur vapor was an eighteen-foot Cyclops Troll—Sam—carrying a massive wicker basket heaped with glowing raw sulfur energy crystals.

Iggy's eyes widened behind his misty lenses. Those crystals were exactly what Loami needed for the raft's thermal burner. Scrambling up the troll's back Santa-sack style, Iggy tapped the monster's colossal shoulder: "Hey! Big guy! Size ain't everything with crystals!"

"I got the best crystals!" Sam roared, swinging a massive hand. Iggy leaned forward and bit the troll squarely on the thumb! Sam howled in shock, dropping the basket. Falling eight feet to the mud, Iggy lashed out with *Mud Lash*, wrapping a hardened clay tendril around the largest glowing crystal, yanking it into his arms, and rolling into a compact bowling ball to bounce away across the delta flats while Sam's companion, Sarah, screamed in fury: "Sam! He bit you!"
