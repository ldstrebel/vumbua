## CHAPTER 16: PINE TEA & BOILER FUEL

Serra Vox watched Rill and Zephyr vanish into the mist, her fingers tapping a nervous staccato against her notebook. "Rill has one of the keenest minds at the academy," Serra murmured. "She works directly alongside Dean Vane. The faculty has an insatiable curiosity regarding the unintegrated frontiers. Perhaps they believe understanding your people will help us understand ourselves." <!-- L0951 -->

"Or help themselves to our land," Britt said quietly. <!-- L0952 --> <!-- L0954 --> <!-- L0956 -->

Serra shrank slightly under Britt's flinty stare, hastily reaching for one of the unsealed jars of moonshine on the paving stones. She took a cautious sip, then passed the jar toward Aggie. "To... mutual survival?" <!-- L0957 -->

Aggie took the glass jar with both hands, inspecting the clear, pungent liquid. She looked to Britt for permission, received a small, fond nod, and brought the rim to her lips. She took a microscopic, exploratory sip, blinked at the fiery burn, and then took a slightly bolder draught. <!-- L0964 -->

"Well?" Britt asked. <!-- L0965 -->

Aggie handed the jar over, her throat working hard to swallow. "There are... a great many new things tonight." <!-- L0966 --> <!-- L0974 -->

Britt took the jar, tilted her head back, and knocked back a generous mouthful. Her eyes flared wide, her leathery green skin flushing a deep olive as she fought down the burn. "That is terrible," she wheezed, wiping her mouth. She took a second mouthful and spat it in a sharp, concentrated jet directly into the glowing coals. A sharp hiss and an orange fireball flared up in response. "Like swallowing liquid pitch."

Lomi strolled back into the circle, wiping a smudge of engine grease from his cheek. "I heard that," he said with an easy grin. "It's an acquired taste. Boilermaker's special. Name's Lomi Sultano, by the way. Deep-Hull boiler crew, third-generation union man." <!-- L0982 --> <!-- L0984 --> <!-- L0988 --> <!-- L0994 -->

"I'm Britt," she replied, shaking his calloused hand. "And this is my cousin, Aggie. In our forest, when we celebrate, we brew pine needle tea." <!-- L0999 -->

Lomi laughed, shaking his head. "To be completely honest with you, pine needle tea sounds awful to me. But hey—you tried my drink, so fair's fair." He pulled up a wooden crate and sat down, resting his elbows on his knees. "So what brings two root-kin folk all the way north to Vumbua?" <!-- L1000 --> <!-- L1002 --> <!-- L1004 -->

"You mentioned working the boilers," Britt countered, neatly deflecting. "What does that mean, exactly? Keeping the fires burning?" <!-- L1005 -->

"Keeping the entire campus alive," Lomi said, gesturing toward the cranes and the hulking ironclad in the bay. "The Deep-Hull provides the steam pressure for the cranes, the lighting grids, and the assembly workshops. Down in the belly, we manage the valves and pressure lines. If the furnaces burn too hot, or if a manifold blows, it's a catastrophe—all power ceases, and things start exploding." <!-- L1006 --> <!-- L1008 --> <!-- L1010 -->

"What do you feed the furnaces?" Britt asked. "Wood?" <!-- L1012 -->

"Not wood," Lomi chuckled. "Coal, sometimes, but mostly condensed crystal batteries from Harmony—and recently, the real revolution: Lucidian stone." <!-- L1014 --> <!-- L1024 --> <!-- L1034 --> <!-- L1047 -->

Ignatius ambled over, tossing his fist-sized chunk of basalt into the air and catching it. "Lavsidian," Ignatius corrected proudly. "Lava and obsidian. The finest export of the Ember Isles."

"It doesn't combust," Lomi explained to Britt, his tradesman's passion shining through. "You don't get soot, ash, or smoke choking the flue. You drop a slab of Lucidian into the water jacket, and it produces an unbroken, blistering thermal output for days on end. It's transformed our boiler efficiency by eighty percent." <!-- L1049 -->

Britt frowned, her gaze shifting from the dark stone in Ignatius's hand to the smoking bonfire. "Is there an endless supply of it? What happens when you dig out the last rock?" <!-- L1050 -->

Ignatius went quiet. The cocky swagger faded from his posture, replaced by a quiet, brooding intensity. "That's why I'm here," he said softly. <!-- L1052 --> <!-- L1058 --> <!-- L1061 -->

He looked down at the dark stone in his palm. "Our volcanoes have been suffering from a great cooling for generations. When Harmony arrived, the heat surged, but nobody knows why. I don't trust the mainland magistrates to keep our hearth warm. I came to Vumbua to find where the fire truly comes from—to find the heart of the world's heat, so my clan never has to beg for warmth again." <!-- L1063 --> <!-- L1065 -->
