## CHAPTER 21: TOMMY, LUCINA & THE CATWALK

The brass door swung closed with a soft pneumatic hiss, cutting off the distant crackle of the bonfire. The air in the vestibule was warm, scented with dry ozone, lamp oil, and the sweet, resinous tang of fresh solder. As Iggy approached the high mahogany desk, the crimson hat dipped forward. A small, sharply pointed face peered over the ledger ledge. <!-- L0526 --> <!-- L0530 -->

It was a gnome with huge, luminous green eyes and a delicate, neatly trimmed blonde beard framing a cheerful chin. He wore a starched white clerical collar beneath a fitted velvet waistcoat, a steel dip pen perched behind one elongated ear. <!-- L0533 --> <!-- L0534 -->

"Oh, hello!" the gnome piped, voice bright as a silver bell. "Who are you? Are you supposed to be here? Nine o'clock at night is rather irregular for visitors." <!-- L0535 --> <!-- L0536 -->

"Iggy," the earth-kin muttered, adjusting his round copper goggles. "Just looking. Nice to meet you." <!-- L0537 --> <!-- L0538 -->

"I am Tommy," the gnome replied politely, blinking at the moss sprouting from Iggy's brow. "Can I help you? Is there something you require? A requisition ledger? A component slip?" <!-- L0540 --> <!-- L0545 -->

"No. Just looking," Iggy repeated, already shuffling past the desk toward the double swinging doors that led into the main facility. "Nice to meet you, Tommy. I'm going to keep walking now." <!-- L0549 --> <!-- L0550 -->

"Well... it was nice to meet you too, I suppose," Tommy muttered to himself, scratching his ear with the blunt end of his pen as he watched the trench-coated figure trundle toward the inner sanctum. "Wait, are you a student? Should I have had you sign the guest register?" <!-- L0553 --> <!-- L0554 -->

Before Tommy could resolve the bureaucratic dilemma, a heavy side door clattered open. A dwarven woman with broad shoulders, soot-smudged cheeks, and thick braided auburn hair emerged into the lobby. She wore canvas mechanic's overalls stained with dark turbine grease, a massive crescent spanner tucked into her tool belt. <!-- L0557 --> <!-- L0565 -->

"Tommy?" she called out in a low, rumbling voice, wiping her brow with the back of a thick leather gauntlet. "Did you just let someone in? Are they lost?" She squinted through the dim light, watching Iggy's small silhouette push through the inner doors. "Wait. Is that a student? Are students allowed back in the turbine bays?" <!-- L0568 --> <!-- L0571 -->

"He said his name was Iggy," Tommy offered helpfully. "He seemed very decisive about walking." <!-- L0572 -->

Lucina shook her head, snorting a laugh through her nose. "Well, if he breaks a turbine, it's on his head. I'm not chasing him down the catwalk." <!-- L0575 --> <!-- L0580 -->

Beyond the swinging doors, the building expanded into a cavernous, cathedral-like industrial hall. Iggy found himself standing on an elevated steel-mesh catwalk that hugged the perimeter of the upper level. Thirty feet below, across the polished stone floor of the warehouse, colossal brass turbines hummed with steady, rhythmic power. Huge cast-iron drive shafts connected the spinning rotors to intricate gear trains that vanished through the eastern masonry toward the rest of the campus. <!-- L0581 --> <!-- L0583 -->

What caught Iggy's breath, however, was the power source. Plugged into heavy bronze terminal blocks along the base of each turbine were glowing, translucent blue rectangular units roughly the size of steamer trunks. Inside each unit, an intricate geometric lattice of hexes and interlocking crystalline triangles pulsed with soft, rhythmic sapphire light. Thin copper antennae protruded from the upper corners, vibrating with a high-pitched frequency that resonated directly in the soles of Iggy's boots. <!-- L0595 --> <!-- L0601 -->

Further along the catwalk, a bright pool of light spilled from an open supervisor's office. Inside, mounted across the entire western wall, was a massive slate blackboard covered in white and colored chalk. To the left was an exquisite, exploded schematic of the blue power units, labeled in crisp technical cursive: *PANDA-5 RESONATOR BATTERY*. To the right, a sprawling coordinate graph traced a jagged blue line that rose steadily across decades before plateauing and plunging sharply downward toward a dashed red threshold marked with violent red question marks. <!-- L0606 --> <!-- L0620 --> <!-- L0635 --> <!-- L0650 --> <!-- L0658 -->

Iggy's fingers twitched. Reaching into the lining of his coat, he pulled out his grease-stained notebook and a stub of charcoal, his eyes darting between the turbines below and the equations on the wall.
