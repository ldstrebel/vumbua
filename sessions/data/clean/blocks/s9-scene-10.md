Aggie darted through the crowd toward Iggy's seat, handing him the coordinates before vanishing down the stairwell toward the Sector 3 wetlands. Meanwhile, near the upper grandstand exit, Loami spotted a familiar silhouette among the temporary security enforcers—a burly firefighter and former UN maintenance colleague from his old neighborhood. <!-- L1065 --> <!-- L1075 -->

"Hey! Raphael!" Loami called out, leaning over the stone barricade. "You're going to see a Mizizi girl and a little stone-looking guy with round goggles together down on the floor. Give this slip to them." <!-- L1090 --> <!-- L1110 -->

The firefighter turned, eyeing the folded note, then looked back at Loami with a wide, knowing smirk. "Loami? You know your Academy coin's no good to me, brother... there's only one thing I'm trading for on duty." <!-- L1130 --> <!-- L1150 -->

Loami grinned, reached into his heavy canvas duster, and pulled out a stoneware flask of his custom, furnace-distilled amber brew along with a fistful of bronze tips. "Freshly bottled from the Zephyr bar. Every drop of tip money I made." <!-- L1170 -->

The firefighter's eyes lit up. He snatched the flask, stashed the note into his tunic, and raised a hand in a crisp salute. "Consider it done, chief!" Turning to his squad of guards, he bellowed: "All right, boys, section five is completely secure! Nothing to see here! Let's go find some Mizizi!" The entire guard detail promptly abandoned their perimeter post to hunt down the Mizizi candidates, leaving a gaping hole in security that Loami slipped through without a second glance. <!-- L1180 --> <!-- L1200 -->
