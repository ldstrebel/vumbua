## CHAPTER 22: THE OVERENGINEERED BATTERY & THE GLOBAL DECLINE

Stepping quietly into the office, Iggy pushed his copper goggles down over his eyes, leaning until his nose was inches from the chalk-dusted slate. His mind worked through the diagrams with the methodical, unyielding patience of subterranean roots seeking water. <!-- L0659 --> <!-- L0662 --> <!-- L0665 -->

The Panda-5 resonator was a marvel of defensive engineering. Looking at the filing cabinet of spare units lined up along the office wall, Iggy could see that every joint was reinforced with heavy copper dampeners, triple-insulated brass sleeves, and high-tolerance heat baffles. It was built not merely to store energy, but to contain a violent, volatile surge—like a blast furnace designed to withstand an explosion from within. <!-- L0674 --> <!-- L0679 --> <!-- L0702 -->

Yet its function was elegant. It was not an engine that burned coal or oil, nor was it a chemical cell. It was a receptor. It operated on the principle of an open vessel placed beneath a rushing waterfall: the energy was always present, constantly flowing, and the battery’s sole purpose was to capture that ambient surge and maintain a strictly regulated threshold. If the incoming power spiked too high, safety conduits safely bled off the excess into grounding rods; if the flow dipped, the internal matrix struggled to hold the line. Overbuilding the casing was a safeguard against catastrophe, ensuring the glass would never shatter even if the faucet was cranked to maximum pressure. <!-- L0703 --> <!-- L0707 --> <!-- L0712 --> <!-- L0713 --> <!-- L0717 -->

His gaze shifted to the sprawling graph on the right half of the board. The vertical axis was labeled *GLOBAL AMPLITUDE*—the total harmonic resonance generated across the civilized world. <!-- L0718 -->

For over two centuries, the line had climbed steadily, stair-stepping upward with every historical milestone. But thirty years ago, the line had flattened. And over the last decade, it had done something unprecedented in recorded history: it had begun to drop. It was sliding inexorably downward toward a heavy red line marked *MINIMUM OPERATIONAL EQUILIBRIUM*. Beneath that red line lay brownouts, dead communications, and failing airship navigation. <!-- L0725 --> <!-- L0730 --> <!-- L0736 -->

Near the very end of the curve, where the timeline reached the present year, a dashed line showed a projected spike that never occurred. Sketched in jagged, frantic red chalk above the failed trajectory were two large exclamation points, a question mark, and three words that made Iggy's heart stop: <!-- L0737 --> <!-- L0740 -->

*WHERE IS THE ASH-BLOOD SURGE?* <!-- L0745 --> <!-- L0748 --> <!-- L0750 --> <!-- L0755 --> <!-- L0760 -->
