## CHAPTER 91: THE SOULLESS FOREST

Along the Sector 3 shoreline, Aggie and Britt held the rendezvous point beneath the damp fog. Aggie gazed into the towering petrified canopy, her brow furrowed. "It looks like home," Aggie murmured softly, "but it's missing the soul. A master craftsman built this place knowing what trees look like, but knowing nothing of how it feels to live among them."
