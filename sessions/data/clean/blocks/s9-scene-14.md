## CHAPTER 86: THE STEAM VENT SPA

Meanwhile, in Sector 4—the volcanic delta—Iggy had found paradise. The basin floor was a labyrinth of bubbling mineral mud pools and roaring geothermal steam vents. Iggy waddled directly into a billowing sulfur fissure, letting the superheated volcanic vapor bake into his packed-clay pores. His round copper goggles fogged up completely as he let out a long, contented groan of subterranean bliss.

"Warm down here..." Iggy murmured, relaxing into the scalding mud. "Best thing Iggy's ever smelled in his whole life. Matrix consolidation ninety percent complete."
