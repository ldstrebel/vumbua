## CHAPTER 92: THE NATTY RENAMING

Midnight in Sector 3. The clearing looked like an open-air shipyard. Spread across the mossy shoreline were six iron-hooped buoyancy barrels, coils of steel cable, high-tension bungee cords, heavy canvas tarps, fuel bladders sealed with dark tar, hollow bamboo rods, and the glowing sulfur energy crystal Iggy had stolen.
