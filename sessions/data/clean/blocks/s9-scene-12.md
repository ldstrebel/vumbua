Halfway down the canyon wall, the lift entered a layer of heavy static ozone. The howling wind sheared against the rock face, and a neighboring lift bucked violently on its steel guide cables. A tiny pixie cadet lost her footing on the slick iron mesh, slipping beneath the safety guardrail and tumbling backward into the sheer open abyss! <!-- L1360 --> <!-- L1380 -->

"Look out!" Britt shouted without a heartbeat's hesitation. <!-- L1390 -->

Channeling her root-kin strength, she snagged the screaming pixie cadet by her collar mid-air with one hand, fired a braided vine grapple upward with the other to hook the lift suspension beam, and swung both of them back onto the grated deck in a single breathless arc. <!-- L1410 --> <!-- L1430 --> <!-- L1450 -->
