## CHAPTER 90: IGGY'S FIRST TREE

Sector 3 was where the Apex Ring stored its forest—a colossal grove of petrified giants, ninety-foot trunks of compressed mineral-wood rising into the hazy sky with root networks sunken deep into the bedrock. Iggy, who had spent his entire life in the stone trenches and manicured median strips of Bamboo, stood at the tree line and simply stopped moving.
