## CHAPTER 83: THE LOOM DROP

Friday morning at the Apex Ring amphitheater. The morning sun broke over the basalt canyon rim, illuminating fifty thousand cheering first-year candidates packed into the stone tiers. High on the central observation dais, Dean Isolde Vane stepped to the brass resonance horns, her grey cloak billowing in the updraft. Behind her, the canyon walls rumbled as colossal steam-lifts—massive iron platforms suspended from steel gantry cables—rattled into position along the cliff face. <!-- L0925 --> <!-- L0940 -->

"Cadets of Vumbua!" the Dean's voice boomed across the half-mile amphitheater, cold, resonant, and clear. "Your written exam slates are sealed. Your preliminary scores are recorded. Your first year begins now! Behind me lies the arena basin. Down on that floor, your squads must prove your worth before the Senior Captains. Your voyage depends on what you build, what you discover, and how you endure!" <!-- L0960 --> <!-- L0980 -->

As the final bell tolled and the telepathic suppression wards dissolved across the grandstands, Aggie turned to Britt, her red-spotted mushroom cap tilting with concern as she reached out through their shared mycelial bond. <!-- L1000 --> <!-- L1020 -->

"Britt, are you feeling healthy and ready to proceed?" Aggie transmitted, her thoughts gentle and grounding. "There's no rush to return to the forest tonight... but I can speak to plants. If I make for Sector 3 where the wetlands and petrified roots lie, I can scout the terrain and locate the supply caches through the root network." <!-- L1040 --> <!-- L1050 -->

"Got it," Britt replied, adjusting the straps of her harness. "Deliver the rendezvous note to Iggy, then head toward Sector 3. We've each got our assignments. Let's move." <!-- L1055 --> <!-- L1060 -->
