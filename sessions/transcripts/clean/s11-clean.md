# Session 11: Don't Touch My Biscuits

## Session Delta (Session 11)

### Canonical Title
- **Session 11: Don't Touch My Biscuits**

### New / Updated NPCs & Bestiary Entities
- **[[Mwaza-Kasa]]** (`characters/npcs/mizizi-spirit-tortoise.md`) — First physical appearance in-session. The sacred Spirit Tortoise of the Mizizi clan, a tortoise that looks normal, except for its slightly larger than normal size, and bark-like shell. Accepted a biscuit from Pip, collapsed into a catatonic state to anchor Aggie and Britt into the deep memory network, then plummeted 50ft to the ground to lead the party.
- **[[Mwaza-Chui]]** (Canopy Apex Jaguars) — Bioluminescent where their patches dapple their dark black fur, these frequency-biting apex predators in the Mizizi canopy try to shock their prey with their static-charged roars that suppress spellcasting and mind-links.
- **[[Mewoders]]** (Tree Cats / Coyobi / "Yaba Daba") — Small, fast tree-cats native to the canopy boughs, somewhere between a coyote and a bobcat. Aggressive and territorial.

### Existing NPCs with New Appearances
- **[[Pip]]** — Squad 05 (Harvesters) member snatched by a Storm Raptor while clutching her bag of bacon biscuits. Rescued by Squad 907, bopped a jaguar with her snack sack, petted Mwaza-Kasa, and fell into a peaceful sleep after the network vision.
- **[[Bramble]]** — Squad 05 member who communicated with Aggie through the trees using *Speak to Plants* (just like Aggie does). Apologized for misleading her earlier with false plant signals, explaining he didn't realize it was Squad 907 until he saw the vision of Iggy filter through the tree ranks.

### New Locations
- **High Canopy Tree Fortress / Tempest Clearing** — High-altitude petrified boughs above the Mizizi canopy where Squad 06 constructed living timber ramparts and Squad 907 docked their Canopy Raft.

### New Terms & Lore
- **[[Mwaza-Chui]]** — Crystalline static apex jaguars with dappled bioluminescent fur.
- **The Sacred Memory Network (Grove Shared Memory)** — Aggie and Britt touched Mwaza-Kasa as an anchor to enter the deep fungal network, unlocking suppressed childhood memories of entering a sacred grove and recalling lost clan members who died of green sludge/rot.
- **Tree Igloo (`Wild Fortress`)** — Aggie's grown living barricade protecting Pip and Loami.
- **Fungal Bait & Raft Dislodgement** — Aggie, Britt, and Loami piled glowing mushrooms on the raft, coordinated a group luring check, and cut the bungees to tip the raft and jaguars into the canopy abyss.

### Player Knowledge Changes
- ✅ Squad 907 reached **Level 5**.
- ✅ Pip was successfully rescued from the Storm Raptor and jaguars.
- ✅ Bramble re-established communication with Aggie through the trees once Squad 907's presence was recognized.
- ✅ Touching Mwaza-Kasa is taboo to the Mizizi, and as the PCs learn it acts as a physical anchor in the absence of the trees themselves. Forces traversal into the deep Mizizi memory network.
- ✅ Aggie and Britt remembered entering a sacred grove as children and saw visions of past clan members lost to the network.
- ✅ Mwaza-Kasa survived the fall to the forest floor and nodded for Squad 907 to follow it on foot.
