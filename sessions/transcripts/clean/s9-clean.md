## Play-by-Play

### Scene 0: Flashback — Five Sectors in the Sand

*GM Narration: On the VIP deck of the airship Zephyr, hours before the Reso Race and the final written exam, the ambient hum of golden dirigible engines echoes through the polished wood and brass lounge. Loami stands behind the mahogany bar, wiping down glasses and pouring heavy rounds of grog.*

*Sitting at the bar is Dee, an Arena Logistics Officer dressed in an elegant black dress, looking thoroughly inebriated from the last round Loami poured. She slumps over her glass, waving her hands in frustration.*

**[[Dee]]:** "It's impossible! I tell you, there's no way that we can reset the ring in a day. It's preposterous! I mean, look at the carnage they're wreaking down there!"

*Loami looks on with keen interest, casually pouring another drink and sliding it across the polished bar surface.*

**[[Dee]]:** "I mean, they expect me to just find all these materials and stuff and set them out in the sand. I just don't understand why!"

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "What materials, Dee?"

*Dee pulls a rolled parchment manifest from her coat and lays it out across the bar. It lists five distinct sectors of the Apex Ring tin, marked with specific instructions to seed them with heavy scrap materials, canvas, bungees, and industrial fittings.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "Dee, know why they need these materials?"

**[[Dee]]:** "I don't know... but they always make squads after the first year, so it makes sense that they're gonna put them to some test or something."

*GM Narration: The words hit Loami like a sudden spark. That is indeed how Vumbua operates every year. Candidates are sorted into squads following the written exam, and the long-standing rumor among blue-collar mechanics is that these squads are formed by the natural harmonic connections generated between candidates over their first week of classes.*

*Loami pauses, looking at the five sector coordinates on the manifest. Behind the scenes, a subtle harmonic intuition takes hold. He thinks through the bonds formed over the week—the fiery energy of Ignatious, the sturdy root-kin resilience of Britt, the secretive earthkin nature of Iggy, and the quiet fungal wisdom of Aggie. Loami accurately predicts the exact crew that will form Squad 907 long before the Loom punch cards fall.*

### Scene 1: The Loom Drop & Squad 907 Scramble

*GM Narration: Friday morning at the Apex Ring bleachers. Dean Isolde Vane stands atop the high podium overlooking 50,000 cheering first-year cadets. Behind her, the canyon walls rumble as window-washer style steam-lifts rise into position along the basalt cliffs.*

**[[Dean Isolde Vane]]:** "Cadets of Vumbua! Your written results are recorded. Your first year begins now. Behind me lies the Apex Ring. Down on that floor, your squads must prove your worth before the Senior Captains. Your voyage depends on what you build and what you discover."

*As the written exam finishes and the slate tablets turn silver, the telepathic suppression wards lift. Aggie turns to Britt to check on her health following her morning infirmary visit.*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *(Async telepathic check-in)* "Britt, are you feeling healthy and ready to proceed? There's no rush for us to head back to the forest tonight... if we don't get back for a week or more, it will be totally fine. But I can talk to plants! If I go to one of the zones that has a lot of plant life in it, I can quickly find out where things are."

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "Got it. Aggie, delivered the note to Iggy, then head toward Sector 3 to scout the plants. We've got things we need to do, and you've got things you need to do."

*Aggie nods and runs toward Iggy to deliver the note before heading off toward Sector 3 (Wetlands / Mycelium Maze).*

*Meanwhile, near the upper grandstands, Loami spots a familiar face among the temporary security guards—a fellow firefighter and former UN maintenance colleague from his hometown.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "Hey! You're gonna see Humazi and a little stone-looking guy together down there. Give this to them."

*GM Narration: Loami's old firefighter colleague looks at the note, then back at Loami.*

**[[Firefighter Guard]]:** "Loami? You know that your gold's no good for me, buddy... there's only one thing I want."

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "All right, fine. Here. I made this at the bar the other day. Here you go. It's literally every tip I got... but it's worth it."

*Loami hands over a bottle of his custom homemade bar brew along with a handful of tip coins.*

**[[Firefighter Guard]]:** "All right! You know, for you, I'll do it. But two Mizizi and a stone?"

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "All right. Yeah, it's a little stone-looking guy. He's tiny. You got these goggles on. You can't miss him."

**[[Firefighter Guard]]:** "Okay. All right. I'll see what I can do. Good luck on the test!"

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "Thanks. Thank you. I'm feeling pretty lucky."

*The firefighter dabs Loami up and takes the brew and the note. Returning to his squad, he shouts:*

**[[Firefighter Guard]]:** "Yes, they got this handled right! There's nothing to be worried about over here. What are we even doing? Let's go find some Mizizi!"

*The entire corner of firefighter guards abandons their section security to go search for the Mizizi, creating a massive security lapse right in front of Loami.*

*Across the stands, several failed candidates—including the Castellan siblings (Lyra and Ludo)—begin protesting their elimination.*

**[[Ludo Castellan]]:** "This is preposterous! We demand a re-evaluation!"

**[[Security Guard]]:** "Shut up! Shut up! Stop whining! Do better on the next test!"

*Ten guards swarm over to restrain the Castellans, adding to the chaotic distraction as the steam-lifts open for descent.*

### Scene 2: Into the Basalt Ring — The 50,000 Candidate Drop & Lift Rescue

*GM Narration: 50,000 candidates scramble toward the lifts. Two sections over, Ignatious wakes up at his exam desk, his literal flame hair sparking into life as he rubs his eyes.*

**[[Ignatius]] (PC):** "Yes! Yes, I won! I touched the Obelisk! Where am I?"

*Britt runs over, seeing the sparkle hair light up.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "Did you take your exam?"

**[[Ignatius]] (PC):** "Uh... you know, I think so! It was a very fast exam. You know, it's just you're right or you're wrong. We just move on. I've been wrong on a lot of them, but that's fine. It's fine."

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "Yeah... you're not wrong. Okay."

*Britt grabs Ignatious by the hand and pulls him toward the lifts. Wanting to hear the official challenge briefing, Britt moves laterally across the bleachers toward a shouting dwarf attendant near Sector 2.*

**[[Arena Attendant]]:** "Okay! Okay! Maybe they should listen to me! So arena is your first week of class, but really it's just your first day of class! Whatever! They want you to get down there and show them what you can do this time with what you actually do, not just what you can learn and remember and do on a test! So your first test: get across the arena! They have dinner waiting for you on the other side! It's night. There's stuff down there. Try not to get hurt. If you do get hurt, medical staff will be in gliders to come and pull you out. It will cost you credits though, so don't do it! Academy credits, that is—like points towards your graduation! And they also told me to say: at 8:00 AM tomorrow there is a challenge, and if you're still in the arena at that time, you are eligible for the challenge. It is very dangerous! They recommend evacuating at no later than 5:00 AM if you do not want to participate in this challenge! Again, they will rescue you, but people have died because we cannot protect you from everything—even your own stupidity, especially that last one! I think that's everything I'm supposed to say. Good luck!"

*Britt and Ignatious step onto the fourth steam-lift as it begins its multi-hundred-foot descent into the canyon. As the lift passes through a fine static ozone layer, fine sand particles float through the air, their ears pop, and all external crowd noise is instantly muted—leaving each cadet hearing only their own heartbeat.*

*Near the canyon floor, Britt spots a tiny pixie cadet frozen in terror directly beneath the descending lift weights.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "Look out!"

*Britt drops onto her belly, reaches under the elevator platform, grabs the pixie cadet by the collar, and yeets them across the landing zone.*

**[[Spectator Cadet]]:** "Holy shit! Is that worse or better than getting crushed?!"

**[[Britt]] (PC):** *(In the zone)* "Okay. Let me go."

### Scene 3: Gravity Flight & Aerial Scavenging

*GM Narration: On the canyon floor, Ignatious looks at Britt and grin.*

**[[Ignatius]] (PC):** "Hey, let's go check out the far side of the arena!"

*Ignatious activates his Hermes-style ankle wings—tiny winged flames fluttering at his boots—and grabs Britt under her arms, launching straight up into the air.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "What?!"

**[[Ignatius]] (PC):** "We're taking the air route! We're air shopping, baby!"

*They fly directly into the gravity-scrambled canyon corridor. Directional gravity shifts violently, creating aerial rip tides. Ignatious clips an outcrop as gravity flips sideways, taking 10 damage from the collision. Channeling his inner drive, he spends prayer dice to absorb the impact down to 9, staying on course.*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "There! I see bamboo thickets and supply piles near Sector 2!"

**[[Ignatius]] (PC):** "I will take some fuel bladders, leaky coolant hoses, heavy tar buckets, hollow bamboo tubes, and a sponge boom block! Thank you!"

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "Leaking coolant hoses for the table, please!"

*Ignatious flies low while Britt reaches down from his grip, scooping up a full bushel of hollow bamboo, fuel bladders, coolant hoses, tar buckets, and a sponge boom block. To manage the heavy load, Ignatious spends Hope to clear stress on Britt as they fly toward Sector 3.*

### Scene 4: The Steam Vent Spa & The Cyclops Troll

*GM Narration: Sector 4 (The Delta). Iggy drops into a hazy landscape of roaring geothermal steam vents and sulfur deposits.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** "'S warm down here... best thing Iggy's ever smelt in his life."

*Iggy walks directly toward a billowing steam vent. The super-heated steam burns for 2 HP of damage, but deeply hydrates his soil-and-clay skin like a luxury spa moment. Snagging several dense clay plugs from a nearby sub-fissure (marking 1 stress), he moves toward a glowing deposit of raw sulfur crystals.*

*Standing over the highest quality crystals is an 18-foot-tall Cyclops Troll, holding a handful of massive, high-purity crystals.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** "Hey! Big guy! Size ain't everything with crystals, ya know!"

*Iggy climbs up the troll's back Santa-sack style, holding his pouch in one arm while tapping the troll's shoulder with the other.*

**[[Sector 4 Cyclops Troll]]:** "Hey, hey, hey! What are you doing? I got the best crystals! I did not make a mistake!"

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** "Size isn't always better! Feel the power!"

**[[Sector 4 Cyclops Troll]]:** "Yes it is! With sulfur crystals, they both have a stronger aromatic and sulfuric presence, and that's what we need! No thanks! Your crystals, my crystals! Your crystals, my crystals!"

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** "You can have one of my crystals in exchange for one of your crystals!"

**[[Sector 4 Cyclops Troll]]:** "I don't negotiate! Your crystals, my crystals!"

*The troll lifts Iggy 10 feet into the air. Iggy immediately leans forward and bites the cyclops troll right on his massive hand!*

*The troll shrieks in shock, dropping all his crystals. Iggy falls 8 feet to the ground (taking 2 fall damage to his fragile wizard body), but immediately fires a mud whip via his Mud Lash ability (marking 1 stress).*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** "Gotcha!"

*Iggy snags the largest raw crystal with his mud whip, leaving one of his small crystals behind on the ground as an "apology present."*

*A female cyclops NPC nearby screams in fury:*

**[[Female Cyclops NPC]]:** "Sam! He bit you!"

*Her eyes turn blood-red as she charges toward Iggy. Clutching his giant raw crystal and small plugs, Iggy scuttles at top speed out of Sector 4 toward Sector 3.*

### Scene 5: The 200-Foot Tunnel Overclock

*GM Narration: Sector 1. Loami navigates the gravity canyons at high speed, Spider-Man climbing along left and right walls as directional gravity flips between down, left, and right. He takes 5 damage jumping between wall shifts, rolling through the impact.*

*He reaches the edge of Sector 5—a colossal 200-foot vertical fall tunnel where gravity pulls straight down into a deep basin pool.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "No time to climb. Going down!"

*Loami activates his Overclock ability (marking 1 stress), pushing his body into maximum overdrive as he dives headfirst down the 200-foot tunnel.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** *(screaming in a deep rising pitch)* "AAAAAAAHHHHHHH!"

*Mid-fall, Loami snags a heavy canvas tarp and rusted iron grappling hooks from a scrap shelf, leaving behind unstable lift-stone chunks. He impacts the basin water at high speed as a ton of metal scrap crashes down on top of him.*

*Loami surfaces a moment later, rubbing his head and burning an armor slot and 1 HP (suffering a minor wound), with the canvas tarp, bungees, and grappling hooks intact.*

### Scene 6: Sector 3 Rendezvous & Bio-Scout Alignments

*GM Narration: As dusk falls over the Apex Ring, Iggy emerges into the deciduous mycelium maze of Sector 3. Looking up at the darkening sky, he spots a red comet flying low over the canopy—Ignatious carrying Britt with a mountain of bamboo and fuel bladders.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** "'Over here! Got the big crystal!"

*Ignatious and Britt touch down softly in the clearing. Moments later, Loami emerges from the water channel, dripping wet but grinning as he hauls the canvas tarp, bungees, and grappling hooks.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** "Canvas tarp, bungees, and grappling hooks secured! Aggie's already ahead of us in the wetlands setting up the plant node alignment."

**[[Britt]] (PC):** "We've got fuel bladders, coolant hoses, bamboo, and sponge boom. Phase 1 scavenge complete."

*GM Narration: The five candidates of Squad 907 gather their haul in the Sector 3 clearing, ready to assemble their build before tomorrow morning's 8:00 AM deadline.*
