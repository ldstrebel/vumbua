# Session 7.5: Britt & Aggie's VIP Day

> [!NOTE]
> Britt and Aggie's early boarding of the Zephyr and their encounters on the Colonnade promenade are played out asynchronously via survey.

## Aggie's VIP Day

**Result:** Recipe: Mycelium Spore-Dampener (1)

1. **[root]** THURSDAY AFTERNOON

   > You step out of the heavy wooden archway of Sterling Hall, the echoes of your last class fading behind you. The campus is buzzing, but before you can plan your next move, a colorful, eccentric figure ambushes you in the courtyard.
   > 
   > It is **Angela Galaspora** (Aunt Angela), laden with herbs, dangling satchels, and pockets bulging with botanical ingredients. She beams, adjusting a green leaf crown in her hair. Angela calls out to you:
   > 
   > "Hey girls! Just the Mizizi I was looking for. Come with me—we have a day ahead!"

   * **Decision:** **Follow her into the crowd** _Sighing and looking at each other, you shrug. Knowing Aunt Angela is a force of nature._

2. **[who_are_you]** WHO ARE YOU?

   > Aunt Angela's gushing chatter fills the air as she herds you toward the university gates. Select your character to proceed.

   * **Decision:** **I am Aggie** _Re-evaluate the day from Aggie's perspective._

3. **[aggie_recap_node]** AGGIE'S REFLECTIONS

   > You walk alongside your cousin and Aunt Angela, keeping your eyes open. You review the clues you've gathered this week: Serra Vox posing as a commoner and Rill's warning at the bonfire, studying in the courtyard with Pip and Bramble, and securing the VIP passes from Valentine Sterling Jr. in the cafeteria.
   > 
   > The puzzle pieces are coming together, but you must stay alert. Aunt Angela is by far the most bizarre of your elders. You remember the uproar she caused several years ago when she asked if the Forest maybe shouldn't die? It was almost as silly as her asking what lay outside the bounds of the tall Mizizi trees. Whatever she's doing here, she looks decades younger outside the canopy than she looked within.

   * **Decision:** **Keep walking** _Follow Aunt Angela toward the berths._

4. **[recap_complete_check_aggie]** THE COLONNADE (AGGIE)

   > Angela leads you through the university gates. Ahead, the grand marble pillars of **The Colonnade** frame a stunning view. Crisp white stone and polished brass fixtures catch the sunlight. Through the pillars, you look down into the basalt canyon of the **Apex Ring**.
   > 
   > The fairgrounds outside the canyon are packed. Banners of the racing teams flap in the breeze, and the air is filled with the high-pitched hum of next-gen batteries charging, the heavy thrum of engine blocks warming up, and the shouts of race fans carrying drinks from backpack dispensers.

   * **Decision:** **Walk down the berths** _Look around for the airship berths._

5. **[aggie_poison_break]** ROLL AGGIE'S INSIGHT (DC 11)

   > During the brief pause, Aunt Angela whispers quietly to Aggie:
   > 
   > "Is Britt okay, child? She looks... slouched. Out of breath."
   > 
   > Roll Aggie's Instinct/Insight to evaluate Britt's condition.

   * **Roll:** 15 (raw: [4, 9]) - **FEAR**
   * **Decision:** **Success! Spot the sickness!**

6. **[aggie_diagnoses_poison]** THE DIAGNOSIS

   > You take a close look at Britt. The small fungal growths on her shell are dry, and her breathing is shallow. You don't know this poison, but you know something is wrong with Britt.

   * **Decision:** **Tell Auntie what you found** _Reveal your worry to the elder._

7. **[angela_poison_warning]** ANGELA'S WARNING

   > Angela frowns, examining Britt's hands.
   > 
   > "I thought she looked strange. She does not look too bad, but I get a strange feeling of deja vu looking at her right now, though I can't place it. I need my books—you must find me in the forest after exams!"

   * **Decision:** **Board the Zephyr** _Ascend onto the VIP airship deck._

8. **[board_zephyr_intro_aggie]** BOARDING THE ZEPHYR

   > You step onto the deck of the **Zephyr**. The ship is a marvel of Harmony weight-reduction science—warm mahogany wood, polished brass railings, and soft gaslight lanterns. The promenade deck wraps around a massive, circular glass floor looking down a mile into the basalt canyon of the Apex Ring.
   > 
   > Standing at the entryway are **Valentine Sterling Sr.**, the explorer you recognize from the short time he stayed with the Mizizi after discovering them. He is holding a notebook which has sketches of various vehicles on it and a map on the back. Next to him stands an Ash-blood you don't recognize. Angela gushes at them:
   > 
   > "Ah Mr. Cade, thank you thank you for arranging this adventure and pass my thanks along to Lady Ignis! Mr. Sterling, it is of course fantastic to see you again. I am so excited to see this ship fly and to learn about this Resy Race."

   * **Decision:** **Join the greeting circle** _Step forward with Angela._

9. **[diplomats_chat_aggie]** THE DIPLOMAT'S CIRCLE

   > Valentine Sterling Sr. greets you:
   > 
   > "Hello Elder Angela, welcome to the Zephyr, please note it is a Reso race not resy, but I am very excited for this season to begin—do you know any of the racers?"
   > 
   > Angela replies:
   > 
   > "Well I think our fellow Mycelium Circle member Rill had something to do with one of the teams, but honestly I forget which—wow look at these ferns...!"
   > 
   > As Sterling turns back toward the race chatter inside, leading you along, Cade Ashveil tries to fill the silence:
   > 
   > "Well these ferns are great, almost as cool as the rocks out in the race."

   * **Decision:** **Discuss how the ferns make great cover for racers** _Pitch a tactical thought about track foliage._

10. **[explore_options_aggie]** THE ZEPHYR DECK

    > The passenger deck of the Zephyr is relaxed, with well-dressed servants carrying food and drink moving among the guests. You have time to explore before the starting horn sounds. Where do you go?

    * **Decision:** **Find Pip and Bramble at the food table** _Join your study group friends for a chat._

11. **[study_group_encounter_aggie]** THE SNACK TABLES

    > You spot Pip stuffing sky-bites into her pockets while Bramble sighs, clutching a heavy textbook on structural Mycelium. They look relieved to see a friendly face.

    * **Decision:** **Swap textbooks with Bramble** _Trade your aero-static guide for his growth paths book._

12. **[book_swap_resolved_aggie]** KNOWLEDGE SWAP

    > You swap textbooks with Bramble. He is incredibly relieved to get the aero-static guide, and you get his notes on Mycelium Growth. Reading between his messy handwriting, you unlock the recipe for brewing a **Mycelium Spore-Dampener**.

    * **Decision:** **Head to the stateroom corridor** _Go find Val Jr._

13. **[find_val_stateroom_aggie]** VAL'S VIEW

    > You walk over to Valentine Jr., who is standing alone in the stateroom corridor looking down at the basalt Ring. He looks up and smiles, relieved to see you:
    > 
    > "I'm glad you're here. I wanted to pick you guys up from the dorms myself, but my father wouldn't let me leave the ship. He sent Ludo instead... who I see is currently enjoying his off-duty status at the bar."

    * **Decision:** **Ask Val about the upcoming exam** _Get his thoughts on tomorrow's test._

14. **[val_exam_warning_aggie]** VAL'S WARNING

    > Val leans in close, lowering his voice:
    > 
    > "Look, you need to be ready. I plan to lock myself in my room to study tonight. My father won't say much, but there is something after the written test though even my father won't tell me what. He's fair if nothing else."

    * **Decision:** **Thank Val and promise to keep it secret** _Prepare to rejoin Aunt Angela._

15. **[survey_final_recap_aggie]** THE FIRST HORN

    > You slip back to Aunt Angela just as the starting horn of the Reszo Race echoes across the canyon basin. The airship engines thrum beneath your feet, and the race begins.
    > 
    > You have the warning. Now you must survive the morning.
    > 
    > Congratulations! You completed the Mizizi VIP Day. Your final choices and rolls have been saved to the database. Tell your GM what you did!
    > 
    > **Based on your choices, you have earned:**
    > 
    > -   **Loot:** None
    > -   **Experiences:** None
    > -   **Clues:** None
    > -   **Stress:** 1 Stress

    * **Decision:** **Complete the Catchup** _You are ready for Session 8._
