## CHAPTER 15: ENGINE GREASE MOONSHINE & ZEPHYR'S LIGHTNING

"Your home sounds fascinating," Percival mused, tilting his head as he studied Britt and Aggie. "Our estates in the south are surrounded by manicured pine groves, but your canopies sound ancient, primeval—like stone turned to moss." <!-- L0832 -->

"It is stone and swamp and seed," Aggie said quietly. "Everything grows, and everything feeds the soil." <!-- L0834 -->

"And what happens when someone passes?" Percival asked gently. "In the capital, families erect marble mausoleums. My own ancestors bought an entire chapel just to store our crypts. Two years ago, my grandmother passed, and we sat through six days of solemn cathedral hymns." <!-- L0837 --> <!-- L0840 --> <!-- L0842 -->

"We lay them beneath the great root-burls," Aggie answered. "They nourish the fungi, the fungi feed the roots, and the circle begins anew." <!-- L0844 -->

"Congratulations," Britt offered gravely.

Percival blinked, bewildered. "What? Oh... thank you, I suppose. A very... ecological philosophy."

"Death is the way of things," Britt said simply. "Without it, there is no tomorrow." <!-- L0846 --> <!-- L0848 --> <!-- L0849 -->

Before Percival could unpack the theological implications, a heavy clinking of glass announced Lomi's return. The mechanic strolled into the circle carrying the wire milk-carrier, setting the six jars down on the flagstones with a solid *thud*. <!-- L0851 -->

The platinum-haired girl bounded across the circle like an unleashed terrier. "Is that the grease?! Let me at it!" She grabbed a jar, effortlessly popping the wax seal with her thumb, and took a deep, appreciative sniff. "Smells like industrial turpentine and fermented cherries! Fantastic! You first, boiler man." She shoved the jar into Lomi's hands. <!-- L0853 -->

Lomi took the jar, raising an eyebrow. "Down below, we call this Chime. One sip cleans the soot out of your lungs; two sips makes you forget you work for a living." He brought the rim to his lips, took a measured pull, and let out a satisfied whistle as the alcohol scorched its way down his throat. "Good batch. Strong." <!-- L0854 --> <!-- L0858 --> <!-- L0860 -->

Grinning, Lomi dropped to one knee beside the pit, took a mouthful of the spirit, and sprayed a fine mist across the embers. The alcohol ignited with a violent *crack*, throwing a brief sheet of yellow flame into the night air. <!-- L0862 --> <!-- L0864 --> <!-- L0866 -->

The girl squealed with delight. "Holy bees, that is magnificent! Give it here!" She snatched the jar back, tipped her head back, and took an enormous gulp. <!-- L0867 -->

Almost instantly, she doubled over in a violent, hacking coughing fit, spitting a feeble spray of droplets that sputtered into weak sparks on the hearth. "Ugh! Terrible! Completely failed the draft!" She wiped her chin with the back of her wrist, eyes watering furiously. "I'm going again." <!-- L0869 --> <!-- L0873 -->

"Easy, kid," Lomi warned, holding up a hand. "Takes practice—"

She didn't listen. She threw her head back and took a massive, reckless swallow.

Lomi froze, instinctively taking two steps back. Something fundamental in the air had shifted. The smell of burning pine was suddenly obliterated by the sharp, metallic stench of pure ozone. The hair along Ignatius's arms stood straight on end; tiny blue sparks crackled across the iron buckles of Britt's pack. <!-- L0874 --> <!-- L0875 -->

The girl puffed her cheeks, her pale skin flushing a vibrant, electric violet. Her platinum hair snapped outward into a corona of wild, standing needles, humming with static tension. She leaned over the fire pit and expelled the mouthful of moonshine. <!-- L0877 -->

The reaction was catastrophic.

A deafening *CRACK* split the sky. A jagged spear of violet lightning plunged from the clear night clouds straight into the fire pit, detonating the alcohol in a concussive shockwave of thunder and purple fire. Sparks rained down across the quad, washing the stone walls in blinding lavender glare.

Ten feet away, the automaton student jerked backward, the concussive boom dislodging its brass jaw, which clanged onto the paving stones with a hollow ring. <!-- L0883 -->

"Holy gods above," Bramble choked out, clutching his bark-plated chest.

The girl shook her head, her hair collapsing back down around her shoulders in limp, smoking strands. She looked over at Lomi, completely oblivious to the crater she had just blown in the kindling, and gave him a bright, triumphant grin. "Now *that* was pretty good."

"That was... certainly something," Lomi managed, staring in shock.

Before anyone could speak, a sharp, authoritative voice cut through the courtyard. A young woman in a crisp blue travel romper and patterned turban pushed through the stunned crowd, seizing the lightning girl firmly by her collar. <!-- L0885 -->

"There you are!" Rill scolded, shaking her head in exasperation. "I have been searching every quad from Block 1 to the harbor! Come on, we're going back."

"No!" the girl whined, trying to dig her heels into the dirt. "It's so boring over there! All they do is ask questions! Out here they have fire and engine oil!"

"I brought hot chocolate," Rill said flatly.

The girl paused mid-struggle. "Genuine chocolate?"

"Genuine chocolate."

"Fine," the girl muttered, turning to follow. "The moonshine tasted like varnish anyway." <!-- L0887 -->

Ignatius stepped forward, calling after them into the dark. "Wait! Hey, Lightning Girl! What's your name?" <!-- L0890 --> <!-- L0892 --> <!-- L0894 -->

Rill paused, glancing over her shoulder. Her gaze swept past Ignatius, landing squarely on Britt and Aggie standing by the embers. A warm, knowing smile touched her lips, and she raised a hand in greeting. <!-- L0895 -->

"Her name is Zephyr, not Lightning Girl, you Lava Boy!" Rill called back, her eyes twinkling. "Aggie, Britt—find me tomorrow after classes in the grass-roofed hall on the bluff!" <!-- L0897 --> <!-- L0899 --> <!-- L0901 -->

Ignatius turned slowly to Lomi, his brow furrowed in utter disbelief. "Did she just call me Lava Boy?" <!-- L0902 --> <!-- L0904 -->

"You called her Lightning Girl," Lomi pointed out, smothering a grin. "Fair's fair. Maybe you two already have pet names." <!-- L0906 --> <!-- L0908 --> <!-- L0910 -->

"I was asking for her name!" Ignatius protested, throwing his hands in the air. "And are we just going to ignore the fact that she summoned a literal bolt of lightning out of thin air? I control flame, but that was completely unhinged!" <!-- L0911 --> <!-- L0917 --> <!-- L0920 -->

The automaton picked his brass jaw off the flagstones, clicked it back into its socket with a metallic *snap*, and nodded solemnly. "No, she definitely caused that. Truly electrifying." <!-- L0923 --> <!-- L0925 --> <!-- L0927 -->

Aggie stepped to the edge of the pit, watching Rill's retreating silhouette disappear into the campus shadows. "She recognized us," Aggie whispered. "She was the exchange student in our grove last year." <!-- L0935 --> <!-- L0943 --> <!-- L0945 -->

"She works with Dean Vane now," Britt added, her voice low and guarded. "Tomorrow after class, we find out why." <!-- L0950 -->
