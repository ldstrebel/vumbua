## CHAPTER 4: LAB BRIEFING AND PURPLE LIQUID CACHE

Inside Elijah's upper laboratory, glowing brass lanterns illuminated rows of glass retorts, crystal incubators, and polished petrified-wood workbenches. Rill stepped up to Britt, holding out a sealed brass case. "Britt, come into the lab," she called out. <!-- L1050 -->

Opening the container, Rill revealed a small crystal cache sample filled with a pulsating, neon-purple liquid compass fluid that shimmered with internal light. <!-- L1092 -->

Britt leaned over the bench, her sharp eyes widening as she examined the substance. "Yeah... that liquid looks just like what caused my rash," Britt confirmed, gesturing to the faint glowing fungal marks along her arm. <!-- L1093 -->

"This came from the canyon where I grew up before I joined the Exchange," Rill explained solemnly. "It's super lethal... if you drink this once a week, it will stall its progression. Otherwise, Britt, you're going to die in two weeks." <!-- L1096 -->

Britt looked closely at the glowing liquid. "Have you seen material like this met by Mizizi before?" Britt asked. <!-- L1111 -->

"Yeah, my elders taught us about it," Rill nodded, her voice hushed with reverence. "It's sacred to the Wadi." <!-- L1112 -->

"It's super weird," Britt noted, holding the crystal vial up to the light. <!-- L1115 -->

"We keep it sealed," Rill warned gently. "Don't let it touch your skin." <!-- L1116 -->
