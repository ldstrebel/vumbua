Lucky brought Sarge's body to the Ironclad steamship anchored at the harbor pier. A broad Goliath captain wearing metal plate armor sewn into a heavy leather jacket—inscribed with the brass lettering *Ironclad*—nodded solemnly as rain streamed off his iron pauldrons, taking Lucky by the arm as he flagged down his crew members. A squad of crisp naval pallbearers hoisted Sarge onto their shoulders, marching up the wooden gangplank toward the steamship deck. <!-- L1018 -->

Nearby on the mudflats, Val sat silently beside the triage cots, his head bowed in quiet grief for his lost squadmates under the dark grey morning sky. <!-- L1021 -->

"Follow him, turtle dude!" Ignatius called out, adjusting his dark traveler's cloak as he directed Mwaza-Kasa across the mudflats toward the coast line. <!-- L1024 -->

Moments later, the Ironclad crew formed a pristine pallbearer honor guard along the starboard railing and fired a 21-gun naval salute—firing massive brass artillery cannons that detonated in thunderous clouds of red, amber, and gold smoke, giving Sarge a proper warrior's farewell into the harbor waters. Lucky turned back to Lomi, wiping a tear from his eye as he stepped toward the spider-walker. "All right. I'm ready." <!-- L1037 -->

The full crew of 11—Britt, Iggy, Ignatius, Lomi, Aggie, Val, Pip, Bramble, Saffron, Zephyr, and Lucky—boarded Professor Ink's colossal spider walker, Elijah.

The moment all boots cleared the lower hatch, Mwaza-Kasa tore a split in the earth and rocketed toward the ancient forest at extreme speed, leaving a spinning V-wake of grass and mud in its wake. The spider walker galloped in swift pursuit, its eight jointed mechanical legs thundering against the forest floor.
