## CHAPTER 3: THE ARACHNID WALKER AND THE REPRESSED KNOWLEDGE

Lomi made the split-second choice as they ran through the sprawling medical camp, keeping his eyes fixed on Britt's retreating form. "Stay with Britt," he called out to the group over his shoulder. <!-- L0711 -->

They followed Rill up the wet brass boarding ladder of a spindly, elegant arachnid walker moored at the harbor piers. Rain rattled against its iron-and-petrified-wood hull as they climbed past crew bunks and shimmering research laboratories, entering a spacious high-altitude cockpit lounge. Inside stood Professor Ink, a tall elven woman with dark raven hair, dressed in a sharp black scholar's suit adorned with silver brass fittings and leather tool harnesses.

Rill burst into the cockpit lounge, her blue hair soaked and her breath coming in frantic gasps. "Professor, professor, professor! There's something here! The elders told me there's never been a tortoise that's left its grove, and it's right there, walking through the forest!" <!-- L0717 -->

Professor Ink held up a calm, elegant hand, turning from a brass map display. "Slower and more clearly," she instructed gently, her voice steady against the howling wind outside. She turned to Britt and Lomi, offering a traditional Mizizi fist-bump with a warm, respectful nod. "It is a pleasure." <!-- L0720 -->

"I would not normally do it this way," Professor Ink continued, gesturing around the walker's warm, wood-paneled lounge deck, "but I would like to select your group and at least one other to come with us into the Mizizi forest. It's going to take us about three days to make it on Elijah here, and I believe I will need a guide through the forest. Would you come with me?" <!-- L0734 -->

"Yeah, half our friends are headed there right now," Britt agreed immediately, nodding without hesitation. "So, yes, let's go." <!-- L0737 -->

"Great," Professor Ink smiled, smoothing her black lapels. "Well, then I will meet you at the edge of the camp in one hour's time and we will board and move." <!-- L0738 -->

Lomi turned to Britt, rubbing his chin thoughtfully. "Yeah, that's why Lomi was going to get Iggy to talk to Professor Kante anyway. So this works." <!-- L0744 -->

Across the muddy camp, the communication stone in Ignatius's hand crackled to life with a hum of Aetheric static. Professor Kante's voice echoed through the polished crystal speaker over the rustle of academic manuscripts. "Professor Kante, please come in... My working theory is that the Mizizi have been misinformed. Maybe what they have been taught is preventing their connection. Everything that they have told us has resulted in no progress—it must be something repressed!" <!-- L0748 -->

Britt picked up the matching speaking stone resting on the walker's console, her voice cautious and weary as she spoke into the crystal. "A tortoise has exited the forest and we need to go back immediately. That's what we know." <!-- L0791 -->

Professor Kante's voice filled the cabin, earnest and urgent over the crystal link. "Could you tell me more about this? What you know, we need to know! If you do not trust us, then we have yet to prove ourselves, especially after tonight." <!-- L0796 -->
