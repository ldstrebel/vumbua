## CHAPTER 30: THE AMBER AMBUSH IN THE ALLEY

Outside the Celestial Lounge, the limestone alleys curved into the deeper, shadowed folds of the upper city. Britt walked a few paces behind the three men, her boots clicking softly against the damp paving. Up close, the two flanking escorts were massive, their broad shoulders draped in rough-spun cloaks, intricate dark dreadlocks swaying against silver piercings in their ears. They moved in uncanny silence, communicating with fluid, practiced hand signs that cut through the dark without a whisper.

The smaller man in front—slender, wearing a tailored wool overcoat—paused beneath a guttering gas lamp and turned with an easy, practiced smile. "You said Block 99, sister? You're a first-year candidate?"

"Yes," Britt said, her voice dry with fatigue. "I'm Britt. Just trying to find my bunk before morning."

"I'm Azer," the man replied smoothly, gesturing for her to follow him down an archway leading into an unlit passage between two monolithic lecture halls. "I know this city inside and out. Attempted the candidacy run myself a few seasons back. Didn't make the cut, but I know the shortcuts."

A hundred paces back, Iggy padded through the fog, trying to keep Britt's silhouette in view. But as Azer led her deeper into the labyrinth, the turns became erratic—a sharp right down a narrow drainage sluice, a sudden left beneath a flying buttress. It wasn't a direct route to campus; it was a game of evasion. Iggy scrambled down a cobblestone ramp, trying to anticipate their trajectory, but on an unfamiliar terrace, his boots slipped on wet moss. By the time he righted himself, the misty alley ahead was empty.

In the blind alley, Azer came to a sudden halt before a dead-end stone wall. He turned, hands resting lightly in his coat pockets. "Well," he said softly, his voice losing its warmth. "I think we lost your little shadow." He bowed his head with mocking remorse. "I really am sorry about this, sister."

Before Britt could draw breath, a violent, crushing impact exploded against the base of her skull. Two heavy lead saps struck from the shadows behind her with bone-splintering force—nineteen points of raw, savage momentum that rattled her teeth and sent fire blooming across her vision. Instinctively, Britt threw her weight forward, the ancient bark-grain of her skin tightening as she channeled every ounce of ancestral resilience, burning all four of her protective armor lattices to absorb the blow. The shock wave tore through her, dropping her to her knees, blood welling in her mouth, but the shell held.

Weighted cordage whipped through the dark. A heavy hemp net dropped over her shoulders, entangling her arms as the two silent bruisers pinned her against the stones. Calloused hands tore into her pockets, rifling through her travel kit.

Fighting through the ringing in her ears, Britt focused inward. Drawing upon the living mycelial pulse that connected her to her grove, she forged a frantic mental tether through the stones, burning with sharp urgency: *Right. Left. Down the sluice. Find Iggy. Ambush.*

With a feral snarl of bark and sinew, Britt planted her boots and heaved upward, snapping the net cords with raw, desperate strength. But as she broke free, the taller bruiser tore her candidate registration receipt from her coat lining, brandishing the stamped copper-bordered paper.

Azer peered into the torn lining, his sneer deepening into disgust. "Nothing? Not a single brass sovereign? Did they send you from your backwater jungle with empty pockets?" Then his sharp gaze dropped to her throat, catching the glint of a dark wooden pendant resting against her collarbone—a petrified acorn shell, carved from the sacred heartwood of her ancestral tree. "Wait. That might fetch a price in the antique stalls."

Azer lunged. His first swipe missed as Britt jerked away, but his second hand closed over the cord, snapping the sinew and ripping the sacred heirloom from her neck.

"Nickel! Tus! The receipt!" Azer hissed. The two giants turned and sprinted north into the fog with the paper, while Azer bolted south down the alley toward the thoroughfare.

Britt didn't hesitate. The receipt was paper; the pendant was her soul. Snarling, her face contorted with ancient fury, she gave chase. Despite the heavy, deliberate gait of her turtle-kin heritage, she surged across the cobbles with terrifying speed, her dark hair flying like a storm. <!-- L0151 -->

Azer burst out of the alley onto the broad flagstones of the avenue, gasping for air—and froze. Standing directly in the center of the road, looking mildly surprised behind round copper goggles, was Iggy. <!-- L0153 -->

Before Azer could skirt around the earth-kin, a shadow descended upon him like a falling oak. Britt hurled herself forward, her shoulder striking Azer squarely in the spine. They slammed into the flagstones in a violent tangle of limbs and flying dust. In a heartbeat, Britt was on top of him, knees pinned hard against his biceps, one hand gripping his throat while the other pressed the razor-honed tip of her chipped flint dagger directly against his jugular. Her chest heaved with animal fury, her eyes burning like black coals. <!-- L0154 --> <!-- L0155 --> <!-- L0156 --> <!-- L0157 -->

Iggy blinked, took two steps backward, and quietly seated himself upon a flat granite mounting block. He folded his hands in his lap, nodded politely to the night air, and decided Britt had the situation entirely under control. <!-- L0158 -->
