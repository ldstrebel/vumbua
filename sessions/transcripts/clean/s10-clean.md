## Play-by-Play

### Scene 0: Pre-Rendezvous Scavenging & Magnetic Sand Surfing
*GM Narration: Sector 1 Canyons. Before reaching the Sector 3 rendezvous point, Loami uses salvaged copper-wound industrial anchors and magnetic clamps to scavenge heavy hardware from the shifting canyon sands.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** *"The friction of the sand creates static electricity that temporarily charges up these magnetic clamps..."*

*Loami trudges through the canyon like a living metal detector as static friction charges his clamps. Shifting sand waves—40 to 80 feet tall—solidify into sheer cliffs in seconds. Ignatius attempts to hold the magnets overhead as a flying spotter, but the sudden magnetic surge yanks him down into a sand wave.*

*Adapting quickly, Loami mounts a sheet of scrap metal, using magnetic polarity flips to surf and sled across the shifting canyon waves while Ignatius calls out search targets from above. Loami collects iron-hooped barrels, cables, and copper fittings before heading to Sector 3.*

---

### Scene 1: Iggy's First Tree & The Forest Loop Chase

*GM Narration: Sector 3 Tree Line. Iggy exits the steam-vented terrain and enters Sector 3. He stands at the edge of the forest, staring up at 90-foot tall petrified trees.*

*GM Note: Iggy has never seen a real forest or full-sized tree in his entire life (having only seen manicured city median trees in Bamboo). Intrigued, Iggy sets down his gear, pulls out his journal, and begins sketching the massive stone trunks. He carefully peels bark samples to store in his knapsack.*

*A frustrated roar echoes through the clearing steam—Sam the Cyclops is closing in.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *"Let's go, let's go!"*

*Iggy packs his bark samples and attempts to navigate southwest through the dense woods.*

* **Navigation Attempt 1:** Iggy walks for 20 minutes, but the canopy brightens and he steps right back OUT of the tree line—where Sam is now three-quarters closer.
* **Navigation Attempt 2 (The Pinball Loop):** Iggy tries again, bouncing off stone trunks like a pinball machine. He pops back out of the forest line a second time—directly in front of Sam, Sarah (the female cyclops), and the young elf girl. Sarah stands with her arms crossed, smirking at Iggy and holding out her hand for her stolen crystal.

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *"Hold please!"*

*Iggy chugs a stamina potion, drops into his rolling ball form, and bolts for the basin water 100 yards away.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *(dropping his bark samples on the ground as he runs)* *"A peace offering! A tiny version of an apology!"*

*Sam tries to trap Iggy under his massive stone hands, but forgets to close his fingers, allowing Iggy to pop out like a bowling ball. Frustrated, Sam rears back with a 20-foot petrified tree trunk and hucks it like a spear at Iggy on the riverbank.*

*The tree trunk crashes into the mud right beside Iggy, exploding mud and shockwaves into his side for 6 physical damage. Iggy burns 3 stress to absorb the impact.*

*GM Note: As a result of having a 20-foot tree hurled at his head by a screaming cyclops, Iggy acquires a permanent traumatic phobia of trees.*

*Clutching his head in shock, Iggy leaps off the muddy riverbank into the 1,700-foot deep water basin to escape.*

### Scene 2: The Soulless Forest & The Shoreline Pebble Signal

*GM Narration: Along the Sector 3 shoreline, Aggie and Britt wait at the rendezvous point. Aggie reflects on her walk through the woods.*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *"It looks like home, but it's missing the soul. A master artist created this place knowing nothing about what it feels like to live in it."*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *"Are we worried about anyone not being able to find us?"*

**[[Britt]] (PC):** *"Nope."*

*Aggie walks up to an ancient petrified tree and places her hands against the rough bark, channeling her Mizizi instinct.*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *(Rolling Instinct check: 17 with Hope)* *"Tell me where our people are."*

*GM Narration: The forest doesn't speak in words, but sends vivid mental images to Aggie's mind: "We were told not to tell you where your people were... but one of yours tried to enter the forest twice and got lost. We lost track after one of our brethren was felled and thrown at him."*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *"Someone threw a tree?!"*

*Realizing Iggy is hiding underwater nearby, Aggie kneels at a flat basalt ledge and taps river stones rhythmically over the water—a steady, repeating acoustic signal echoing across the basin.*

*Submerged 30 feet below, Iggy hears the clacking, follows the sound waves, **collects every dropped river stone from the underwater silt**, and crawls up onto the muddy shore.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *(shivering, glaring back at the tree line)* *"The trees... they're hostile. They threw themselves at me."*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *"The trees didn't throw themselves, Iggy! The cyclops threw them!"*

### Scene 3: Midnight Construction & The "Natty" Renaming

*GM Narration: Midnight. Loami and Ignatious arrive hauling scavenged barrels, tar buckets, coolant hoses, canvas, and raw sulfur crystals.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** *(handing Ignatious a swig from a wineskin)* *"First of all, your name is great and all, but we already have an Iggy. We have an Aggie. So, you're now Natty. I'm calling you Natty."*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** *"Alright, crew. Phase 1 is done. Now we build."*

*Squad 907 works through the night constructing the Canopy Raft (`raft.png`):*
1. **The Deck & Hull:** Six heavy iron-hooped buoyancy barrels bound with steel cables and high-tension bungees, supporting a wooden deck and canvas tarp. Four heavy rusted shovels serve as steering oars.
2. **The Balloon Canopy:** Fuel bladders sealed with dark tar and painted in forest camouflage, suspended over a central burner mounting Natty's raw sulfur crystal.
3. **Armament:** Two coolant-hose slingshots loaded with petrified seed pods wrapped in glowing green bioluminescent moss (glowing tracer rounds) and mycelial root bundles.

**[[Loami]] (PC):** *(Rolling Crafting check: DC 15 — Full Success!)* *"She's airtight, buoyant, and hidden!"*

### Scene 4: Rill's 04:00 AM Barrier Warning

*GM Narration: 04:00 AM. Silver river fog rolls over the Sector 3 shoreline. A figure steps silently out of the mist—Rill, captain of Team 6 (The Silent Drift).*

**[[Rill]]:** *"You've built something impressive out of scrap, candidates."*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *"Rill! What are you doing out here?"*

**[[Rill]]:** *"Warning you. The 08:00 challenge isn't a standard run. The Dean and the Logistics Officers are dropping the sector suppression barriers. Whatever is inside these sectors is coming out. If you're staying past the 05:00 evac deadline, survival itself is what gets you noticed... they love their heroes."*

*Rill hands Aggie a heavy brass-bound flare gun.*

**[[Rill]]:** *"If the storm takes the sky or you get pinned in the basin, fire this straight up. Don't die out here."*

*Rill melts back into the misty tree line. The 05:00 AM evacuation bell bongs in the distance.*

### Scene 5: The 08:00 Barrier Drop & Dagger Sharks Attack

*GM Narration: 08:00 AM. Sirens blare. Bruised-violet static arcs crack down from colossal iron Walker-Core legs as sector suppression barriers shatter.*

*Squad 907 pushes their raft 100 yards out into open basin water. Long chitinous fins slice through the foam—a pod of 12-foot bioluminescent, armored Dagger Sharks with glowing violet lateral stripes.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *(panicking)* *"Dagger sharks! They track living energy signatures! They took my friends in the lower trenches! We have to get off the water NOW!"*

**[[Ignatious]] (PC):** *"I'm lighting the sulfur crystal! Get the balloon up!"*

*Natty uses Sparking Engines to ignite the burner. As flames erupt, a 12-foot Dagger Shark leaps out of the foam, biting Natty's shoulder armor for 8 physical damage. Natty burns an armor slot to hold the burner steady.*

**[[Aggie]] (PC):** *(using Wild Touch)* *"Hold the fire steady, Natty! I've got you!"*

*Aggie supercharges the flame. The camouflage balloon swells, lifting the Canopy Raft 15 feet into the air just as Dagger Sharks snap empty jaws below.*

---

### Scene 6: Flying the Raft & Canopy Anchoring

*GM Narration: The Canopy Raft hovers 30 feet above the basin, drifting toward the Sector 3 petrified forest canopy.*

**[[Iggy]] (PC):** *"Distraction tactic! They're still tracking our bio-resonance! Aggie, Brit—fire the seed pods toward the forest bank!"*

*Aggie and Britt load seed pods wrapped in glowing bioluminescent moss into the coolant-hose slingshots, launching them 100 yards onto shoreline mudflats (**THWWACK-SNAP!** / **SPLAAASH!**). The kinetic impact and bioluminescent glow lure the entire Dagger Shark pod away from the vessel.*

**[[Loami]] (PC):** *"Grappling hook deployed! Tie us off to the high canopy branch!"*

*Loami launches a rusted grappling hook around an 80-foot petrified canopy bough. Squad 907 secures the heavy steel cables, anchoring their camouflaged balloon-raft safely inside the elevated tree foliage—hidden, elevated, and safe from the storm below.*
