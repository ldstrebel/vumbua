# Vumbua Master Campaign Narrative Bible & Macro Arc Tracker

> **The Single Source of Truth for Novelization**: This living document bridges individual micro-chunk scene novelizations with the grand multi-session narrative arc. Every scene drafted must be cross-referenced against these rules, character empathy anchors, and ongoing plot threads to guarantee zero plot holes and flawless reader immersion.

---

## 🏛️ Core Principles: The Novelist's Charter

### 0. Player Canon & Soul Over Literary Smoothing (The Supreme Law)
* **#1 Priority**: Capture what the players and GM actually describe, say, and do in the sessions. Their jokes, specific tactical ideas, unique character quirks, and comedic instincts are the sacred heart and soul of Vumbua.
* **Fidelity Over Polish**: I would rather have slightly less elegant prose than override or sanitize the canonical descriptions that the players gave.
* **Transferring Mediums with Soul**: We are moving from table audio to literature, which requires connective tissue (sensory staging, physical mannerisms, internal reflections), but this added text must *serve* the player's intent, never replace it.
* **Deliberation Gate for Retcons**: We **never** unilaterally rewrite, retcon, or add plot points. Any retcon or structural adjustment happens *only* after explicit deliberation between you and me, and only when we believe it resolves an impossible audio gap or truly elevates what the player was attempting to do.

### 1. The "Cold Reader" Grounding Rule (Zero Table Presumption)
* **Never assume the reader knows the lore:** The reader did not sit at the table, does not have character sheets, and does not know the GM’s private notes.
* **No Unanchored Proper Nouns:** Words like *The Loom*, *The Cull*, *Lavsidian*, *The Three Cores*, *Ash-Bloods*, or *The Seat* must never drop into prose without physical, sensory, or emotional context.
* **Show the Mechanics Through Character Interaction:**
  - Don't lecture the reader on *The Loom* in a summary paragraph. Show the tactile click of copper typewriter keys, the ticking gears, the cold flag springing up, and the attendant snatching the slip.
  - Don't explain *Embodied Energy* abstractly. Show Ignatius feeling his hearth-fire drain into fatigue, or Lomi tightening a brass conduit until the hum settles into a smooth frequency.

### 2. The Micro $\leftrightarrow$ Macro Cross-Referencing Gate
Before writing and after auditing any scene block (`sN-scene-XX.md`), we check:
1. **Thread Progression:** Which active plot thread does this scene advance or foreshadow?
2. **Lore Grounding:** Did any new faction, technology, or location appear? Is it grounded for a first-time reader?
3. **Character Empathy:** Does the character's internal reflection reveal *why* they care?
4. **Future Canon Lock:** Does anything in this scene contradict later revelations (e.g. Session 12's six ancient clans, Mwaza-Kasa, or Serra Vox's lineage)?

## 👤 Core Cast Empathy & Character Anchors

Every character must have an **Empathy Hook** that a cold reader can immediately connect to within their first scene:

### 1. Britt (The Root-Kin Guardian)
* **Empathy Hook**: Overwhelming sisterly protectiveness masked by intense, unblinking focus. Britt would tear down an institution before letting Aggie be left behind.
* **Sensory Signature**: Living root dreadlocks that shift with her mood, gray linen tunic, acute botanical and tactile instincts, stubborn stillness.
* **Internal Vulnerability**: Terrified of being separated from Aggie; deep suspicion of Harmony’s sterile, mechanical order.
* **Writing Mandate**: When Britt does something erratic (e.g. knocking over a typewriter, claiming Bluetooth is broken), internal prose must make it clear that this is calculated desperation to buy Aggie time.

### 2. Aggie (The Gentle Heart)
* **Empathy Hook**: An observant, artistic soul navigating an aggressive, high-stakes military academy that doesn't understand her pace.
* **Sensory Signature**: Soft, gentle movements, expressive eyes, instinctive emotional perception of those around her.
* **Internal Vulnerability**: Feels like a burden to Britt, terrified that her slower test-taking or physical limits will drag Britt down.
* **Writing Mandate**: Highlight Aggie's quiet perceptive wins. When Britt is frantically protecting her, Aggie is often the one who notices the subtle emotional cues Britt misses.

### 3. Lomi (The Blue-Collar Rig Mechanic)
* **Empathy Hook**: The fiercely loyal working-class hero who fights for the underpaid and overworked, using humor and machine banter to hide his fear of failure.
* **Sensory Signature**: Woolen flat cap (with tiny ribbon), canvas collar, calloused hands permanently smudged with engine grease, sharp eye for pneumatic fittings and stress fractures.
* **Internal Vulnerability**: Imposter syndrome among academy aristocrats; profound empathy for the manual laborers, janitors, and guards discarded by the system.
* **Writing Mandate**: Lomi evaluates the world through engineering and labor dignity. He doesn't just see guards—he asks about their overtime and health benefits.

### 4. Ignatius (The Hearth-Flame Scion)
* **Empathy Hook**: A warm, charismatic youth bearing the stifling expectations of a noble Ash-Blood lineage, searching for people who value him for himself rather than his clan’s fire.
* **Sensory Signature**: Literally burning hearth-flame hair that flares or dims with his emotional state, glowing ember crown, traveler's cloak, effortless physical grace.
* **Internal Vulnerability**: Constantly compensating for the violent, authoritarian reputation of his clan; deeply craves unpretentious camaraderie.
* **Writing Mandate**: Ignatius brings warmth and levity to dark rooms. His magic should feel vibrant and living, not cold or destructive.

### 5. Iggy (The Ancient Earth-Kin)
* **Empathy Hook**: The pure, curious child of the deep earth who perceives ancient truths others are blind to, yet is constantly underestimated due to his small stature.
* **Sensory Signature**: Humanoid packed-clay and soil body (**NO TURTLE SHELL**), green moss and sprouts on head, oversized round copper goggles filled with water, heavy oversized wool trench coat.
* **Internal Vulnerability**: Yearns to see the wide world ("touch the sky"), instinctively grieves for dying or corrupted ancient beings.
* **Writing Mandate**: Iggy speaks little, but his actions are profoundly symbolic (climbing the crane, laying basalt over fallen comrades, drawing arrows to the seabed). Treat his perspective with poetic, elemental wonder.

## 🔍 Macro-Review Checklist for Every Scene Block

When drafting and auditing each micro-chunk block:

1. **Cold Reader Test**:
   - [ ] Did a new reader learn what the setting looks, smells, and sounds like?
   - [ ] Are character actions emotionally motivated (internal reflection present)?
   - [ ] Is dialogue formatted cleanly with physical action beats instead of `-ly` adverbs?
2. **Lore Grounding Test**:
   - [ ] Are all in-universe terms explained through sensory interaction or character banter?
   - [ ] Zero ungrounded jargon or meta-table abbreviations.
3. **Plot Thread Continuity**:
   - [ ] Does this scene advance at least one thread in the Master Arc Ledger?
   - [ ] Does this scene plant or pay off foreshadowing cleanly?
4. **Parity & Audit Gate**:
   - [ ] Passed 0-token linter (`leak_detector`, `lore_guardian`, `echo_detector`, `style_analyzer`).
   - [ ] Passed 100% transcript line ledger reconciliation (`verify_parity.py`).
