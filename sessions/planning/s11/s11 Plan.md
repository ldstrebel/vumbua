# Session 11 Plan: The Tempest Clearing & Canopy Raft Encounter

---

## 🌪️ Environment: The Tempest Clearing
* **Tier:** 3 (Traversal / Event) | **Difficulty Benchmark:** 17
* **Description:** A high-altitude basalt clearing above the Mizizi canopy where storm winds batter Squad 907's Canopy Raft.
* **Impulses:** Pitch the deck, pull targets overboard, snap tethers.

### 🔥 GM Environment Actions & Reactions
* **Hurricane Gale (GM Action — Spend 1 Fear):**
  * A violent wind shear sweeps the deck. All PCs on the raft must make a **Strength Reaction Roll (DC 17)**. On a failure, they are knocked **Restrained** in the rigging or pushed to the deck edge.
* **Snagged Anchor (GM Reaction — Triggered on Failure w/ Fear):**
  * When a player rolls a Failure with Fear, the raft's trailing steel cable snags on petrified treetops, jerking the platform. Anyone not anchored takes **2 Stress** and drops whatever item they are holding.

---

## ⏱️ Encounter Clocks & High-Stakes Mechanics

1. **Dawn Survival Clock (Countdown 8 — Session Progress):**
   * Progress clock tracking remaining time until dawn and extraction.
   * Ticks **DOWN (advances)** on player action rolls:
     * **Critical Success:** Tick down 3.
     * **Success with Hope:** Tick down 2.
     * **Success with Fear:** Tick down 1.
     * **Failure:** 0 ticks.
2. **Eyewall Storm Surge Clock (Countdown 6 — Severe Hazard):**
   * Hazard threat clock that ticks **UP** whenever a player rolls a **Failure with Fear**, or when the GM spends **1 Fear**.
   * **Trigger at 6 (Catastrophic Eyewall Surge):** The eyewall tears open, dealing **3d10+8 Physical/Lightning Damage** to all unshielded PCs, knocking them **Vulnerable** and threatening Near-Death. Resets clock to 0.
3. **Mwaza-Kasa (Spirit Tortoise) Sync Clock (Countdown 4 — Climax Goal):**
   * Advanced when a player spends an action to commune with Mwaza-Kasa (**Instinct or Presence Roll — DC 16**).
   * **Completion Trigger at 4 (Sheltering Sentinel Activation):** Mwaza-Kasa anchors into the petrified tree node and projects an **indestructible Aetheric Shell-Wall dome** over the Canopy Raft, rendering all allies completely immune to Eyewall Storm Surges and beast assaults!
1.  **Gondola Equilibrium (Dynamic Countdown 8):**
  * The Canopy Raft must stay centered in the storm's eye. Every PC action roll with Fear ticks this clock down by 1.
  * **Trigger at 0:** The deck pitches violently into the eyewall. All PCs must make an **Agility Reaction Roll (DC 17)** or take **3d8+5 Physical Damage** and become **Vulnerable** as they hang precariously over the edge.
  * **Stabilization Action:** A PC can spend an action to make an **Agility or Finesse Roll (DC 17)** to adjust winch ropes and tick the clock back **UP by 2** (or **+4** if Bramble is rescued and lashing lines).
* **Vertical Line of Sight:**
  * Aerial enemies hover at **Far Range** vertically. Enemies on the forest floor below are at **Very Far Range**.
  * Shifting raft height closer to canopy branches brings the deck within **Close Range** of climbing beasts.

* **Mirror-Step (Action — Spend 1 Fear after attack):** Teleport 40ft into canopy shadows, leaving behind an aetheric decoy.
* **Frequency Overload (Action — Spend 1 Fear):** Spew static-charged embers in a Close line (**3d6+4 Magic Damage**). Disrupts all spellcasting and active mind-links for 1 round.

### 🐱 Mizizi Bobcat (Stunted Canopy Swarm — 1 BP Total)
* **Role:** Tier 3 Minions (1 BP for group of 5) | **Difficulty:** 15 | **Evasion:** 15

* **Minion (9) (Passive):** Every 9 points of damage dealt by an AoE or multi-target attack defeats an additional Bobcat.
* **Group Attack (Action — Spend 1 Fear):** Swarm up winch cables and make one shared attack roll. On success, deal combined physical damage.

### 🐢 Spirit Tortoise (Mwaza-Kasa) (1x Neutral Aetheric Guardian — 0 BP)
* **Role:** Tier 3 Neutral Entity | **Difficulty:** 12 | **Evasion:** 12
* **Primary Attack:** **Pacifying Pulse** (Close, +4 to Hit | DC 16 Presence/Strength check or pushed to Far range and **Pacified**)
* **Aetheric Hum (Passive):** Feral beasts cannot enter Close range or attack the Tortoise directly.
* **Resonance Symbiosis (Passive):** Humanoids within Close range gain **+1 Evasion** and **+1 Armor**.
* **The Taboo (Reaction):** Staring at or touching the Tortoise triggers a DC 15 Presence roll. Failure: Take **2 Stress** and become **Vulnerable** (vision collapse). Success: Gain **+2 bonus** to the next Tortoise Sync roll.

## 🌿 Tactical Rescue Objective: Squad 06 & The Canopy Tree Fortress

### 🏰 Squad 06's Master Plan: "The Tree Fortress"
Rather than relying solely on a swaying gondola, **Squad 06 (The Harvesters)** pooled their talents to construct a living **Canopy Tree Fortress** in the high petrified boughs to weather the eyewall storm:
* **Bramble's Living Wood:** Grown and woven massive root-vines into thick, interlocking timber ramparts.
* **Saffron's Blueprints:** Sketched the structural layout and airflow channels in her leatherbound book.
* **Kael's Crystal Stabilization:** Calculated tension math and anchored Umber crystal resonance spikes to brace the fort against gale-force wind shear.
* **Pip's Snack Provisioning:** Appointed official "Quartermaster" to stock the fortress with emergency breakfast pastries (biscuits and bacon).

### 📻 The Connection: Bramble & Kael's Radio Transmission
* **Misinformation Resolved:** Bramble was previously sending static/misinformation across the tree network to conceal their position. However, when the image of **Iggy** and Aggie's relayed questions came through the network, Bramble put two and two together and realized it was **Aggie** and Squad 907!
* **Speaking Stone Activation:** Knowing Aggie's location, Bramble immediately tells **Kael** to activate the prototype **Speaking Stone** he gave Aggie back in `s4.5`.
* **The Transmission:** The Speaking Stone in Aggie's satchel screeches to life with a sharp static whine before **Bramble's low, rumbling voice** echoes out across Squad 907's Canopy Raft: 
  > *"Aggie? Is that you? Pip ran back for biscuits in front of the storm wall—she's right below us!"*

### 🦅 The Crisis: Pip & The Storm Hawk
* **The Snatch:** Pip was sprinting back in front of the wall of storm clouds, almost reaching the Tree Fortress, when a **Storm Raptor (Storm Hawk)** swoops out of the eyewall and snatches her into the air!
* **The Sight:** Squad 907 looks up to see a chaotic flurry of feathers and wind shear, followed by Pip's tiny dangling legs kicking wildly as she shouts at the top of her lungs:
  > **"LET ME GO YOU STUPID BIRD!!!"**
* **The Pastry Dilemma (Bailing on the Snacks):** The Storm Hawk is actually hunting the greasy, savory scent of Pip's giant sack of bacon pastries. If Pip drops the pastries, the bird will immediately let go of her to dive after the falling food. However, Pip refuses to surrender her breakfast unless persuaded (**DC 15 Presence/Presence Roll**) or if a PC shoots the bird's talons (**DC 17 Ranged Attack**).

### 🎯 Rescue Interventions & Skill Checks
1. **Snack Bail Persuasion (Presence DC 15):** Convince Pip to drop her pastry sack, causing the Storm Hawk to release her into the Tree Fortress / Raft.
2. **Talon Strike (Ranged/Finesse DC 17):** Shoot or sever the Storm Hawk's talons to free Pip while she clings tightly to her pastries.
3. **Mid-Air Catch (Agility/Strength DC 17):** Position Squad 907's Canopy Raft underneath to catch Pip (or her pastry sack) as she falls.

### 🎁 Tactical Rescue Rewards
* **Tree Fortress Haven (Safe Zone):** Rescuing Pip allows Squad 06 to fully lock down their **Tree Fortress**. Squad 907 can dock their Canopy Raft directly against the Fortress ramparts.
* **Bramble's Root Anchors:** Bramble lashes Squad 907's raft to the Tree Fortress (+2 bonus to **Gondola Equilibrium** rolls).
* **Pip's Pastry Morale Boost:** If the pastry sack is saved, Pip shares her bacon pastries with Squad 907, allowing each PC to clear **1 Stress** upon rescue.
* **Kael's Aetheric Resonance:** Kael tunes his speaking-stone to **Mwaza-Kasa**, granting a **+1 bonus** to all **Mwaza-Kasa Sync** rolls.

## 🧭 Wave-by-Wave Tactical Flow & GM Running Loop

### Wave 1: The Radio Transmission & Canopy Ambush (Range: Far / Close)
* Aggie's Speaking Stone screeches to life as Bramble contacts Squad 907.
* Two **Storm Raptors** dive from the storm eyewall at Far range. One snatches Pip right in front of the Tree Fortress while she screams *"LET ME GO YOU STUPID BIRD!"*
* A pack of **Mizizi Bobcats** climbs the winch cables toward Kael and Saffron.

### Wave 2: The Heavy Assault & Mid-Air Rescue (Range: Close / Melee)
* Three **Mizizi Jaguars (Mwaza-Chui)** leap onto the deck, targeting spellcasters and mind-linked characters with *Frequency Bite*.
* *Complication (Pip in Peril):* Squad 907 must choose whether to convince Pip to bail on her snacks or perform a daring mid-air Talon Strike/Catch while under Jaguar assault.
* *Tortoise Opportunity:* PCs can haul **Mwaza-Kasa** onto the deck/fortress rampart (DC 17 Strength/Finesse) to begin the **Mwaza-Kasa Sync Clock**.

### Wave 3: Eyewall Climax & Shell-Wall Activation (Range: Melee)
* The **Eyewall Storm Surge Clock** approaches 6 while beasts make desperate final attacks against the Tree Fortress and Raft.
* Completing the **Mwaza-Kasa Sync Clock (4 ticks)** triggers **Sheltering Sentinel**, projecting the Aetheric Shell-Wall dome around the entire Tree Fortress & Canopy Raft complex to seal out the storm as dawn breaks.

## 🧠 DM Reminders & Status Conditions

### 🏷️ Status Conditions Quick Ref
* **Vulnerable:** Target cannot take Reactions, and attacks against them have Advantage.
* **Restrained:** Target cannot move, and physical action rolls have Disadvantage.
* **Pacified:** Target cannot take hostile actions for 1 round.

### 🔥 GM Fear Economy Summary
* **Spend 1 Fear to:**
  * Interrupt player turn order to steal spotlight.
  * Make an additional GM move.
  * Activate adversary features (*Snatch & Pitch*, *Frequency Overload*, *Mirror-Step*, *Group Attack*).
  * Trigger environment hazard (*Hurricane Gale*).
