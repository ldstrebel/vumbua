## Phase 2: The Exam

### Scene: The Written Exam

**The Venue — The Basin:**

The Resonance Race basin doubles as the largest classroom in Harmony. A **mile-wide** open-air stadium, with terminals set up along the upper tiers. The remaining **~70,000 students** file in and take their seats at individual testing stations.

The scale is staggering. The Basin can hold far more than 70k — it was built for the Race, and the exam is just borrowing the space. Students sit in long stretches around the upper rim, each with a terminal. The basin floor far below is empty — for now.

**The Exam Format:**

- Terminal-based written exam
- Multiple-choice and short-answer using [[The Loom]]-connected terminals
- Questions cover the first week of general education: resonance theory, node science, navigation basics, survival protocol
- **~20,000 students will fail** and be sent home

> [!NOTE]
> The study guide Val sold to Lucky comes in a few formats for your reference:
> - [[raw-study-guide]]: The blank questions.
> - [[luckys-study-guide]]: The guide with Lucky's incorrect answers marked.
> - [[vals-study-guide]]: The guide with Val's snarky annotations and hand-written corrections.

**GM Pressure Points:**

- The PCs should feel the weight of 70,000 people taking the same exam
- If they studied (or got Val's cheat sheet, or talked to Rill about Weeping Nodes), they have advantages
- The exam itself should be **quick in real-time** — don't simulate every question. Hit 2-3 key moments with dice rolls, then move to results

---

### Scene: The Drop

**The spectacle of failure.**

The **20,000 students who failed** get one consolation: they're allowed to **drop into the Basin** — the only way to set foot on the storied basin floor. They walk to the rim, step off, and slide/drop down the concave walls to the basin floor below.

From there, they walk out through the front gate and straight to port. Ships are waiting. It's the most dignified exit the Academy can offer — you failed, but you got to touch the floor that legends race on.

**For the PCs:**

- This is **cinematic**. Describe 20,000 people slowly walking to the edge and dropping in. Some are crying. Some are laughing. Some are determined to come back.
- The PCs *passed*. They're watching this from the upper tiers. It should feel heavy.

**For the PCs who passed:**

- They're told they can descend into the Basin too, if they want — to get a head start on exploring the space where they'll eventually need to impress the Captains.
- This is optional. Some students sprint down. Others linger, watching the failed candidates walk to port.

### Scene: Team Assignment & The Loom

**The Loom speaks.**

After the exam, the remaining **~50,000 students** receive their team assignments. The [[The Loom|Loom]] — the ancient engine in the [[Walker-Core]] that powers the Academy's systems — assigns each student to a squad.

**How it works:**

- Each student approaches a Loom terminal (or the assignment appears on their existing exam screen)
- The Loom delivers a **one-liner** — a short, cryptic statement that serves as both assignment and prophecy
- Students with matching one-liners are on the same team

**Example Loom one-liners** (for the PC squad):

- *"The fire that doesn't burn feeds the root that doesn't grow."* → Ignatius + Britt?
- *"What sinks in still water rises in the current."* → Aggie + Iggy?
- *"The hammer asks the mountain: who shaped whom?"* → Lomi?

> [!NOTE]
> **GM Decision:** Craft the actual Loom one-liners for each PC. These should feel like fortune-cookie-meets-prophecy — short, poetic, and thematically tied to the character's arc.

**What happens next:**

- Students are told they can now **prepare to see the Captains**
- A rest week follows, during which teams interview with available Captains for their first **venture journey** — a one-month trial expedition
- The best Captains take the highest-ranked candidates. Your team's initial ranking determines your options
- Each PC's clan suggested a Captain in the first survey — that's their "recommended" match

**GM Notes:**

- The team assignment should feel **momentous** — this is the party officially forming
- The Loom is ancient and somewhat mysterious. Its one-liners are never wrong, but they're often only understood in retrospect
- Leave the Captain interviews for Session 5 — this session ends on the team forming
