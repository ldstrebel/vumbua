This document outlines the pacing, NPC encounters, mechanics, and narrative beats for Session 8 of the Vumbua campaign.

## 🕒 Phase 0: Mizizi VIP Day (Session 7.5 Async Survey)
Aggie and Britt play through [mizizi-vip-day.json](file:///d:/Code/vumbua/sessions/planning/s7/mizizi-vip-day.json) asynchronously before the session. The decisions and rolls they make there directly impact their starting state for Session 8:

1. **The Poison Sickness (`experience_poison_knowledge`):**
   * *If Aggie diagnosed Britt's poison (Success):* Aggie knows Britt is sick with wood-toxin. She brews the focus remedy specifically to stabilize her. When Iggy and Ignatious burst in seeking help for Iggy's headache, Aggie must choose whether to give the remedy to Iggy (removing his -2 exam penalty) or split the dose to keep Britt stabilized.

   * *All paths converge on Val Jr's warning:* Britt and Aggie enter Session 8 knowing that the written exam is only Phase 1, and that a secret physical test (the Basalt Run) awaits. They can share this info with the group.
3. **Stress Carryover:**
   * *If Britt failed her Presence check at the Colonnade fair:* She begins Session 8 with **1 Stress** from sensory overload.

## 🕒 Phase 1: Last-Minute Prep Hooks & Lore Rewards

Before reporting to the stands, the players have a brief window to interact with NPCs in their residential blocks or the quad. Solving these quick mini-encounters rewards them with a **specific quote** that answers a question on the written exam.

### 👥 Prep Encounters & Quote Rewards

#### 1. [[loami]] (Harmony-born, Block 99-Piston)
*   **The Scene:** Block 99-Piston is a screaming pressure cooker. The air is thick with the smell of wet coal dust, burnt engine grease, and cheap chicory coffee. The floorboards vibrate with a bone-rattling *thud-thud-thud* from the water pumps below, and the pipes are whistling twice as loud as usual because the central Loom is drawing power for the exam. The corridor is a blur of blue-collar candidates panicking.
*   **The NPCs:**
    *   **Bjorn (The Nordic Roommate):** The tall, lean-muscled roommate who Loami paid with a ticket and grog last session. He has a massive hangover from Loami's "engine grease" and is currently sitting on the floor leaning against a pipe, rubbing his temples.
        *   *Staged Reaction:* *"Sultano... whatever you brewed for the race was absolute lightning, but my head feels like a piston is firing inside it. Tell me the exam hall has dim lighting. Please."*
    *   **"Greasy" Gus:** An old, soot-stained boiler operator who failed his trials fifteen years ago. He sits on an upturned coal crate, puffing a pipe of bitter soot-leaf, watching the kids panic. 
        *   *Staged Reaction:* *"Look at 'em. Half these rust-buckets are gonna throw up on their slates. Hey, Sultano! You actually think you're gonna be a captain, or are you just trying to get a free ride to a fancy grave?"*
    *   **Penny Miller:** A regular Harmony cadet from the Mid-Core whose parents run a bakery. Dressed in a pristine, slightly oversized uniform, she is desperately clutching color-coded flashcards and trying to avoid touching the greasy walls.
        *   *Staged Reaction:* *"Is the ceiling supposed to be dripping black oil? I got a smudge on my sleeve! How am I supposed to write my slate if I get soot on it? Do you think the exam hall has chairs? Real chairs, not coal crates?"*
    *   **Julian Abernathy:** A posh, nervous Harmony candidate whose uncle is a minor clerk in the Upper Core. He is frantically pacing, clutching a copy of the Academy charter.
        *   *Staged Reaction:* *"This block is a structural disaster! The noise levels violate three distinct housing safety codes. If I fail because this pipe-vibration gives me a migraine, my father is going to petition the Board of Regents!"*
    *   **Toby:** A scrawny, trembling first-year clerk trying to paste official notices on the vibrating walls with wet paste. He has had far too much chicory coffee.
        *   *Staged Reaction:* *"No one is allowed in the libraries! Stay out of the Sterling berths! Report to the Ring! Oh gosh, the pressure's spiking again, we're all going to blow up before the test even starts!"*
*   **The Roommates' Assist:**
    *   **[[Ratchet]]** (maintenance roommate) is packing his heavy wrench set, yelling warnings over the din: *"Sultano! The bypass valve is linked to the main riser! Don't let it blow the secondary seal or the whole block goes cold before the exam!"*
    *   **[[Soot]]** (Piston-worker roommate) tosses Loami a pair of thick, oil-stained leather welder's gloves: *"Catch! Don't touch the bypass wheel bare-handed or you'll peel your skin off!"*
*   **The Hook (Sarge and the Steam Blowout):** 
    *   **[[Sarge]]** is frantically trying to etch logistics formulas onto the inside of his welding visor with a copper nail. In his frantic pacing, he accidentally kicks the **Piston-9 Bypass valve**, shearing the rusted gasket. 
    *   A high-pitched, blinding spray of superheated steam erupts, filling the hallway. Toby screams and drops his placards; Julian shrieks about suing the school and ducks behind a water tank; Penny gasps and drops her color-coded notes straight into a puddle of dark engine oil.
    *   **Bjorn** groans, stands up, and tries to block the steam spray with a heavy canvas blanket, yelling: *"Sultano! The grease-maker! Stop the pressure before we get boiled!"* Gus just laughs and takes another puff: *"Told ya. Meat grinder's starting early."*
*   **The Check:** Loami must act quickly to save Sarge's notes (and skin). 
    *   *Option A (Finesse/Tinker - DC 11):* Weave through the steam and crank the emergency cutoff wheel using Soot's gloves.
    *   *Option B (Strength - DC 12):* Physically force the vibrating, hot pipe joint back into alignment long enough for Sarge to slip a securing bracket over it (Bjorn can assist here, granting **Advantage** on the Strength check due to his help).
*   **The Outcomes:**
    *   *Success:* Loami secures the pipe. Sarge's visor is saved (though slightly smudged). Penny's notes are slightly damp but readable (she frantically thanks him). Toby manages to stick the placard. Sarge coughs and gives Loami the **Quote Reward**.
    *   *Failure:* The steam continues to spray. Loami takes **1 Stress** from minor scalds. Sarge's visor formulas are washed away by condensation (meaning Sarge is going to have to guess on the exam). Penny's notes are ruined in the oil puddle (she bursts into tears). Julian runs out of the block entirely. Sarge still shares the quote, but coughing up soot: *"Dammit, Loaves... well, at least you tried. Just remember..."*
    *   *Critical Success:* Loami vents the valve with style. He receives the **Quote Reward**, and Sarge is so impressed he hands him a **Sarge-Grade Spanner** (+1 to mechanics checks) or Penny gives him a **Sugar-Bun** (heals 1 Stress when eaten).
*   **The Reward (Sarge's Quote):** Once the steam settles, Sarge coughing shares a piece of Union survival wisdom:
    > *"Gods, Loaves, my skin... Hey, don't forget the Union safety codes: if the hull blows in both the galley and the gears, **always patch the Engine room first**. You can starve in a drifting boat, but you can't eat if the whole boiler blows you into the sky."*
    *   *(Answers Module 5: The Sacrifice)*

## 🏃 Phase 4: The Freshman Run ("The Basalt Run")

Before they can rest, the new cadets are dropped directly into the **Apex Ring basin** on foot. This is the **Freshman Run**—a traditional gauntlet designed for the new cadets to show off their skills while the senior Captains watch from the stands to establish a baseline of what each squad can do.

### 🏟️ The Set Pieces (Show-off Spots)

#### 1. Juxta's Lift-Stone Pylon (The Grav-Climb)
*   **The Vibe:** A towering basalt pillar surrounded by orbiting boulders and shifting gravity fields.
*   **The Goal:** Ascend the pillar to retrieve a synchronization tag.
*   **Show-off Opportunities:**
    *   *Earthkin/Trench-Kin:* Can mold clay to anchor themselves to the stone or absorb fall impacts.
    *   *Agile/Finesse:* Can parkour across the floating stones during low-gravity pulses.

#### 2. Settika's Prism-Water Estuary (The Torrent)
*   **The Vibe:** A fast-moving, high-pressure water channel filled with glowing, heavy Prism Water.
*   **The Goal:** Cross the rapids or dive to activate a submerged node key.
*   **Show-off Opportunities:**
    *   *Mechanics/Engineers:* Can rig cable slides or override the flow turbines.
    *   *High-Density shells:* Fungal turtles can walk along the bottom of the channel unaffected by the current.

#### 3. Feltland's Mycelium Maze (The Spore-Dampener)
