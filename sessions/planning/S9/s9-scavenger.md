# 📜 Session 9: The Master Scavenger & Tag Manifest

This document is the official **Dean's Scavenger Manifest** for the Basalt Run. It lists all synchronization tags, component items, and resource nodes distributed across the 9 geography zones of the Apex Ring Arena Basin.

---

## ⚖️ Rules of Engagement & Arena Governance

1. **Minimal Policing:** Candidates are given near-total operational freedom on the arena floor. The Academy monitors for cheating via localized resonance dampeners, but does not interfere with tactical maneuvering.
2. **Violence & Conduct:** Physical confrontation and contesting tags between squads is a **legitimate method** to demonstrate combat capability. However, **unprovoked malice, lethal intent, or sabotaging another squad's survival** is strictly frowned upon by Harmony authorities and will destroy standing with noble, Scrivener, and Mizizi Captains.
3. **Emergency Evacuation (The Glider Patrol):** **Second-Year Cadets** and **Indigo Turban Guards** patrol the upper rim on tactical **Aether Gliders**. Cadets who break their emergency seal or fall unconscious will be evacuated via glider drops within 90 seconds (disqualifying them from Gold-rank commissions).

## ⏱️ The Countdown Clock & Audio Broadcast

Every 3 hours, the Scrivener spires broadcast an automated audio chime that echoes across all 9 zones:

> 🎙️ *"All Candidates — the first challenge deals in Aether-Beast Defense. Evacuate the Ring by 0800 to avoid this challenge."*

### 🧪 The True Test:
This is a psychological test of **Expedition Survival & Squad Bonding**. After an airship crash in unexplored territory, can a squad quickly assemble, establish defensible shelter, organize roles, and resist an unexpected threat?
