# Session 9 Campaign Plan: The Basalt Run

This document outlines the pacing, NPC encounters, mechanics, and narrative beats for Session 9 of the Vumbua campaign.

---

## 🕒 Phase 1: The Proving Run (The Basalt Run Gauntlet)
The newly sorted first-year squads descend into the Apex Ring basin on foot. The party, newly sorted into **Squad 907**, faces their first coordinated physical test.

### Objectives:
- Retrieve synchronization tags from designated locations across the arena basin.
- Impress the senior captains watching from the grandstands.

### Locations & Environmental Features:
- **Juxta's Lift-Stone Pylon (The Grav-Climb):** Gravitational anomalies and shifting boulder platforms.
- **Settika's Prism-Water Estuary (The Torrent):** Rapid currents and heavy Prism Water channels.
- **Feltland's Mycelium Maze (The Spore-Dampener):** Fungal spores that block visibility and scramble navigation gear.
- **Ash-Blood Basalt Flats (The Vent Run):** Active vents venting high-pressure heat and steam.

---

## 🕒 Phase 2: Captain Savior Opportunities
Following the freshman run, Squad 907 must begin seeking backing from prospective captains to secure their "captain savers" within the week.

### Hooks:
- Cross-reference the **Captain's Dossier** to identify requirements and targets.
- Impress local faction leads using their showing in the Basalt Run.
- Manage Britt's growing shell contamination by seeking Elder Zaza in the forest.
