# Session 9 Campaign Plan: The Basalt Run

This document outlines the pacing, NPC encounters, mechanics, and narrative beats for Session 9 of the Vumbua campaign.

---

## 🕒 Phase 1: The Proving Run (The Basalt Run Gauntlet)
The newly sorted first-year squads descend into the Apex Ring basin on foot. The party, newly sorted into **Squad 907**, faces their first coordinated physical test.

---

## 📜 Scene 0: The Loom Announcement & Squad Scramble (Friday Morning)

When Dean Vane activates the Great Hall Loom output spires on Friday morning, 100,000 punch cards spill into the courtyard and squad numbers light up on the brass spires. 

Here is how each crew reacts to the news, the chaos as they scramble to find each other, and their strategy for approaching the Senior Captains:

### 🏆 1. Squad 01: The Echelon (The Elite Rivals)
* **Roster:** Valentine Sterling (Gold), Finch Gable (Copper), Baron Bolt (Gold), Lady Glimmer (Gold).
* **Reactions & News:**
  * **Val Sterling:** Acts smooth and unbothered, though privately relieved he has high-status names.
  * **Finch Gable:** Furious — groans loudly that he was assigned to "aesthetic decorative nobles" who don't know basic load-balance math.
  * **Baron Bolt & Lady Glimmer:** Appalled to find a Copper-rank, grease-stained math nerd in their squad, complaining about his lack of hygiene.
* **The Scramble Chaos:** Finch immediately pulls out his brass stylus and calipers, measuring Baron Bolt's boots and barking that Bolt's stride length is dragging down their theoretical velocity by 14%. Bolt tries to bribe Finch to be quiet, while Glimmer applies perfumed powder to avoid smelling the arena mud.
* **Captain Strategy (The VIP Escalator):** They ignore the lower arena floor and head straight for the **Gantry Lift**. Val plans to leverage House Sterling and Gilded connections to get the VIP Captains in the Box Seat Apartments to lower the lift and invite them directly into the luxury suites.

### ⚡ 2. Squad 02: The Wildcards (Volatile Elemental)
* **Roster:** Zephyr (Copper/Silver), Ember (Silver), Jorge (Gold), Iron-Jaw Jax (Silver/Gold).
* **Reactions & News:**
  * **Zephyr:** Cheers loudly, treating the Loom sorting like a giant party game.
  * **Ember:** Narrows her eyes and immediately starts taking notes on her squadmates like a surveillance report for Valerius's radio show.
  * **Jax:** Tries to act tough and establish leadership, while **Jorge** brings his potato-mapping charts.
* **The Scramble Chaos:** Zephyr gets excited and accidentally discharges a static shock, zapping Jax. Jax's mechanical jaw drops off onto the flagstones; he curses and snaps it back into place while Ember writes it down as "broadcast material." Jorge tries to get them to look at his potato map, which Zephyr immediately tries to eat.
* **Captain Strategy (The Fireworks Display):** They plan to put on an unmissable, explosive show of raw elemental power in the Ash-Blood Basalt Flats to catch the eye of daredevil, high-firepower captains like **Captain Ignis "Scorch"**.

### 📚 3. Squad 03: The Scholars / The Sixfold
* **Roster:** Percival Vane-Smythe III (Gold), Serra Vox (Gold), Cassius Thorne (Gold), Sarge (Rust).
* **Reactions & News:**
  * **Percy:** Ecstatic — he has the ultimate academic dream team to test the Sixfold Theory.
  * **Serra Vox:** Scribbles frantically in her notebook, fascinated by the anthropological potential.
  * **Cassius Thorne:** Turns bright red because he's stuck in a squad with his crush (Serra).
  * **Sarge:** Rubs his temples in utter agony: *"I'm babysitting three gold-blooded library nerds."*
* **The Scramble Chaos:** Percy tries to hold an impromptu academic lecture at the staging gate. Sarge physically grabs Percy and Cassius by their leather collars to stop them from wandering straight into an active steam vent while arguing about thermal distribution.
* **Captain Strategy (The Scientific Manifesto):** Percy intends to compile a 20-page field analysis during the gauntlet and present it directly to research-minded captains (like Professor Kante or Dean Vane's inner circle) to prove the commercial value of Node exploration.

### 📦 4. Squad 04: The Ablative (Block 99 Scrappers)
* **Roster:** Lucky (Silver), Soot (Copper), Lance (Copper), Scrapper allies.
* **Reactions & News:**
  * **Lucky:** Immediately starts scanning the crowd for hustle opportunities, pitching "Basalt Run Failure Insurance" to nervous freshmen.
  * **Soot:** Covered in engine grease and hammering his boots.
  * **Lance:** Trying to act like a hardened scrapper despite his Harmony background.
### 🔮 5. Squad 05: The Axiom (Construct & Signal Logic)
* **Roster:** Calculus Prime (Gold), Theorem (Silver), Lemma (Silver), Kael (Copper).
* **Reactions & Scramble:** Calculus Prime calculates their probability of survival at 89.2%. Kael demonstrates his speaking stones, but the acoustic resonance causes feedback with Calculus Prime's internal gears, making Calculus chime uncontrollably like a clock tower.
* **Captain Strategy (Zero-Error Navigation):** Approach technical/construct captains with a 100% mathematically precise navigation telemetry log.

### 🌿 6. Squad 06: The Harvesters (Bio-Flora & Cartography)
* **Roster:** Dr. Rose Halloway (Gold), Bramble (Silver), Pip (Copper), Saffron (Copper).
* **Reactions & Scramble:** Dr. Halloway treats the gauntlet like a garden stroll. Pip brings a giant sack of breakfast pastries and tries to stuff them into Bramble's wooden armor. Saffron gets lost sketching fungal spores on the canyon wall.
* **Captain Strategy (Resource Utility):** Offer rare biological specimens and sustainable field rations to exploration captains preparing for long-haul Mist voyages.

### 🔥 7. Squad 07: The Kiln (Pyrotechnic & Heavy Cover)
* **Roster:** Cinder-4 (Gold), Hearth (Silver), Kindle (Copper), Maria Wall (Silver).
* **Reactions & Scramble:** Cinder-4's internal furnace hums at extreme temperatures, scorching Maria Wall's shield handle as Maria tries to plant a defensive perimeter.
* **Captain Strategy (Vanguard Breachers):** Demonstrate to industrial captains that they can hold and clear any high-heat bottleneck.

### 🌊 8. Squad 08: The Bathysphere (Subsurface & Pressure)
* **Roster:** Captain Barnacle (Gold), Pressure (Silver), Depth (Silver), Brawn (Copper).
* **Reactions & Scramble:** Captain Barnacle's heavy brass diving suit echoes loudly and moves at 1 MPH; Brawn eventually picks up Barnacle and carries him on his back.
* **Captain Strategy (Sub-Surface Vaulting):** Target deep-vault captains looking for crews capable of entering extreme pressure zones.

### 🥷 9. Squad 09: The Silence (Shadow Recon)
* **Roster:** Nyx (Gold), Kaelen (Silver), Mira (Silver).
* **Reactions & Scramble:** A silent, brooding stealth squad. Chaos ensues because all three members went invisible/stealth in the crowd and can't find each other.
* **Captain Strategy (Covert Extraction):** Demonstrate shadow recon and stealth extraction capabilities.

## 🌪️ Scene 1: The 50,000-Student Arena Drop & Rendezvous Chaos

### 📊 The Attrition Stakes:
* **Total Candidates Dropping:** **50,000 first-year cadets** (over 10,000 squads) descend into the Apex Ring basin simultaneously via steam-lifts and rope-rigs.
* **The Cut:** Only **~20,000 cadets** (~4,000 squads) will earn a "Captain Saver" and secure a spot on an actual **Proving Voyage** into the Mist!
* **The Relegation:** The remaining **30,000 cadets** will be relegated to **Simulated Exploration** inside the arena basin while the lucky ones sail out on real airships!

---

### 📢 Clever Student Rendezvous & Signaling Methods:
Across the overwhelming sea of 50,000 shouting freshmen, candidate squads improvise creative ways to locate each other:

1. **Basalt Wall Painting:** Squads use stolen chalk, industrial grease, or crushed berries to paint massive squad numbers (e.g. `SQUAD 06`) across the canyon walls.
2. **High-Point Observation (Val Sterling):** Val Sterling took the very first lift down and immediately claimed the apex of **Juxta's Lift-Stone Pylon**, standing tall so his squadmates can spot him from the descending lifts.
3. **Improvised Squad Banners:** Cadets strip off their uniform jackets, scribble squad numbers with mud, and tie them to spears or surveyor poles as makeshift flags.
4. **Magical Flares (Race & Class Magic):**
   * **Ash-Blood Flares:** Pyromancers launch glowing firebursts into the sky.
   * **Mizizi Spore Beacons:** Druids release bioluminescent spore clouds.
   * **Zephyr's Lightning Beacon:** Zephyr repeatedly discharges purple static bolts, acting as an unintentional erratic lightning beacon for Squad 02.
5. **Harmony Tech Nodes (Speakerphones & Acoustic Stones):**
   * **Speaker-Nodes:** Candidates tune stolen Harmony megaphone nodes to echo squad calls across the canyon.
   * **Speaking Stones (Kael):** Kael uses his acoustic speaking stones to broadcast a localized audio beacon for Squad 05.

* **Basin Dimensions:** **3.0 Miles Wide** (15,840 ft diameter / **1.5 Miles (7,920 ft) Radius to Center Obelisk**).
* **Basin Surface Area:** $\approx \mathbf{197,060,000 \text{ sq ft}}$ (~4,523 acres / ~7.0 square miles).
* **Per Individual Cadet (50,000 Cadets):** $\approx 3,941 \text{ sq ft}$ (~$62.7 \text{ ft} \times 62.7 \text{ ft}$ personal area).
* **Per Squad (10,000 Squads of 5):** $\approx \mathbf{19,706 \text{ sq ft}}$ per squad (~$140 \text{ ft} \times 140 \text{ ft}$ squad square area).
* **Expansive Fanned-Out Proximity:** When spread out across the 7-square-mile arena floor, the distance between adjacent squad centers is **~140 feet** (~half a football field apart)! Teams can travel freely in open terrain, seeing distant flags and magic flares on the horizon without bumping into each other every 10 seconds.
* **Push to Center (1.5 Miles):** A direct sprint from the outer wall gates to Apex Island (Zone 9) is **1.5 miles** (~10–12 minute full sprint, or ~25 minute steady march). A full 3-zone scavenger tag sweep spans **3.5 to 5 miles** (~1 to 1.5 hours of tactical navigation).

### 🔍 Zone-by-Zone Terrain Density:
* **Chokepoint Bottlenecks (Zone 9 Apex Island & Bridges):** High tension! Hundreds of squads compress onto the Stone and Wood Bridges, standing **10 to 20 feet apart** or fighting for position on the ramps.
* **Constricted Canyons & Petrified Pillars (Zone 1 & Zone 5):** Narrow slot canyons funnel squads into single-file lines, passing within **15 to 25 feet** of rival teams.
* **Open Grass & Basalt Plains (Zone 2 & Zone 6):** Squads achieve maximum spread, maintaining **140 to 200 feet of clearance** between teams as they sprint for tags.
* **Wetland Channels & Sandbars (Zone 3 & Zone 8):** Streams and mudflats compress squads onto dry footpaths **30 to 50 feet apart**.

## 🏛️ Zone-by-Zone DM Field Descriptions & Scavenger Tags

### 🌲 Zone 1: Petrified Forest (West / Northwest) 
  > *"You step under the shadow of colossal stone trunks that tower eighty feet above the basin floor — ancient trees turned to solid, pale-gray granite. The air here tastes like dry chalk and ozone. Heavy boulders hover motionlessly in mid-air between the stone pillars, suspended in a silent, gravity-defying grid."*
* **Active Node Effect (Aerostatic Spire):** **Low Gravity Field.** Jumps carry candidates 3x higher, falling damage is negated, and loose gear/boulders float. Gravity flips near high pillars.
* **Scavenger List Tag:** **Petrified Core-Stone Tag** (Mounted atop a 40-foot floating rock boulder; requires a low-gravity leap to snag).
* **Squad Activity:** **Squad 06 (The Harvesters)** with Dr. Rose Halloway and Bramble are collecting petrified bark samples, while **Pip** drops a pastry that floats away into the sky.

### 🌋 Zone 2: Volcanic Plains (North)
* **DM Read-Aloud Description:** 
  > *"The ground beneath your boots turns to jagged black obsidian and cracked basalt. White steam vents blast out of deep fissures with terrifying shrieks, filling the air with sulfur and intense heat. The ground pulses underfoot like a furnace heartbeat."*
* **Active Node Effect (Thermal Vent Resonator):** **High Heat & Steam Blasts.** Extreme ambient heat. Every 2 rounds, steam vents blast high-pressure heat (DC 13 Agility to dodge or take minor heat stress).
* **Scavenger List Tag:** **Obsidian Thermal Tag** (Anchored directly inside a glowing, steaming basalt fissure; requires fire resistance or clever mechanical retrieval).
* **Squad Activity:** **Squad 02 (The Wildcards)** is putting on a show here — **Zephyr**'s purple static arcs into the steam clouds while **Jax** and **Ember** brag to the Box Seats above.

### 🌿 Zone 3: The Wetlands (Northeast / East)
* **DM Read-Aloud Description:** 
  > *"Deep, winding river channels cut through dense banks of thick mud and vibrant, overgrown reeds. The water glows with a faint, translucent turquoise light. Small bioluminescent spores float off the water weeds like underwater fireflies."*
* **Active Node Effect (Prism Water Surge):** **Bioluminescent Mud & Plant Growth.** Deep mud counts as difficult terrain. The water glows bright turquoise and heals 1 Stress if drank, but causes temporary glowing skin.
* **Scavenger List Tag:** **Prism-Lotus Tag** (Submerged in a glowing, 8-foot-deep water channel beneath overgrown lily-reeds).
* **Squad Activity:** **Squad 05 (The Axiom)** is attempting to measure the bioluminescent wavelength while Kael's speaking stones feedback against the water.

### 🌊 Zone 4: The Delta (Southeast)
* **DM Read-Aloud Description:** 
  > *"The river channels converge into a wide, rushing delta that opens into the southern drainage gates. White-water rapids churn against ancient stone breakwaters, spray soaking the gravel banks."*
* **Active Node Effect (Hydro-Flow Node):** **Rushing River Currents.** Strong water currents pull swimmers 20 feet per round toward the outer drainage gates.
* **Scavenger List Tag:** **Hydro-Mooring Tag** (Buoyed in the center of the white-water rapid channel, tethered to a stone breakwater).
* **Squad Activity:** **Squad 07 (The Kiln)** is using Maria Wall's heavy shield as a river anchor while Cinder-4 tries to reach the tag.

### 🏜️ Zone 5: Canyons (South / Southwest)
* **DM Read-Aloud Description:** 
  > *"Steep, towering red sandstone cliffs twist into narrow, maze-like slot canyons. Shadows lie thick between the rock walls, and every footstep, breath, and whisper echoes back ten times louder than it was spoken."*
