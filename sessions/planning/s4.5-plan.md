## Part 2: Val's Escape (Dorm Scene)

**Location:** The Sterling Wing (High-end, yet Val tries to keep his room "academic" and "approachable").

**The Sycophants:**
- **The Favor-Seekers:** Students who want on his "Dad's crew." They hover in the halls with "refreshments" and "extra ink."
- **The Hangers-on:** Wealthy heirs assuming Val will lead Squad 01 (which he might).
- **The Traps:** People sent by his father's rivals to record him being "un-Sterling-like."

**Val's State:** He’s frantically updating his "Study Guide 2.0." He looks like he hasn't slept, narrating his own fatigue like a radio play: *"And here we see the Scion of Sterling, defeated by a simple triple-integral... but wait! He reaches for the caffeine-stick!"*

**The Goal:** The party catches him as he's performing a "Tactical Retreat" out of his window or through a laundry chute to avoid a group of sycophants waiting outside his door with a custom-engraved brass sextant gift.

## Part 3: The Day 2 Market (Outside the Gates)

A "positive" encounter to offset the rough night before. 

**Mood:** Vibrant, bustling, smells of roasting nuts and ozone-cleaning fluid. People are haggling, but there's a sense of communal preparation for the "Great Filter."

### Set Piece: The "Kindling" Exchange
An elderly street-food vendor, **Ma'o**, spots the party as they navigate the market. They look noticeably more beat-up and exhausted than the average first-year student. Without waiting for them to order, he hands them a tray of "Sun-Cakes" (warm, dense pastries filled with spiced honey). 
- *Ma'o:* "You look like the 'Filter' already chewed you up and spat you out, kids. Survival isn't just about passing tests; it's about remembering to eat. First round is on the house—call it a Vumbua Welcome."
- **Benefit:** Eating the warm cakes provides a minor "Comforted" buff (advantage on the next Stress-related roll).

### Distraction Options:
1. **The "Broken" Compass:** A vendor is being bullied by a student over a "malfunctioning" aether-compass. The party can intervene and realize the compass is actually tracking the nearest source of HTech.
2. **Lucky's Rivals:** A street-game of "Crown & Ruin" where the dealer is actually being fair, unlike Lucky. A chance to win back some silver or gain a minor "Good Luck" buff.
3. **The Scrivener's Error:** A frantic student has lost their notes. Finding them reveals a secret shortcut through the ventilation to Hangar 12.
