### [[Session 02Pt5|Session 2.5: The Power Room]]
**Date:** 2026-02-16
**Type:** 1-on-1 side session ([[Iggy]] / Holly)

While the party socializes at the bonfire, Iggy slips away to explore the Academy's power plant. He discovers the resonator battery infrastructure and meets **[[Professor Kante]]**, a tortoise professor of harmonics who draws him into a conversation about the [[The Power System|Global Amplitude]] decline and the Ash-Blood Anomaly.

**Key Events:**
- Iggy Irish-goodbyes from the bonfire to investigate the power plant
- Meets [[Tommy]] (gnome clerk) and [[Lucina]] (dwarf mechanic) in the lobby
- Discovers over-engineered Panda 5 resonator batteries and charts showing the [[The Power System|Global Amplitude]] decline
- [[Professor Kante]] appears and explains the Minimum Crisis and the missing Ash-Blood surge
- Kante gives Iggy an umber crystal and invites him to return as a collaborator
- Iggy rejoins the group for the Celestial Lounge trip

**Players Discovered:**
- The Global Amplitude measures Harmony's total ether power — it increases with each node integration
- The amplitude has been declining for 30-40 years — unprecedented in recorded history
- The Ash-Blood integration produced only ~20 amps instead of expected +300 to +800
- Panda 5 batteries are surge regulators/receptors, not storage — powered by umber crystals
- The Night of Sparks (~400 years ago) burned the world for two months due to unregulated surge
- The Apex 1 next-gen battery line was scrapped because the expected surge never came
- Professor Kante's theory: integration works through cultural connection, not resource extraction
- The [[Trench-Kin]] are known to Harmony as border defenders ("trail tales from the Trench")

### [[s7-clean|Session 7: Race Day]]
**Date:** 2026-07-08

It's Reszo Race Day and the group splits up to hustle, explore, and scheme. Loami teams up with Lucky to hawk "Engine Grease" from backpack dispensers, then cons his way aboard the Sterling family airship by posing as a booze delivery crew. Ignatious and Loami investigate the Zephyr's engine room with Val as their unwitting tour guide, uncovering the deep resonance anomaly: the Ash-Blood integration failed to increase global amplitude as predicted, leaving the next-gen Panda resonators dangerously unstable. A drunk Iggy inadvertently spills Professor Kante's classified research to Val. Meanwhile, Ignatious forges a diplomatic connection with Cade Ashveil, Lady Ignis's new ambassador, learning that the Ash-Bloods' political future depends on helping the Mizizi integrate — and that Ignatious himself has begun to believe the Ash-Bloods' future lies with Harmony, not isolation. Pudge wins the race on his Griffin, and the party heads back to cram for Friday's exam.

**Key Events:**
- [[Loami]] and [[Lucky]] set up a mobile grog operation with backpack dispensers
- [[Loami]] and [[Ignatious]] con their way aboard the Zephyr via a fake delivery
- [[Loami]] becomes an impromptu bartender on the Zephyr's promenade deck
- [[Iggy]] explores the Zephyr's looming glass (Renali magnification tech) and gets motion-sick
- [[Valentine Sterling]] explains resonance theory, the Night of Sparks, and Umbra crystals in the engine room
- [[Iggy]] (drunk) reveals Professor Kante's classified research on the global amplitude anomaly
- [[Ignatious]] reconnects with [[Cade Ashveil]], Lady Ignis's ambassador, and learns Ash-Blood political strategy
- [[Ignatious]] declares his belief that the Ash-Bloods' future is with Harmony, not isolation
- [[Pudge]] wins the Reszo Race on his Griffin
- The group gets invited back to the next race but receives zero pay for their bartending

**Players Discovered:**
- Harmony's power comes from ambient resonance ("DC current"), captured wirelessly by resonators — not generated locally
- The Night of Sparks (~400 years ago) was caused by an unregulated integration surge that burned half the world
- Umbra crystals (Vox family product) act as resistors capping power thresholds
- The Ash-Blood integration did not produce the expected global amplitude increase — the core mystery
- Next-gen Panda resonators fluctuate because amplitude stays below the new resistor threshold
- The Castellans face financial ruin if the new resonator line fails
- Lady Ignis's political strategy: help Mizizi integrate to strengthen Ash-Blood position
- Nstyl's silence is the most powerful node effect — its sands are used to soundproof across Harmony
- Cultural strength directly correlates with node power in the Reszo Race
