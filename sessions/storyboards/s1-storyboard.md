# Comic Storyboard: Session 1 (Orientation & Outbursts)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 1: Orientation & Outbursts**, styled as a detailed 2D graphic novel.

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive manga-style linework, crisp black ink outlines, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic volumetric lighting, warm atmospheric twilight gradient`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, spacesuit, tank treads`

---

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Scattered Housing
![Final Page - Session 1, Page 1](assets/s1_page1.png)

*Layout Design:* **Asymmetrical Grid Layout** — 1 wide top panel showing the group, and 2 bottom panels (left: vertical, right: square) showing them splitting up.

#### Panel 1 (Wide Top Panel): Dorm Assignments
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A wide-angle comic book panel showing a bustling courtyard outside the Vumbua administration office. Five newly enrolled first-year students—Britt (gray turtle-girl), ignatious (Ash-Blood human with spiky fire-red hair), Loami (broad blue-gray soot-covered grease monkey), and Iggy (tiny clay-brown earthkin)—stare at a large wooden notice board listing dorm assignments. A speech bubble above Loami says: "I'm in Block 99. The boiler rooms are right below." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "Housing assignments scatter the newly formed group across the modular districts of Vumbua..."
    *   *Speech Bubble:* "I'm in Block 99. The boiler rooms are right below."

#### 🖤 Gutter Transition 1:
*   *Environmental Transition:* **From the crowded notice boards to the separate dormitory paths.**

#### Panel 2 (Bottom Left Vertical): Saying Goodbye
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A tall vertical panel on the bottom left. ignatious is slinging a canvas duffel bag over his shoulder, nodding to Britt and Loami. A speech bubble is drawn next to him: "See y'all tomorrow. Don't get lost in the pipes." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble:* "See y'all tomorrow. Don't get lost in the pipes."

#### Panel 3 (Bottom Right Square): Iggy Departs
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A square panel on the bottom right. Iggy is already walking away in the distance, dragging his oversized trench coat. A speech bubble is drawn next to him: "Block 12. Far away." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble:* "Block 12. Far away."

### Page 2: Iggy's Sky Climb
![Final Page - Session 1, Page 2](assets/s1_page2.png)

*Layout Design:* **Inset Panel Layout** — 1 large vertical panel showing the massive rusted loading crane, with a small square detail inset panel in the top right showing Iggy's perspective.

#### Panel 1 (Main Vertical): The Crane
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A tall vertical comic book panel. Iggy, the small trench-kin with cyan fluid-filled goggles from the reference image, climbs hand-over-hand up the metal ladder of a massive, rusted copper loading crane towering over Hangar Block 12. Below him, the lights of Vumbua flicker as twilight falls. Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "Seeking space and a view, Iggy scales the highest scaffold he can find..."
    *   *Sound Effect:* `CLANK CLANK CLANK`

#### Panel 2 (Top-Right Square Inset): Touching the Sky
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A small square inset panel in the top-right corner. A close-up of Iggy sitting on the edge of the crane's boom, dangling his legs. His giant goggles reflect the starry twilight sky. A speech bubble next to him says: "To touch the sky. Comfortable." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble:* "To touch the sky. Comfortable."
