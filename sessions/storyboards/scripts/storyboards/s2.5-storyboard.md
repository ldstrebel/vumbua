# Comic Storyboard: Session 2.5 (The Power Room)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 2.5: The Power Room**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Sneaking into the Lobby

#### Panel 1: Slipping Away
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel showing Iggy, the small trench-kin, slipping away from the crowded Block 99 bonfire into the dark alleys, leaving the glowing orange campfire behind him. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "While the party socializes at the bonfire, Iggy slips away to explore Vumbua's power plant..."

#### Panel 2: The Lobby Desk
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A panel showing the grand, brass-and-stone lobby of the power plant. Lucina the dwarf mechanic leans on the reception counter, chatting with Tommy the gnome clerk who is scribbling on a clipboard. Iggy's small shadow creeps across the marble floor. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Tommy):* "The regulators are leaking again, Lucina. Kante is going to lose his shell if the amplitude drops further."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Lucina):* "Tell Kante the batteries are over-engineered. We're doing our best."

### Page 2: The Resonator Charts

#### Panel 1: The Decline Chart
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A comic book panel showing Iggy standing on a stool, peering through his cyan goggles at a massive parchment chart pinned to a slate board. The chart displays a glowing blue line graph representing 'Global Amplitude' over decades, showing a steep, alarming decline. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Iggy, reading):* "Amps dropping. 30 years of decay. Why?"
    *   *Narration Box:* "The Global Amplitude measures Harmony's total ether power—it should increase with each integrated node. But for decades, it has been dying."

---

### Page 3: Professor Kante's Theories

#### Panel 1: Meeting Kante
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A warm panel showing Professor Kante, the tortoise scholar, slowly emerging from the shadow of a massive Panda 5 battery bank. He looks down at Iggy kindly, tapping his brass cane. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Kante):* "Ah, a visitor in the dark. Fascinated by the drop, are you? The expected Ash-Blood surge never came."
    *   *Speech Bubble (Iggy):* "Anomaly. Only 20 amps."

#### Panel 2: The Umber Gift
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A close-up panel showing Kante placing a small, raw, warmly glowing amber Umber Crystal into Iggy's hand. Iggy's goggles glow in response to the crystal's resonance. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Kante):* "Take this, my small friend. A key to study. Integration is a connection of spirits, not a resource to be mined. Remember that."
    *   *Dungeon Master Cameo (Leaning in from peeled bottom-right corner):* "Kante's research aligns with Iggy's instincts. A powerful partnership begins."
