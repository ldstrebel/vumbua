# Comic Storyboard: Session 2 (The Bonfire & The Boilers)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 2: The Bonfire & The Boilers**, styled as a detailed 2D graphic novel.

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive manga-style linework, crisp black ink outlines, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic volumetric lighting, warm atmospheric twilight gradient`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, spacesuit, tank treads`

---

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Stacking logs at Block 99
![Final Page - Session 2, Page 1](assets/s2_page1.png)

*Layout Design:* **Asymmetrical Grid Layout** — 1 wide top panel and 2 bottom panels (left: vertical, right: square).

#### Panel 1 (Wide Top Panel): Geometric Log Stacking
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A wide-angle comic book panel showing Cassius Thorne, an earnest student, carefully and obsessively stacking logs into a perfect hexagonal bonfire grid in the Block 99 courtyard at dusk. Loami (broad blue-gray grease monkey from reference image) sits nearby on an empty crate, holding a flask and grinning. A speech bubble above Loami says: "You've got a real eye for structural load, Cassius. That's a beautiful stack." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic warm lighting.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Narration Box:* "A bonfire at Block 99 becomes the stage for new friendships and philosophical debates..."
    *   *Speech Bubble:* "You've got a real eye for structural load, Cassius. That's a beautiful stack."

#### 🖤 Gutter Transition 1:
*   *Environmental Transition:* **From the careful building stage to the roaring heat of the lit bonfire.**

#### Panel 2 (Bottom Left Vertical): Cassius Explains
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A tall vertical panel on the bottom left. A close-up of Cassius Thorne gesturing to his log layout, slightly blushing. A speech bubble says: "If the geometry is off, the resonance decays unevenly!" Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
