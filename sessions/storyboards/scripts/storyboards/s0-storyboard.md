# Comic Storyboard: Session 0 (The Trials)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 0: The Trials**, styled after *The Adventure Zone* graphic novels (art by Carey Pietsch).

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats with warm atmospheric gradient maps, highly expressive facial features, whimsical high-fantasy adventure webcomic aesthetic, art by Carey Pietsch`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, text, typography, speech bubbles`

---

## 👤 Character Reference Prompts

### 1. Britt & Aggie (Mizizi Cousins)
> **Prompt:** `Character design sheet for Britt, a gray-skinned dusty fungal-turtle person with a mossy dark-green shell, and Aggie, her cousin who is a red-and-white spotted mushroom-turtle person with a red-spotted shell. Turnaround sheet, highly expressive turtle-kin faces. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`

### 2. Iggy (Trench-Kin / Earthkin)
> **Prompt:** `Character design sheet for Iggy, a small, sickly-looking trench-kin person made of packed, slightly crumbling brown earth. He has giant round bug eyes covered by oversized glowing cyan goggles and wears a dark, oversized heavy wool trench coat. Turnaround sheet. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`

### 3. ignatious (Ash-Blood Pilgrim)
> **Prompt:** `Character design sheet for ignatious, a rebellious Ash-Blood elf teenager with wild spiky fire-red hair, wearing a travel backpack, dark linen clothing, and fingerless gloves. Determined and defiant expressions. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`

### 4. Loami (Harmony Boiler Worker)
> **Prompt:** `Character design sheet for Loami, a broad-shouldered Harmony-born Octoumba student. He is a working-class grease monkey covered in coal dust and soot, wearing a heavy leather boiler apron and grease-stained gloves. Proud, stubborn expression. Turnaround sheet. Vibrant 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive linework, cel-shaded color flats, art by Carey Pietsch --no 3d, realistic`
