# Comic Storyboard: Grading & Quality Criteria

Use this grading checklist to evaluate each page and panel during layout iteration and image generation.

---

## 📋 The Checklist

### 1. Story & Narrative Accuracy (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Key Scene Translation:** Are the core campaign developments, mechanics (e.g., rolls, stats), and plot milestones of the clean transcripts represented in the panels?
*   **Narrative Flow:** Does the narration box bridge the gap between panels smoothly, maintaining clear chronological pacing?

### 2. Character Design Consistency (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Physical Description Overriding Names:** Did the prompt describe the character's *physical features* instead of just using their name? (e.g., *“a gray-skinned fungal-turtle person with a green mushroom-patterned shell”* instead of *“Britt”*). Image generators do not know who "Loami" or "Iggy" is.
*   **Consistent Attire & Features:** Are key accessories (e.g., Iggy's cyan goggles, Rovaldi's copper mechanical eye, Loami's broad shell, ignatious's red fire-elf hair) present and visually stable across all panels on the page?

### 3. Speech Bubble & Dialogue Alignment (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Speaker Attribution:** Are the tail-pointers of speech bubbles directed at the correct character?
*   **Transcript Quote Fidelity:** Do the dialogues use *actual, direct quotes* from the clean transcripts?
*   **Pacing & Density:** Is the bubble text short and snappy (under 25 words per bubble) to avoid cluttering the visual artwork?

### 4. Gutters & Panel Flow (PASS/FAIL)
*   **No White Gutters:** Are the gutters black, dark gray, atmospheric colors, or non-existent? (White gutters are an automatic fail for this specific graphic novel style).
*   **Fluid & Dynamic Borders:** Do the panels use diagonal cuts, vertical splits, or overlapping borders rather than a rigid, monotonous rectangular grid?
*   **Frame Breaking:** Do highly dynamic elements (e.g., flying toast, fire sparks, reaching hands) break the panel borders to overlap the gutters, adding depth?

### 5. Color Palette & Lighting (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Scene Palette Unity:** Does each page or distinct scene utilize a cohesive color harmony/gradient map to visually bind the panels together?
    *   *Examples:* Sunset orange/gray for basalt cliffs; deep teal/cyan glow for hangars; warm amber/fiery red for the bonfire; bright, saturated daylight for the cafeteria.
*   **Atmospheric Lighting:** Is there clear lighting direction (e.g., volumetric sunbeams, warm firelight cast on characters, glowing mechanical lenses)?

### 6. Expressions & Emotion (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Emotive Facials:** Are characters' faces drawn with exaggerated, expressive cartoon emotions matching the tone of their dialogue? (e.g., *“eyes wide in shock,” “cheeky grin,” “sweating and nervous”*).

### 7. Sound Effects & Onomatopoeia (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Comic Lettering:** Are key physical actions accompanied by stylized, hand-written onomatopoeia text (e.g., `THWACK!`, `CRUNCH!`, `WHOOSH!`, `CLINK!`) placed in or overlapping the gutters?

### 8. Dungeon Master Cameos (PASS/FAIL)
*   **Corner Peel Effect:** Is the "peeling back the corner of the page" visually defined as an organic paper curl with a drop-shadow overlay?
*   **Cameo Frequency:** Are DM cameos used sparingly, reserved only for meta-commentary, mechanic rolls (e.g., "Roll for initiative!"), or out-of-character humor?
