# Comic Storyboard: Session 7 (Race Day & Harmonic Anomaly)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 7: Race Day**, styled as a detailed 2D graphic novel.

*Revised after 3-pass creative team audit: Lore-Master, Novel Designer, Storyboard Editor.*

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive manga-style linework, crisp black ink outlines, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic volumetric lighting`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, spacesuit, tank treads, text, typography, watermarks`
