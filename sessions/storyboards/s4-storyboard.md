# Comic Storyboard: Session 4 (First Day of Classes)

This storyboard documents the page-by-page panel layouts and image generation prompts for **Session 4: First Day of Classes**, styled as a detailed 2D graphic novel.

---

## 🎨 Art Style Guide

To maintain visual consistency when generating these images:

*   **Prompt Style Core:** `Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean expressive manga-style linework, crisp black ink outlines, cel-shaded color flats, cinematic volumetric lighting, warm atmospheric twilight gradient`
*   **Negatives/Bans:** `--no 3D render, realism, hyper-detailed, muddy colors, dark grit, muted tones, spacesuit, tank treads`

## 📖 Comic Pages Layout & Prompts

### Page 1: Logistics Class
![Final Page - Session 4, Page 1](assets/s4_page1.png)

*Layout Design:* **Asymmetrical Grid Layout** — 1 wide top panel and 2 bottom panels (left: vertical, right: square).

#### Panel 1 (Wide Top Panel): Chalk Snipe
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A wide-angle panel set in a high-ceilinged, steam-heated classroom. A strict professor stands at a chalkboard. ignatious (Ash-Blood human with spiky fire-red hair from reference image) is slouching at a wooden desk, looking bored. A flying piece of white chalk hits ignatious square in the forehead. A speech bubble above the professor says: "Focus, ignatious!" and another above ignatious says: "Ouch! Emotional damage." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Sound Effect:* `TAP!`
    *   *Speech Bubble (Professor):* "Focus, ignatious!"
    *   *Speech Bubble (ignatious):* "Ouch! Emotional damage."

#### 🖤 Gutter Transition 1:
*   *Environmental Transition:* **From the stuffy interior of the lecture hall out to the busy student corridors.**

#### Panel 2 (Bottom Left Vertical): The Rebuff
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A tall vertical panel on the bottom left. Loami approaches Finch Gable, a snobbish student in glasses, in the hallway, holding a blank scroll. Finch walks past Loami with his nose in the air, ignoring him. A speech bubble above Loami says: "Hey, Finch, any chance I could copy your notes?" and above Finch says: "I do not share notes with boiler-grease." Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
*   **Script & Overlays:**
    *   *Speech Bubble (Loami):* "Hey, Finch, any chance I could copy your notes?"
    *   *Speech Bubble (Finch):* "I do not share notes with boiler-grease."

#### Panel 3 (Bottom Right Square): Loami's Frustration
*   **Art Generation Prompt:**
    `A square panel on the bottom right. A close-up of Loami crumpling the scroll in his hand in annoyance, sparks of soot drifting off his shell. Detailed 2D graphic novel style, clean manga-style linework, cel-shaded color flats.`
