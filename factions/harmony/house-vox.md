# House Vox (The Spark)

> *"Power is Life."*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sphere** | Energy, Crystal Batteries, Surge Protection |
| **Element** | Umber Crystals (Batteries) |
| **Council** | Venture Council |

## What Players Know

House Vox is the "newish money" of Harmony (though still quite old). They control the modern energy supply through their monopoly on umber crystals, mined from Umbra Mountain. Their unique and key ability is to **store connection (or cap it)** within these umber crystals. While House Gilded originally built the Chime Spires to capture ether, House Vox perfected the art of bottling it. They seek to explore to learn and to grow, but unlike other houses, their future is currently secure thanks to the Umbra mines. They are ruthless in their pricing and highly protective of their proprietary technology.

## Key NPCs

- **Arch-Duke Cymon Vox** - The ruthless patriarch of the family, focused on corporate dominance.
- **[[Serra Vox|Seraphina "Serra" Vox]]** - Gold-rank student (Squad 01). Cymon's youngest daughter, hiding her lineage to escape the pressure of her name.
- **[[Professor Kante]]** - A tortoise researcher whose harmonics research is sponsored by House Vox, leading to the creation of the latest battery generations.

### The Power Monopoly

While their future is currently financially secure, House Vox's stranglehold on energy has made them the primary target of House Gilded, who secretly funds House Sterling's exploration to find an umber crystal alternative to break the Vox monopoly. House Vox explores purely to learn and grow their technological edge, attempting to stay one step ahead of the Vault.
